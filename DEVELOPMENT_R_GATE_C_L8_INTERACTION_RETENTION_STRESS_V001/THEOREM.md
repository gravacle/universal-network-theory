# Exact L8 interaction/retention stress of accumulated records

## 1. Scope

Start from the conditional R-B prepared history. Each retained head qutrit is
in

\[
 |+\rangle={|B\rangle+|x\rangle\over\sqrt2},
\tag{C01}
\]

after the adopted `alpha=r0`, `Phi=pi/4` write and native full transfer. The
attachment remains conditional and is not derived from bare F3.

This packet turns on a bounded interaction among accumulated qutrit records.
It does not claim generic dynamics, a phase, continuum behavior, or gravity.

## 2. Native two-record stress block

For two neighboring retained heads `1,2`, use only BS07--BS09 terms

\[
 H_{12}=-tT_{12}+t(q_1-q_2),\qquad t>0.
\tag{C02}
\]

The onsite term commutes with both occupations. In the one-occupation basis
`A=|x,B>`, `B=|B,x>`,

\[
 H_{12}=t\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad H_{12}^2=2t^2I.
\tag{C03}
\]

At `tau_C=pi hbar/(2 sqrt(2)t)`, the normalized one-occupation component
`(A+B)/sqrt2` maps to `iB`. The zero- and two-occupation sectors are fixed.
Thus the complete qutrit occupation probabilities change from

\[
 p_{\rm before}(BB,Bx,xB,xx)=(1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4)
\tag{C04}
\]

to

\[
 p_{\rm after}(BB,Bx,xB,xx)=(1/4,1/2,0,1/4).
\tag{C05}
\]

Total expected retained occupation is exactly one both before and after.
Local expectations change from `(1/2,1/2)` to `(1/4,3/4)`. The physical
integrated current from `1` to `2` is `1/4`, giving exact ledgers

\[
 -1/4+1/4=0,\qquad +1/4-1/4=0.
\tag{C06}
\]

The per-pair total-variation contrast of the complete positional occupation
read is `1/4`. This is redistribution, not record loss: the total occupation
distribution is unchanged (`0,1,2` records with probabilities `1/4,1/2,1/4`).
Because equal-content carriers are indistinguishable after coupling, this
packet does not claim that an individual source-lineage label moves with an
occupation. It measures retained amount and positional read only.

## 3. L8 tiling

The R-B history has 256 retained heads: all cells with odd first coordinate.
Pair heads along the second generator from even to odd second coordinate.
There are

\[
 4\times4\times8=128
\tag{C07}
\]

disjoint interaction blocks. Their Hamiltonians commute. Consequently:

- expected retained total stays `128`;
- retention fraction is exactly `1`;
- absolute oriented interaction throughput is `128/4=32`;
- net periodic boundary flux is zero;
- every cell record-ledger residual is zero;
- the expected number of occupied odd-second-coordinate heads rises from
  `64` to `96`, while the even partners fall from `64` to `32`.

The complete terminal instrument is the product of all qutrit PVMs and the
failure-inclusive controller/work/reference PVMs inherited from R-B. No

## 4. Disposition

This is a bounded interaction/retention stress. It proves that the selected
native interaction redistributes positional record probabilities while
exactly retaining total occupation and closing every discrete ledger. It does
not prove generic lineage persistence, criticality, or a universal
accumulation law.

A future nonzero record-ledger residual would be classified as a possibly
necessary active owner or collective term before being judged. No continuum
diagnostic is used.
