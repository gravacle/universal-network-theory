# Bounded connected-ring time/coupling scan — numerical result

## Scope

The selected microscopic parent is unchanged from the audited connected L8
ring stress: 32 identical eight-site hard-core blank/content carrier rings,
native nearest-neighbor transfer, a staggered onsite term, inherited adopted
`alpha=r0` preparation, and the complete failure-inclusive product read.

The scan uses `t_hop=1`, staggered strengths `delta=(0,1/2,1,2)`, and pulse
times `tau=(pi/(4sqrt(2)),pi/(2sqrt(2)),pi/sqrt(2))`. Each point is computed
in the full `2^8=256` ring space. Occupation changes and independently
integrated edge currents enter the same discrete record-ledger equation used
by the preceding ring packet.

## Raw envelope

Across the 12 preregistered points:

- staggered occupation amplitude ranges up to `0.5688670625126272`;
- absolute oriented throughput per ring ranges up to
  `1.1377341250252546`;
- absolute nearest-neighbor connected correlation ranges up to
  `0.06132656332408998`;
- retained-total change per ring is at most `1.78e-15` in magnitude;
- record-ledger residual is at most `2.56e-15` in L1 and `5.56e-16` in
  Linf;
- norm, energy, and record-number-law controls are at most `7.78e-16`,
  `2.67e-15`, and `8.89e-16`, respectively.

At `delta=0`, the staggered occupation response and integrated oriented
throughput vanish to numerical precision while connected correlations can
still develop. This is a useful zero-bias control: correlation formation and
net positional redistribution are distinct record observables.

## Classification

This is **controlled numerical evidence** for a prepared microscopic family.
The residual values are reported as raw record-ledger residuals and remain
unassigned until owner classification. Their scale beside the stronger
unitarity, energy, retained-total, and number-law controls is consistent with
floating-point closure, but they are neither promoted as exact zeros nor
called physical defects.

The non-monotone time/coupling response is recorded without fitting a scaling
law or identifying a critical state. No generic retention law, continuum
limit, Ward behavior, mature macroscopic dynamics, or gravity follows.
