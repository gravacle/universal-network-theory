# Connected L8 paired-ring circuit depth trajectory — numerical result

## Scope and custody

This packet retains the independently audited 16-record selected circuit:
two native eight-record generator cycles paired by eight native
third-generator supports, reversed stagger preparation, transfer angle
`pi/8`, inherited adopted `alpha=r0`, and a complete failure-inclusive
terminal product read. Sixteen copies partition the 256 retained L8 heads.

The tuple labels only enumerate the inherited finite family. They are not a
physical grid or distance. Depth counts repetitions of the declared ordered
finite circuit and is not continuum time.

At every depth from zero through eight the packet records expected retained
occupation, occupation range, ring-to-ring transfer, connected correlation,
and the gate-owned record ledger. Two current summaries are kept distinct:

1. cumulative net edge throughput sums the magnitudes of net current on each
   support and can decrease under reversal; and
2. gate-event absolute traffic sums the magnitude of every gate transfer and
   therefore retains reversed traffic rather than canceling it.

## Raw trajectory

Expected L8 retained occupation stays at `128` within `7.11e-13` throughout.
All 24 cumulative supports, including all eight inter-cycle supports, are
active at every positive depth.

From depth zero to eight:

- gate-event absolute traffic grows from `0` to `5.387857637651692` per
  cluster;
- cumulative net edge throughput grows to `5.102760095563551`;
- cumulative net inter-cycle throughput grows to `2.0433764227644584`;
- connected edge correlation is non-monotone and reaches a maximum absolute
  value `0.06592583672451707` at depth three;
- local occupation extrema vary non-monotonically, with the widest sampled
  range approximately `0.31949` to `0.68051` at depth two;
- the two cycle totals exchange small amounts but remain close to four.

The raw per-cluster ledger-residual envelope is `4.53e-14` in L1 and
`4.95e-15` in Linf. Norm error remains below `4.67e-15` and record-number-law
change below `6.39e-16`. The initial implementation bound of `5e-13` on the
L8 retained-total drift failed at `7.11e-13`; before audit the bound was
repaired to `1e-12`, consistent with the observed eight-depth floating-point
norm accumulation. This is an execution-tolerance repair, not a physical
term or a post-audit change.

## Classification

This is **controlled numerical evidence** for one prepared microscopic
depth family. The nonzero raw residuals are unassigned until owner
classification. Their joint behavior with norm, number-law, and total
retention controls is consistent with floating-point accumulation, but they
are neither called physical defects nor promoted to exact zeros.

The trajectory does not fit a critical or scaling law. It establishes no
individual post-mixing lineage identity, continuum behavior, Ward structure,
mature macroscopic dynamics, or gravity.
