## 2. Isolated-channel result is useful, but is not a large graph

The seven isolated receipts keep all microscopic coefficients fixed and use
h=Omega/N for N=1,2,4,8,16,32,64. The exact two-block formula and four-state
eigensystem agree, including the full complex endpoint, complete energy,
nonzero copying-off work and the actual imperfect endpoint's hold evolution.
For orientation, the N=1 target probability is 0.9997587445519683 at
T=162.52116216792606; N=64 gives 0.9999997805570664 at
T=10255.256047306739. Times are the protocol's hbar=1 model times.

This verifies the separately proved O(N^-2) error bound and O(N) time route
without setting g=h. It does not prove that observed infidelity is strictly
monotone for arbitrary N or coefficients: the discrete beat-phase rounding
can oscillate within its analytic bound. At N>1 the four-state calculation
deliberately omits other native edges; it is not an N-width slab.

Copying-off work is positive and retained, rather than silently set to zero.
The seventeen hold samples start from the actual endpoint. The all-time
retention statement comes from the independent norm inequality; a finite
sample grid alone cannot establish it. `actual_hold_failure_bound` includes
the source's conservative +1e-9 roundoff allowance inside its square root.
That numerical padding is not a physical leakage floor or a change to the
analytic asymptotic theorem.

## 4. Exactness and restart qualifications

The protocol says integer/bit identities must agree exactly, but its common
`Checks.equal` helper actually accepts residuals up to 1e-9. In this run the
recorded residuals are exactly 0.0 for assembly, Hermiticity, baseline,
endpoint resonance, donor-swap identity, star reduction, star symmetry and
both quadratic amplitudes. Thus the implementation's weaker gate did not
admit a visible discrepancy. Zero floating-point residual is still not by
itself a symbolic proof; the diagonal/bit identities have independent hand
derivations. Future protocols should either use an explicit zero check on
their exactly representable identities or describe the actual tolerance.

The tiny append-minus-seed differences at 0.01 and 0.02 are far below the
declared tolerance and do not numerically authenticate the proposed order-t^8
contrast. That higher-order claim needs its separate exact polynomial/path
audit; it is not among this worker's frozen acceptance checks.

Durable receipts use exclusive creation, flush/fsync, freeze identity and
payload hashes, and a recorded failure blocks automatic retry. The saved run
does not demonstrate a stopped/resumed execution: source support for restart
must not be relabeled an experimentally tested restart in these receipts.

## 5. Highest-leverage next discriminant

Test whether the native connected advantage survives an actual copying-off
hold, using the complete evolved state without resetting or postselecting
the intended configuration. Freeze stop/hold times before new execution and
report all outcomes, switching work and the full energy ledger. This tests
durable connected preparation rather than optimizing another transient peak.

A minimal inclusive readout is available without adding a force, geometry or
Hamiltonian term. In the existing notation (x,y,u,v), connectivity of the
new-record site a1 to the original occupied-record site b0 has projector

    C_source = n_u + (1-n_u)n_x n_y n_v.

Connectivity to the other donor b1 has

    C_other = n_v + (1-n_v)n_x n_y n_u.

Measure q*C_source and q*C_other, their difference
q*(n_u-n_v)*(1-n_x*n_y), and disconnected/blank complements inclusively.
These Boolean projector identities include altered background rows and
three-edge routes; they do not divide by q occupation or successful transfer.
The present receipts do not contain the required q-weighted moments, so this
is a prospective discriminant, not an additional result inferred from the
stored marginal link probabilities. Native source-specific feedback ablation
and the frozen-star comparison can subsequently identify which mechanism
produces any surviving advantage, with each arm's work separately retained.

Separately, the analytical matching-pool merger route is worth pursuing, but
must retain closure-versus-merger competition and cannot replace the actual
state by a desired matching without a preparation ledger. None of these next
steps requires first claiming a cosmological interpretation.

## 7. Collective-star extension: accepted with a small-gap condition

Independently reviewed Section 6 of
`NATIVE_OVERLAPPING_RECORD_PAID_FORMATION_V001.md`. Its spectral projector
contains both entire k=0 and k=1 record blocks, not only their ground bands.
The nearest discarded k=2 lower band is separated by the positive delta
given there. Splitting the adjacent-k weighted shift gives resolvent bounds
3*sqrt(2)/delta and 3/delta, whose sum is below 8/delta uniformly in donor
number m. Higher-k diagonal growth controls the remaining blocks.

Uniform contour integration of both the resolvent and z times the resolvent
controls the selected spectral projector and its Hamiltonian. The canonical
rotation therefore has an O(alpha^2) effective-Hamiltonian remainder with
constants independent of m. At the declared diamond beat time O(hbar/alpha),
its Duhamel state error is O(alpha). After choosing the ideal target's global
phase, the orthogonal-to-target amplitude is O(alpha), so target FAILURE is
O(alpha^2), not an unjustified square of a probability error.

This is a constructive uniform-in-m theorem for the conditional symmetric
star. The sufficient perturbative premise is alpha=h*sqrt(m) sufficiently
small compared with delta(U,g); for small fixed g,
delta=4g^2/(3U)+O(g^4/U^3). Its constants need not be favorable at small N,
and it is not an error certificate for the present N2 run. The growing-time
full-slab background problem remains open, as does selection of one donor:
permutation symmetry still allocates exactly 1/m of total one-edge success
to each specified donor.
