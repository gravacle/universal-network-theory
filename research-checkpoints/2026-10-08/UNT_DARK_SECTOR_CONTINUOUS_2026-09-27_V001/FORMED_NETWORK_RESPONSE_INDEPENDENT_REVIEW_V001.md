## 3. What the common-response checks establish

For A_e(s)=U_hold(s)^dagger n_e U_hold(s), the code uses the correct
convention chi_ef=i*< [A_e(s),n_f] > in hbar=1 units. An external impulse
with perturbation -f*n_f acts as exp(+i theta n_f). Since n_f is a projector,
the centered observable difference divided by 2*sin(theta) is exactly the
linear derivative, not merely a small-theta approximation. For the degree
and total-incidence generators, the source correctly uses ordinary centered
differences and Richardson extrapolation rather than that projector formula.

The vertex-edge incidence matrix has the correct a0,a1,b0,b1 ordering.
For Phi=B*chi*B^T, an external record-equivalent vertex source predicts

    delta mu / delta j = -lambda_R^2 Phi,
    delta kappa / delta j = t_hop^2 lambda_R Phi.

The signs/factors (-4 and +0.5 here), and the uniform-incidence projection,
agree with the independent kicked-state readouts. Mu is an insertion-work
coefficient at the fixed blank registers a0,b1; at other registers it is
only a coefficient field, not a second available insertion or a mass.

The separate 128-state carrier Hamiltonian and graph-major/carrier-minor
indexing are correct. Projection away from the initially occupied carrier
vertex removes graph-only terms and the irrelevant scalar baseline. The
first-order arrival norm is therefore t_hop^2*<degree>, with all graph and
record correlations retained. This is a short-time transfer curvature, not
a finite-time trajectory or a force. Native lambda_J=0 and epsilon_psi=0
are the declared fixture values, not omissions of active terms.

The mean-link substitute fails by the predicted nonnegative quantity
t_hop^2*sum Var(n_e). For example, in the 2T arm at hold age zero and lag
0.5, the a1 carrier curvature is 0.2535267278261551 and the mean-link deficit
is 0.11461294876279823. This is a substantial state-dependent correction,
not roundoff. It follows from n_e^2=n_e and must not be represented as an
independently fitted transport coefficient.

These probes include every q outcome; q-weighted connectivity and the
unconditional kernel are distinct observables. Kernel entries vary with
formation time, hold age and lag. For the 2T arm at lag 2, the uniform
response is +0.0057750327 at age zero, -0.0000553092 at age one and
+0.0062527696 at age four. There is no single age-independent scalar response
in these data. Sign changes in a time-domain retarded kernel alone would
not diagnose failure of passivity; even passive oscillators can oscillate.
A passive stationary effective medium needs its own state and spectral
qualification rather than a selected positive kernel sample.

This worker measures commutator response, occupations and carrier curvature;
it does not compute the symmetrized/unsymmetrized two-time noise spectrum or
a fluctuation-dissipation relation. The separate one-link noise formulas
therefore remain untested by this run. Likewise, stationary susceptibility
must not be substituted for these age-resolved responses.

Kicks are explicit external probes and their before/after energy differences
are recorded. They are not autonomous record creation or free control. The
common-kernel agreement is a useful same-parent cross-check tying insertion
cost, carrier access and incidence response in an actually formed state.
Because all probes use the same microscopic Hamiltonian, it is not an
independent observation that nature follows that Hamiltonian.
