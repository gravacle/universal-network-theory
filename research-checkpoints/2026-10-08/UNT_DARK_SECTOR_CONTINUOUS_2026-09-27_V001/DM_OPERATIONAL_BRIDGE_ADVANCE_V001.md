## 1. Restore units and separate three measurable coefficients

Use the established Hamiltonian class

    H=Hnet - sum_e J_e n_e A_e,
    Hnet=D(n)-sum_e h_e X_e,

on a finite simple bipartite possible-edge skeleton with V sites. The
current native carrier calculations have lambda_J=0. The result below
does not silently adopt the different lambda_J=1/4 coefficients appearing
in the older prepared-graph carrier note. Let c_v be the coefficient of
t^6 in the uniform independent carrier's site probability. The proven law,
with hbar restored, is

    c_v = sum_{e=(a,b), f=(b,c)}
      h_e h_f J_e^2 J_f^2 eta_v(e,f) <X_e X_f> /(80 V hbar^6),
    eta_v(e,f)=2 delta_vb-delta_va-delta_vc.                 (1)

The sum counts each adjacent unordered pair once. Diagonal D affects the
actual preparation but is absent from this leading operator.

For a fresh LOCALIZED carrier at one endpoint of e, independent of the
same prepared network density, the leading arrival coefficient is exactly

    kappa_e = lim_{t->0} P(other endpoint;t)/t^2
            = J_e^2 m_e/hbar^2,  m_e=<n_e>.               (2)

There is no mean-field substitution: n_e^2=n_e, not m_e^2, is used in (2).
Network coherences, link fluctuations and all native flips are retained.

An independent basis-state link-flip calibration has

    b_e = lim_{t->0} P(specified flipped-link final state;t)/t^2
        = h_e^2/hbar^2.                                  (3)

For (3), use a declared complete graph/record basis preparation with copying

first-order matrix element is -h_e. This is a distinct calibration

## 2. A hopping-independent, dimensionless motion prediction

Isolate a wedge e,f by the already constructed pair-phase controls, or
subtract their plus/minus predictions. Let c_v^(ef) denote its half-difference
sixth-order coefficient and C_ef the surviving plus-state expectation.
Both controls have the same m_e,m_f. For positive native h_e,h_f and
nonzero m_e,m_f,

    I_v^(ef) := 80 V c_v^(ef)
                 /[kappa_e kappa_f sqrt(b_e b_f)]
               = eta_v(e,f) C_ef/(m_e m_f).               (4)

Proof: the denominator is
J_e^2 J_f^2 m_e m_f |h_e h_f|/hbar^6, canceling every scale in (1).
For signed h values the right side additionally includes sign(h_e h_f).
An overall change of clock units cancels too, since numerator and
denominator each have time dimension minus six.

Equation (4) is a common, operationally normalized relation across probe
hopping strengths—not a proposed equivalence between hopping and inertial
mass. No distance, physical volume, gravitational constant or source radius
has been inserted. The coefficient C_ef is an independent native operator
expectation. It must be independently characterized, or the equation used
to infer it and then predict another readout; estimating it from the same
c_v and calling that a successful prediction would be circular.

The exact center/endpoints pattern is (+2,-1,-1). At branching sites the
full wedge sum must be used; the uncorrected ordinary Laplacian is not
substituted. If m_e or m_f is zero, the divided statistic is undefined and

## 3. Exact limit of a pure hopping-clock interpretation

    c_v(J)/kappa_ref^3
      = [h^2/(80 V J^2 m_ref^3)] times the fixed wedge sum  (5)

for common link-flip h. It retains (h/J)^2 rather than becoming universal.

## 4. Why probability redistribution is not yet a force measurement

Let Xpos=sum_v x_v P_v be ANY specified carrier position readout with
common coordinates x_v for the compared probes. This is a diagnostic
assignment until an independent operational ruler supplies x_v. Put
B=sum_e n_e A_e and take common J. Exact Heisenberg equations give

    Vpos = -(iJ/hbar)[B,Xpos],
    Apos = (J/hbar^2)[Hnet,[B,Xpos]]
                         -(J^2/hbar^2)[B,[B,Xpos]].       (6)

The first term involves live link flips; diagonal D commutes with the
inner commutator. Both joint state and carrier coherences matter. Thus a
count of record-site probabilities is not a complete acceleration readout.
For the uniform initialization, (1) implies only

    <Xpos>(t)=mean_v x_v + t^6 sum_v x_v c_v + O(t^7),
    d^2<Xpos>/dt^2=30 t^4 sum_v x_v c_v + O(t^5).         (7)

Equation (7) neither defines a time-independent gravitational field nor

must predict (6), or an explicitly derived semiclassical reduction of it,
using independently calibrated positions and velocities. It cannot be
obtained by relabeling c_v or its Laplacian as acceleration.

## 5. New exact native shortcut coefficient

Take a declared computational graph G, fixed records, copying off, and a
carrier initially at u. Let v lie on the opposite slab and e=(u,v) be
ABSENT from G. No hopping reaches v in one step. At two Hamiltonian steps,
the only possible route is:

    |G,u> --(-h_e X_e)--> |G+e,u>
          --(-J_e n_e A_e)--> |G+e,v>.

The reverse ordering is zero because n_e initially vanishes. Two carrier
hops cannot reach the opposite slab on a bipartite skeleton. Diagonal
pieces cannot complete the missing first-order hop. Hence

    P_v H^2 |G,u> = h_e J_e |G+e,v>,
    P_full(v;t)=h_e^2 J_e^2 t^4/(4 hbar^4)+O(t^6).       (8)

All native terms are retained; no absent edge has been frozen. The remainder
is even because the Hamiltonian, initial basis vector and projector are real.
Native carrier degree onsite terms, if present, do not change this leading
two-step coefficient. Arbitrary graph-diagonal record energies do not either.

For an occupied edge the leading arrival is instead J_e^2 t^2/hbar^2.
For every initially absent opposite-slab edge, (8) is independent of how
far apart its endpoints lie along OCCUPIED paths in G. Strict earliest
Taylor order therefore cannot serve as that graph's distance.

For native h=Omega/N and common J, summing (8) over all initially absent
opposite neighbors of u gives the exact fourth-order coefficient

    sum_{v opposite, not adjacent_G u} P_full(v;t)
       = (N-d_u) Omega^2 J^2 t^4/(4 N^2 hbar^4)+O_N(t^6). (9)

The coefficient is O(1/N) at fixed degree, Omega,J. The remainder carries
an N label deliberately: this is NOT a uniform finite-time locality claim
or permission to exchange the short-time and size limits.

For a general formed coherent state, the t^2 coefficient (2) can already
