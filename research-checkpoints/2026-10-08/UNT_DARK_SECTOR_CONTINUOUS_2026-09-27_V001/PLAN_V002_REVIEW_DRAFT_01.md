# Universal Network Theory — Joint Dark-Sector Research Plan V002

Date: 2026-09-27. Status: revised execution-plan draft under adversarial review.
V001 is preserved unchanged. This is a **plan revision**, not a new scientific
rule, a completed A0/B0 freeze, a passed scientific gate, or publication authority.

## Objective and the proposed publication claim

Determine whether one fixed UNT record/network mechanism can reproduce the
observations attributed to dark matter and dark energy **without separately
postulating those components as independent physical ingredients**.

The user's intended meaning is **elimination by network-based explanation**:
derive those effects from network structure and dynamics so that separate,
unexplained dark ingredients are unnecessary. The familiar terms may remain
useful effective descriptions. The program does not additionally require
proving that no dark-like excitation or effective stress exists.

This is the result the program seeks, not an outcome it presupposes. If earned,
the bounded claim is: “Within the stated model, assumptions and tested domain,
UNT accounts for the tested dark-sector phenomena without independent dark
matter and dark-energy postulates.” A broader claim requires broader evidence.
An analytic or simulated result is a theoretical finding, not a newly measured
observation of the universe.

The analogy is explanatory economy: Einstein's 1905 formulation dispensed with
the stationary luminiferous-ether premise. The analogy is motivation, not
evidence for UNT. [Original paper, English translation](https://sites.pitt.edu/~jdnorton/teaching/HPS_0410/chapters/origins_pathway/On-the_electrodynamics/index.html).

### What “superfluous” must mean here

1. The observed effects remain to be explained; the claim does not deny them.
2. An effective matter-like or vacuum-like stress is allowed if its dynamics,
   normalization and population law follow from the frozen network rule and
   declared inputs. Giving an independent dark fluid a new name is insufficient.
3. Moving an arbitrary source term to the geometric side of an equation is
   not elimination. Inventory independent degrees of freedom, free functions,
   initial-condition laws and calibration data before and after the change.
4. Universal UNT parameters may exist. State how they are fixed, what uses
   dark-sector observations for calibration, and which subsequent predictions
   remain independent. An independently adjusted dark abundance, arbitrary
   halo profile or arbitrary w(z) does not count as a derived explanation.
5. A successful common network explanation is the intended elimination,
   including when observers would still call its effective behavior “dark
   matter” or “dark energy.” Audit whether the dynamics actually explain the
   behavior or merely encode it in adjustable inputs. No extra ontological
   proof of nonexistence is imposed beyond that explanatory and empirical task.
6. A conditional gravity/volume interface may support useful early tests.
   Its conditional status must survive into the claim; it cannot be advertised
   as a derived universal gravitational law.

## Scope, scientific versions and minimal process

The primary construct remains V001's proposed active/maintenance record-budget
split. It must be derived and specified; it is not already established by
finite record-retention simulations. Keep supporting BS, owner-once L6 and
q4/working-gravity parents distinct unless an explicit mapping is verified.

A failed frozen construct is not a refutation of the entire UNT core. Any new
physical allocation rule, normalization, state-selection law or horizon rule
receives a new **construct ID** and re-runs affected joint tests. Changing a
DM rule may also change DE: no unaffected-branch assumption without checking
the shared ledger. Plan V002 does not silently revise a frozen construct.

Use only the process needed to decide the science:

- One compact attempt/input register: equations, version, free choices,
  exposure/calibration data, tests attempted, failures, and result paths.
- Freeze equations, input identities, target observables and numerical/error
  criteria before new scored outputs. A hash binds bytes, not correctness.
- One independent derivation or implementation and a focused adversarial
  review at a scientific freeze. Preserve V001's different-model-family
  review requirement for A0/B0; report it unmet if unavailable, rather than
  relabel same-family agents. Useful unfrozen exploratory work can continue.
- Stop a failed execution, preserve it and diagnose. No automatic tolerance
  repair, retry or scientific rule change. Existing separate authority controls
  any future production retry.
- Do not repeat settled checks or enlarge simulations unless it changes a
  scientific decision. A proof, bound or counterexample may close a task.

## Part A — Specify the rule, then perform inexpensive decisive screens

### I0 — Minimum physical dictionary and rule; freeze A0

This is not a requirement to prove all of UNT before testing a candidate.
Mark every premise **derived**, **adopted for this conditional test**,
**calibrated from named data**, or **unknown**. Unknown quantities indispensable
to a prediction make that prediction not ready; they are not silently fitted.

No cosmological abundance used as an output-generating input. |
| I0.2 | Specify the gravitating subset/response and the complete energy, work, exchange and stress ledger. Define physical or adopted operational volume and time. | State how count/rate/power becomes energy density or stress, including interactions and binding. No identification of stored energy with maintenance power by name. |

Declare any scheduler, saturation, error-correction/noise or formation assumption. | A closed predictive law; distinguish capacity bounds from a rule choosing the allocation. A trivial zero-maintenance result is retained if the dynamics imply it. |
| I0.4 | Derive the relevant network causal boundary and domain. Do not restrict the answer in advance to the Hubble or particle horizon. | Specify material versus open control volume and its boundary flux. If using a cosmological horizon, give its graph-level justification and required history. |
| I0.5 | Census constants, functions, normalizations, graph ensembles, initial populations/distributions, coarse-graining and band choices; distinguish external inputs, fitted quantities and predictions. | Complete inventory and identifiability/degeneracy statement, including shared cosmological/nuisance inputs. |
| I0.6 | Finalize each scored I1/I2 test: observable, epoch/scale range, statistic, numerical and theoretical error allowance, physical/observational threshold, and stopping rule. | Numbers and sources, or an exact mathematical condition, specified before new scored output; no indispensable null field in an active gate. |
| I0.7 | Freeze A0 and adversarially check conservation, dimensions, hidden data use and the independence claim. | Immutable source/input/protocol hashes and resolved blocking review findings. Unavailable different-family review remains recorded as unmet. |

Hubble length is not automatically a causal horizon, and a moving horizon
encloses an open system. [Horizon distinctions](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0310808).
An adopted effective GR/fluid interface is permitted for a labelled conditional
screen; changing it later requires recomputing affected predictions.

Retain the observed collision constraints while matching their assumptions:
[Bullet Cluster simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0261),
[scattering/estimator dependence](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04307).
General BBN changes require their actual expansion/abundance response:
[response calculation](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0503502).

An analytic contradiction of the frozen rule, or robust failure of an
applicable frozen observational test, stops that construct. A numerical
failure is an execution failure; an undefined prediction is incomplete.
Neither is a passed screen, and neither automatically falsifies the UNT core.

### I2 — Abundance and cross-domain predictions; freeze A2 before evaluation

| ID | Task | Scoring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I2.1 | Derive rho_DM/rho_b from the same rule, including record energy/binding, the baryon relation and formation/exchange history. | Compare the frozen output with a specified reference model/data posterior using joint covariance. | I2.2 | Derive the same-rule present-day rho_DM/rho_DE and any epoch dependence. | No additional allocation/normalization fit. Specify “order unity” numerically if it becomes scored. A present-day ratio does not require a constant ratio at all times. |

The original plan already revealed approximately 5.36 and 0.38; these are
**known targets**, not blind validation data. Its 5.36±.10 “2 sigma” interval
is not adopted without identifying a likelihood, cosmology and covariance.
Planck density posteriors are model-dependent reference results, not direct
model-independent component counts. [Planck 2018 VI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209).

Retain the legacy ±10% comparison only if declared as an exploratory closeness
diagnostic. Neither that match nor a 2-sigma match establishes strong evidence.
Reserve stronger prediction claims for genuinely held-out observables,
uninspected data products or future measurements. Development using known
targets is allowed and logged; its later tests must honestly reflect exposure.

### M1 — Decision

- **Proceed:** A0 is predictive, applicable I1 tests pass at their frozen
  level, and I2 is consistent with the declared comparison without an extra
  answer-setting parameter. Proceed conditionally if interfaces are adopted.
- **Revise construct:** a defined prediction fails or an answer depends on
  an unlicensed adjustment. Preserve it, register the proposed change and
  re-run all affected joint tests. Do not rewrite the failed result.
- **Incomplete:** required dynamics, inputs or a valid test are missing.
  Resolve the specific gap; do not call it either a pass or a disproof.
- **Reject this construct:** a robust contradiction remains under its stated
  premises. Record what failed and which alternative is genuinely different.

[PPF's scope and assumptions](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3125).
A CPL best-fit phantom crossing is not by itself an observation that the
fundamental UNT source crosses. Test the candidate's actual observables;
reject its no-crossing branch when a compatible, predeclared comparison
excludes it, not merely when another parameterization has a crossing best fit.

### F1 — Internal consistency and scaling

1. **F1.1:** Determine analytic limits and benchmark finite-size/resource
   scaling. Choose the smallest graph sizes that resolve the stated exponent
   or response to the required precision, with finite-size and sampling errors.
   V001's 10³–10⁷-node range is a conditional research target, not a required
   brute-force allocation. Quantum Hilbert-space size is not graph node count.
2. **F1.2:** Prove or exhibit whether the physical 1+w can change sign, with
   the exchange/parameterization distinction and stability conditions explicit.
3. **F1.3:** Derive any surviving discreteness effect for a named observable,
   coarse-graining, correlation structure, dimension and error norm. The
   assertion that 10^185 records ensure 10^-61 continuum accuracy is not an
   accepted premise. Retain a proved null effect where appropriate.

### F2 — Early-universe and expansion tests

- BBN: primordial-abundance/expansion/thermal predictions, with a radiation
  proxy only where the candidate justifies it.
- CMB: full acoustic and lensing predictions, including the gravitational
  response responsible for peak structure and its pre-recombination history.
  The test does not assume that the explanation must be a conventional particle.
- Recombination: compatibility with observations in the candidate model;
  not compulsory identity with a reference model's inferred history.
- Acceleration: predict the transition and compare H(z)/distance observables.
  The legacy redshift .5–.8 is a reference screening range until its source
  and model dependence are fixed, not an immutable kinematic measurement.

Use a modified Boltzmann solver only after the background/perturbation
equations and its imported assumptions are specified. No likelihood success
can repair an inconsistent conservation or perturbation system.

### F3 — Prediction record before new evaluation

Freeze w(z), H(z), effective abundances/ratios, growth f*sigma8(z), S8, lensing
and matter-response observables, and any predicted galaxy relation/scale.
Keep the full functions; reduce to (w0,wa) only where that representation is
accurate to the frozen error budget. Identify every calibration and previously
seen dataset. Hash locally; public publication is a separate authorized step.

### F4 — Data comparison and the “independent dark components” test

Retain the complementary data families, but pin exact usable products and
their assumptions before execution:

| Domain | Candidate products / required prediction |
| --- | --- |
| Expansion | DESI BAO release and covariance; predicted distance and expansion relations. |
| CMB | Explicit Planck likelihood/version and any ACT/lensing combination; complete candidate spectra. |
| Supernovae | Pantheon+, Union3 and DES-Y5 or prospectively justified successors, separately; identify shared events/systematics. |
| Growth/lensing | Named DESI RSD and DES/KiDS or successor likelihoods with cross-covariance and nonlinear-validity cuts. |
| Mergers | A specified lensing/gas/galaxy morphology test, with the candidate's transport/gravity forward map. |
| Galaxies | A specified rotation/acceleration and lensing comparison, e.g. an applicable SPARC selection, with ordinary-matter/systematic modeling frozen. |

DESI's published contours are tied to their own CMB/data choices. A Planck2018
baseline is legitimate, but cannot be silently treated as identical to a
Planck NPIPE/ACT combination. [DESI DR2 methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14738v3).

The original optional galaxy branch remains optional for a **restricted
cosmological finding**. The intended broad replacement claim requires DM-like
behavior beyond background cosmology, including declared structure/lensing,
merger and galaxy tests; an unavailable domain limits the claim. Freeze a
finite mandatory test set before results, with no selective omission of failures.

Compare with Lambda-CDM and the declared evolving-DE comparator using the
same data, nuisance handling and common parameter assumptions wherever valid.
Report absolute adequacy and comparative preference separately. Specify
Bayesian priors/evidence or a calibrated likelihood-ratio procedure; include
free functions, initial-condition/calibration freedom and model-search history.

V001's Delta chi²=4, inclusion in 2-sigma contours, and “two of three SN sets”
are not automatic strong-evidence rules. Overlapping SN compilations provide
robustness checks, not independent repetitions. Choose and justify the
numeric decision thresholds in F0.4 before inspecting new scored results.

Audit whether fitted surrogates have recreated an unexplained, independently
adjustable dark component. If so, identify what remains unexplained; do not
present reparameterization alone as elimination by explanation. Derived
network excitations and effective dark-like stresses are not a failure merely
because the old terminology still describes them. A mechanism that fails observations is not
rescued by economy; a good fit with unrestricted extra freedom is not a
parameter-free prediction.

### F5 — Genuinely forward tests

Select exact uninspected/new data products and publishable discriminatory
predictions, their precision and competing predictions before evaluation.
DESI, Euclid and Rubin are possible sources, not guarantees of immediate
cosmology-ready data. Verify release/product availability when scheduling.
No retrospective retuning of that prediction record. Surviving a weak test
shared by all alternatives is compatibility, not decisive discrimination.

## Outcomes and publication language

| Outcome | Required evidence / permitted claim |
| --- | --- |
| Rejected construct | A reproduced internal contradiction or failure of an applicable frozen test. Identify construct and assumptions; do not generalize to all UNT. |
| Incomplete/inconclusive | Missing predictions, unresolved inputs, insufficient discriminatory data or uncertainty. Do not convert it into a pass. |
| Finite-model mechanism | Verified derivation/calculation in a declared model. Publish at that scope; no claim of observed dark-sector explanation. |
| Conditional dark-sector account | Defined physical interface, complete predictions and successful bounded observational comparisons; adopted assumptions remain visible. |
| Partial network explanation | Some dark-like behavior follows from network dynamics, but specified phenomena or inputs remain unexplained. Publish the achieved part without treating the joint target as complete. |
| Elimination by network-based explanation, in tested domain | Fixed network rule explains the frozen multi-domain effects, with complete energy/perturbation laws and no answer-setting unexplained dark surrogate; input/complexity audit and adversarial review pass. Effective dark-like excitations/stresses may remain as derived descriptions. Scope the claim to tested domains and adopted premises. |
| Strong new discrimination | Genuinely held-out predictions succeed with calibrated evidential strength against specified alternatives. This strengthens the precise construct; it does not prove every UNT claim. |

The user intends a publishable elimination-through-network-explanation result.
That is the primary target. A narrower result may still be valuable, but must
not be relabelled completion of that target. Publication itself requires
separate authorization; this plan authorizes research, not uploading material.

## Execution, restart and stopping boundaries

Use one small local numerical worker and bounded, single-thread jobs. Long
calculations must publish durable, authenticated restart units; account for
startup, checkpoint, memory, disk and cleanup within their approved limits.
Heavy calculations require the existing separately authorized other-Mac route.
This plan does not authorize L8, production retries, a new VM/package,
infrastructure changes, account resets/spending, GitHub publication or remote
dispatch. Preserve dirty files, historical failures and sealed results.

Scientific screens may close by proof or small counterexample before large
jobs. Inspect a specific live handle when waiting; a pasted prompt is not
evidence that the other Mac is currently running. Continue safe independent
analytical work while a genuinely external step is unavailable.

Do not assign a weeks-long or hours-long completion promise to an unclosed
derivation. Report measured calculation cost separately from implementation,
review, scientific uncertainty and external observation schedules. Keep the
program moving at the fastest pace consistent with the current decision.
