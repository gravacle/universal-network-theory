# Review of the original joint dark-sector plan

2026-09-27. Review only; the original plan is unchanged. Disposition:
**support the staged research structure, but do not approve every physical
assumption, numerical threshold, or evidential label unchanged.**

The root read the complete original `UNT_DARK_SECTOR_JOINT_PLAN_V001.md`
earlier in this session, including I0–I2, M1, F0–F5 and the schedule. At this
review the original path is a macOS dataless placeholder. This audit uses that
complete retrieved text; it does not claim a fresh byte-level reauthentication.
Primary external sources were checked for the issues cited below. This is not
the different-model-family freeze review specified by the original plan.

## What should be retained

The plan correctly distinguishes one proposed active/maintenance construct
from the entire UNT core. A frozen common rule, explicit borrowed inputs and
free parameters, a complete attempt log, cheap exclusion screens before costly
likelihood work, independent scrutiny, multiple observational probes, and
unretuned forward predictions are all appropriate. Failed constructs should
remain in the record. None of the corrections below removes that structure.

## Highest-priority corrections

### 1. Add the minimum physical dictionary to I0, not a demand to prove all physics

The proposed count D and bandwidth N do not by themselves determine an energy
density or pressure. I0 must distinguish a count, an information-processing
rate, an energy, a power, and a stress. Specify the energy stores and transfers,
what is active/retained/maintained, whether those classes overlap, the volume
and clock used, and the assumed gravitational response. Include formation,
transport and maintenance costs once, without double counting.

A conditional calculation may adopt a stated gravity/volume interface and
test its consequences before a complete microscopic gravity derivation.
That is legitimate early screening, not validation of the interface itself.
Do not make proving all of UNT an additional prerequisite to I1.

Current native evidence makes the distinction concrete: in the declared graph
restriction a record insertion changes H by `epsilon_r I-lambda_R d_v`.
Record count alone is therefore not a universal energy/mass count. Conserved
register occupation also does not automatically supply material transport or
an ongoing maintenance-power law. These constrain I0 rather than replacing it.

### 2. Repair the abundance prediction and blindness rules

I2.1 asks for an energy-density ratio, not simply the number of records per
baryon. The model must also derive their gravitating energy, baryon mapping,
interaction/binding accounting, formation history, and relevant normalization.

The original text itself displays 5.36, its proposed interval and the second
ratio. A derivation after exposure cannot honestly be called blind to those
numbers. Preserve exposure and attempt logs, freeze subsequent predictions,
and use genuinely held-out observables or future data for stronger tests.
Exploration followed by revision is allowed science; it changes the result's
status from a blind prediction to a developed/fitted model, which needs a
new test. It should not automatically condemn all future versions.

Planck's inferred densities are conditional on a specified cosmological model
and data combination. The ratio uncertainty requires their joint covariance,
not an unsourced assertion that 5.36±0.10 is a universal 2-sigma interval.
Use such a number as an explicitly conditional preliminary reference, and
ultimately compare a changed cosmology with the observations themselves.
[Planck 2018 VI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209).

### 3. Keep the collision and early-universe tests; qualify their shortcuts

The observed relative distributions of lensing mass, galaxies and gas are
important constraints. But a particle scattering cross-section per mass only
applies after an appropriate effective scattering description is derived.
Even in particle models its inferred bound depends on assumptions and
confidence level: the original Bullet simulation analysis distinguishes a
1.25 cm²/g, 68%-confidence offset bound from a stronger 0.7 bound requiring
an additional pre-merger mass-to-light assumption. The plan's 1 cm²/g can be
a declared conservative screen, not a model-independent universal boundary.
[Randall et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0261).

For BBN, extra relativistic energy can be expressed through Delta N_eff, but
a general network source may change expansion, exchanges, gravity or thermal
history differently. Evaluate the appropriate expansion/abundance response;
do not equate every possible dark component with a constant extra radiation
species. The numerical 0.3 threshold must be tied to a selected analysis and
confidence level before it becomes binding.
[General expansion-response calculation](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0503502).

Likewise, the CMB is an essential test of the predicted potentials,
perturbations and recombination history. It is not a requirement that UNT
adopt conventional microscopic dark-matter particles by definition.

### 4. Remove the unsupported universal discreteness estimate

F1.3 does not establish that 10^185 records imply continuum accuracy of one
part in 10^61. Record number alone cannot give that error. Relative Poisson
count fluctuations scale as N^(-1/2) under independence; a uniform
three-dimensional spacing scales as N^(-1/3); quadrature bias and correlated
fluctuations can obey other laws. Perfectly correlated bits provide an exact
counterexample to automatic suppression of fluctuations with N.

Specify the observable, coarse-graining, dimension, correlation structure,
error norm and actual origin of the record census; then derive the convergence
or surviving correction. This is especially important for a theory in which
correlations are the proposed mechanism, not an ignorable nuisance.

### 5. Separate physical perturbations from a convenient numerical closure

PPF is a useful treatment for specified smooth dark-energy models and their
crossings; its successful numerical evolution does not derive UNT's stress,
sound speed, anisotropic stress, exchange terms or microscopic stability.
Apply it only after checking that the candidate fits its assumptions, or
derive an appropriate extension. Do not suppress perturbations to obtain a
background fit. [Fang, Hu and Lewis](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3125).

A model-dependent inference of an effective equation-of-state crossing is
not automatically a measurement of a fundamental UNT fluid crossing. A
no-crossing candidate should fail when its actual predicted observables are
excluded under a fair specified comparison, not merely because another
parameterization's best fit crosses. DESI's DR2 analysis reports different
preferences depending on the supernova sample, illustrating why the stated
data/model combination matters. [DESI DR2 II](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14738).

### 6. Strengthen the inference and outcome labels without adding busywork

A modest Delta chi² improvement or inclusion in a 2-sigma contour is not by
itself strong evidence for UNT. Specify the actual model predictions,
likelihoods, covariance and overlapping samples, nuisance and cosmological
parameters, priors, approximation errors and model-search history. Report
agreement, comparative preference and genuinely new predictive success
separately. The original plan rightly asks for parameter counts and Bayesian
evidence, but neither should be reduced to the suggested chi²=4 shortcut.

If w(a) is not of the w0+wa(1-a) form, directly evaluate its observables;
do not force a projection into a published (w0,wa) contour as the primary
acceptance test. Report overlapping supernova sets separately as planned;
two successes do not constitute two independent replications.

## Effect on the current work

The one-link and small-graph calculations constrain possible record energy
and response mechanisms. They are **not** a completed or frozen execution of
the original active/maintenance split. They must remain supporting branches,
not quietly replace the original I0–I2 objective.

The minimum practical revision is a short I0 physical dictionary, a transparent
abundance/test-exposure rule, and justified observation-specific thresholds.
The broad stage order can remain. A candidate may be screened conditionally
before every microscopic bridge is proven; conditional success must retain
that label. No original thresholds or scientific inputs were changed by this
review, and no new cosmological fit or numerical experiment was launched for it.
