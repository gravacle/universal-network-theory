## New exact algebraic discriminator from the positive-pole packet

Suppose a complete positive pole packet has Delta_n>0, R_n>=0,
sum rank(R_n)=6, and positive-definite sum R_n. Define the **abstract matrix
Stieltjes block**, not yet a physical Euclidean functional,

    S(z) = sum_n 2 Delta_n R_n/(z+Delta_n^2),  z>=0.

Frequencies are in energy units (hbar=1) in these reconstruction formulas.

Factor R_n=B_n^dagger B_n with rank(R_n) rows and stack the B_n into a square
invertible B. With each gap repeated its residue rank times,

    S(z)=B^dagger diag[2 Delta/(z+Delta^2)] B,
    S(z)^(-1)=z (2M1)^(-1)+S(0)^(-1),
    M1=sum_n Delta_n R_n.

This follows by direct matrix inversion. The generalized eigenvalues of
the constant and z coefficients are Delta_n^2 with those multiplicities.
Thus such a packet has an exact auxiliary six-coordinate quadratic
realization. It is a constructive test of what its pole data determine,
not merely a claim that all operators are missing.

FY's reported four ranks sum to six and its ground-image/static ranks are
six, so it nominates this test. Its saved numerical rank tests do not by
themselves certify an exact residue decomposition or equality of the full
physical kernel to S. No matrix identity was numerically evaluated here.
Extra poles, rank loss or contact contributions would require revising this
simple affine inverse; they must not be dropped to make it work.

## Why this is not yet the physical or one-loop Hessian

1. **Ordered sources:** FY's R_n is the Q_1^dagger/Q_1 positive-frequency
   packet. It is not a real Hermitian-source time-ordered matrix by default.
   For Hermitian sources a contribution instead has the form
   R_AB/(Delta-i omega)+R_BA/(Delta+i omega); its frequency-even part uses
   2 Delta Re(R_AB)/(Delta^2+omega^2), not arbitrary complex R_AB. Complete
   the opposite momentum and time orientation, or construct Hermitian
   cosine/sine sources, retaining all cross blocks. Reality/time-reversal
   conditions must be demonstrated rather than silently imposed. GC's
   declared cosine-transform convention alone does not prove that pairing.
2. **Contacts and inverse:** the physical source-deformed Hamiltonian's
   second derivatives and controller/nuisance contacts belong to the complete
   connected-functional Hessian. Only after its source convention and
   invertible physical quotient are defined does a Legendre inverse become
   a quadratic 1PI response. Neither the positive pole sum nor the GL6U
   double commutator is automatically that complete object.
3. **Bare versus dressed:** even an exact connected inverse is a dressed
   response. Calling its log determinant a new one-loop vacuum contribution
   can count fluctuations already integrated into that response again.
   A same-parent blocking/matching prescription must identify the remaining
   fast variables, background, action Hessian and integration measure.
   The auxiliary Gaussian reproduces only its selected two-point block;
   it does not reproduce all higher source cumulants of the spin parent.
4. **Spectral dimension:** a finite matrix heat trace tends to its dimension
   as proper-time parameter s tends to zero. Adding a canonically normalized
   continuous time direction to six oscillators gives, per unit time,
   (4 pi s)^(-1/2) sum_n exp(-s Delta_n^2), not a four-dimensional
   (4 pi s)^(-2) heat-kernel density. A spatial mode family, physical cell
   measure and controlled window between lattice cutoff and infrared scales
   cannot be replaced by the six finite gap values. This is not a no-go for
   an eventual intermediate-scale continuum window.
