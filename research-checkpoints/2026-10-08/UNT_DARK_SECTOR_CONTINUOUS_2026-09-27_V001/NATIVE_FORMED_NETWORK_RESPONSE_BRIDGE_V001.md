# An actual formed state supplies a common native response

2026-09-27. Analytical continuation of
`NATIVE_RECORD_PAID_LINK_FORMATION_V001.md` and
`NATIVE_CONNECTED_CARRIER_PROBES_V001.md`. This note uses the BS parent,
not the remote H6 model. No new interaction, adopted parameter change,
numerical calculation, galaxy fit, or freely prepared mean graph is used.
The exact one-link formation primitive is the starting state, not its
conditional ground state. The many-link identities below are exact
conditional on an actual formed state; such a connected state has not
yet been constructed by the one-link result.

## 1. Formation age is observable response data

Use the existing exact formation family, with a>0,
epsilon=epsilon_r=a+lambda, g=h. At the declared endpoint the native
evolution has produced |n=1,q=1>. Switch copying off as already declared
in the formation protocol. The full subsequent link Hamiltonian is

    H_hold=(C+epsilon)I-epsilon n-h X,
    R=sqrt(epsilon^2+4h^2),       omega_0=R/hbar,
    L=4h^2/R^2.                                             (1)

The record stays occupied. For a waiting time w since formation,

    m(w)=<n>(w)=1-L sin^2(omega_0 w/2).                       (2)

Introduce an independent diagnostic detuning -f(t)n. This is a declared
linear-response probe, not a new formation interaction. With the convention

    chi_F(t,t')=(i/hbar)theta(t-t')<[n(t),n(t')]>,             (3)

where the expectation is in the actual endpoint |n=1>, direct two-level
evolution gives, for lag u>=0,

    chi_F(w+u,w)=2h^2 epsilon/(hbar R^3)
       *{[1-cos(omega_0 w)]sin(omega_0 u)
                         +sin(omega_0 w)[1-cos(omega_0 u)]}.  (4)

One derivation evolves the initial Bloch vector under
(-2h,0,epsilon)/R, applies the infinitesimal z rotation generated by the
probe, and evolves for a further lag u. In particular,

    chi_F(u,0)=0                                             (5)

for every u, even though the link is fully occupied at that endpoint.
An instantaneous n kick changes only the phase of an n eigenstate.
Equation (5) concerns an impulse at the endpoint, not a detuning left on
for a finite interval; later source times have nonzero response.

Consequently, replacing the just-formed state by a stationary positive
susceptibility fails exactly. The constructive replacement requires only
the known preparation age and the two-time kernel (4), not cooling,
damping, a freely chosen link occupation, or a second fitted function.

If a phase-mixed preparation or a declared uniform averaging over a full
hold period is actually appropriate, it removes the energy coherence.
Its ground-minus-excited population is epsilon/R, and its response is

    m_bar=1-L/2,
    chi_bar(omega)=2h^2 epsilon/
                  {R^2[R^2-hbar^2(omega+i0)^2]}.             (6)

This is epsilon/R times the conditional-ground susceptibility, not that
ground susceptibility itself. It is passive for epsilon>0. Averaging is
not a derivation of physical decoherence, and (6) is not the response of
each pure state along the hold orbit. Its inverse has the fixed coefficients

    chi_bar^-1=D_F-Z_F(omega+i0)^2,
    D_F=R^4/(2h^2 epsilon),
    Z_F=hbar^2 R^2/(2h^2 epsilon),
    D_F/Z_F=omega_0^2.                                     (7)

These response coefficients are derived, not independently adjustable.
No spatial gradient stiffness or interaction range follows from one link.

A parameter-free cross-prediction is especially useful. Determine L and
omega_0 from the unforced hold occupation or the carrier measurement in
section 2. They imply

    h=(hbar omega_0/2)sqrt(L),
    epsilon=hbar omega_0 sqrt(1-L),
    hbar omega_0 chi_bar(0)=(L/2)sqrt(1-L).                  (8)

Thus the independently measured source response has no remaining free
amplitude. The waiting-time response (4) is fixed by the same two numbers.

The source protocol matters even for a static comparison. Differentiating
the family of independently phase-mixed preparations changes its energy
populations as well as its Hamiltonian. At fixed h,

    d m_bar(epsilon)/d epsilon=4h^2 epsilon/R^4
                              =2 chi_bar(0).               (8a)

This is not the fixed-prepared-state, adiabatically switched response in
(6). For a constant small detuning f switched on at the original formation
endpoint, integrating the actual kernel (4) instead gives, at fixed finite
time t,

    delta m(t)/f=2h^2 epsilon/(hbar R^3)
                  *{2[1-cos(omega_0 t)]/omega_0
                                             -t sin(omega_0 t)}. (8b)

Its initial term is h^2 epsilon t^4/(6hbar^4), not the quadratic term of
the stationary response. The secular factor in this linear derivative
expresses a shifted oscillation frequency; it does not justify extending
finite-f linear response to arbitrarily long times. Repreparation, phase
averaging, and response after preparation must not be interchanged.

For contrast, a blank link with copying off has H=C-a n-hX and starts
in n=0. Its phase-averaged population imbalance is -a/sqrt(a^2+4h^2),
so its stationary susceptibility has the opposite sign:

    chi_bar_blank(omega)=-2h^2 a/
      {(a^2+4h^2)[a^2+4h^2-hbar^2(omega+i0)^2]}.             (9)

This is an active, not passive, phase-averaged preparation when a>0.
Formation changes both the Hamiltonian through q and its occupied state;
changing only a classical mean-link value misses this distinction.

## 2. A fresh carrier and a record-cost probe share the same occupation

Let t_hop denote the native carrier hopping coefficient, to distinguish
it from time, and lambda_J the native degree-energy coefficient. With
copying off, inject a fresh carrier localized at vertex u, independently
of the actual graph/record state rho_F(w). The Hamiltonian retains native
graph flips and the carrier coupling

    K_G=epsilon_psi I+lambda_J D_G-t_hop A_G.

For an incident possible edge e=(u,v), exact short-time perturbation gives

    kappa_e(w):=lim_{tau->0}P(u->v;w,tau)/tau^2
               =t_hop^2 <n_e>(w)/hbar^2.                  (10)

The operator identity n_e^2=n_e is essential. Replacing the graph by its
mean and then squaring a hopping amplitude would instead produce
<n_e>^2 and is generally wrong. Graph-only terms cannot move the carrier
at first order; diagonal carrier degree terms cannot do so either.
Equation (10) therefore allows arbitrary formed graph correlations and
all native flips. The remainder is generally O(tau^3), becoming even
for the real endpoint preparation and real hold Hamiltonian. Summing,

    kappa_u^out=t_hop^2 <d_u>/hbar^2.                        (11)

The native coefficient of inserting an occupied record into a declared
blank register v, while holding the other state variables fixed, is

    mu_v^op=epsilon_r-lambda <d_v>.                         (12)

If blankness is conditioned on, the expectation in (12) must use that
conditioned state. This is an operational insertion-work coefficient,
not an asserted inertial mass, equilibrium chemical potential, or a free
physical insertion protocol. As a coefficient field it is defined at
all vertices, even where an additional insertion is not available.
Equations (11)-(12) give the exact comparison

    kappa_v^out=t_hop^2/(lambda hbar^2)
                              *(epsilon_r-mu_v^op).        (13)

For the exact one-link formation endpoint, the fresh carrier curvature
has risen from zero to t_hop^2/hbar^2, while the cost of an occupied record
at the other, still-blank endpoint has fallen from epsilon to
epsilon-lambda=a>0. No equilibrium or mean-field closure enters this
shared prediction. During the subsequent hold, both quantities follow
the actual m(w) in (2).

Under the feedback ablation with other coefficients fixed, the independent
link starts blank and has occupation at most 4h^2/(a^2+4h^2) at every time.
The target endpoint carrier curvature therefore exceeds every ablated
link curvature by at least

    t_hop^2 a^2/[hbar^2(a^2+4h^2)].                        (14)

This arm is an explicit mechanistic comparison, not an equal-energy
claim about the evolved target and ablated states.

Finite-time carrier transport does not follow just by inserting m(w)
into K_G. Already for one link, the exact carrier parity sectors have

    H_s^car=(common+epsilon_psi)I-hX+z_s n,
    z_s=-epsilon+lambda_J-s t_hop,       s=+1,-1.

If U_s=exp(-iH_s^car tau/hbar), inclusive arrival is exactly

    P_R(tau;w)=1/2{1-Re Tr[U_+ rho_F(w) U_-^dagger]}.        (15)

This retains graph/carrier entanglement and degree backreaction. It also
shows why one link's response gap is not automatically a common clock
for all carrier spectral lines.

## 3. One many-link kernel, local and homogeneous projections

For an actual formed multi-link state, define its full native two-time
response, including all degree interactions and retained link flips,

    chi_ef(t,t')=(i/hbar)theta(t-t')<[n_e(t),n_f(t')]>.       (16)

Let C_ve be the unsigned incidence matrix of possible links. A declared
record-equivalent diagnostic source j_v produces edge detunings
f=lambda C^T j; it is not a replacement of binary record operators by
continuously tunable dynamical occupations. Write Phi=C chi C^T. Then
the same state, same source, and same kernel predict

    delta mu_v^op(t)/delta j_w(t')=-lambda^2 Phi_vw(t,t'),
    delta kappa_v^out(t)/delta j_w(t')=
                         t_hop^2 lambda Phi_vw(t,t')/hbar^2. (17)

The curvature measurement is made by a newly injected carrier after the
source history. There are no independent fitted attraction and transport
functions in (17). In a stationary passive state the static relaxation
functional is -lambda^2 j^T C chi(0) C^T j/2 when the usual adiabatic
linear-response energy interpretation applies. Positivity makes that
quadratic functional nonpositive, but does NOT make every off-diagonal
pair coefficient attractive. One shared passive link gives a local
cooperative insertion response. Nonstationary kernels may change sign.

For M=sum_e n_e, the homogeneous edge source -F(t)M has

    chi_MM=sum_ef chi_ef,
    kappa_bar^out=t_hop^2 <M>/(N hbar^2),
    mu_bar^op=epsilon_r-lambda <M>/N,                       (18)

where the vertex average is over the 2N vertices of one bipartite slab.
Their responses and time derivatives are fixed by this projection of
the SAME kernel. Source work satisfies dot W=-dot F <M>.
The underlying incidence growth, sum_v dot<d_v>=2 dot<M>, is exact.
It is not physical volume expansion: the BS Hilbert space still has a
fixed node/layer inventory. A physical stress or scale factor needs an
independent operational volume/contact definition. It cannot be obtained
by relabeling <M> or omitting the absolute energy constant.

For the actually formed one-link hold, energy conservation explicitly
retains that ledger:

    <H_hold>=C,
    h<X>(w)=epsilon[1-m(w)].                               (19)

The oscillating graph-plus-feedback energy is compensated by flip energy.
The earlier formation cost epsilon-a-lambda=0 remains intact. Response
measurements do not determine the absolute constant C.

## 4. Smallest independent test and the remaining bridge

The existing exact formation endpoint supplies the initial state; no new
formation parameters are required. Hold with copying off. At declared
ages w, measure the fresh-carrier transfer curvature and apply a weak
independent n-detuning impulse. Equations (2), (4), (10), and (12) predict
all outcomes. At w=0 the curvature is maximal but impulse response is
zero. At half a hold period the response is twice the stationary sine
kernel; quarter-period ages include the additional 1-cos term. A declared
phase average must satisfy the amplitude relation (8), with L and omega_0
determined in a separate unforced measurement. No second response fit is
allowed. A full native formation-plus-one-carrier calculation is one
possible subsequent check; none is executed in this note.

This is a constructive common-response bridge, not a completed universal
clock or gravity theory. Its shortest collective continuation is to
produce an overlapping multi-link state by the same BS formation rules
at fixed microscopic coefficients, calculate its actual chi_ef, and test
both a localized source and the uniform source with (17)-(18). The native
h_N=Omega/N, fixed copying amplitude, overlapping degree costs, writer
capacity, controller work, and preparation history must all be retained.
Independent-cell tiling, an arbitrary smooth mean geometry, and remote
H6 history data do not establish that continuation.
