## Decision

The native rules contain real mechanisms that change a held subsystem's
spectral populations. They are not limited to dephasing. However, none of the
inspected completed constructions specifies preparation of the current all-N
first-degree-tie response ground from empty edges with the same coefficients,
records, sources and energy ledger. The missing premise is an owned formation
and boundary protocol for THAT family, not an absence of all native exchange
operators.

The shortest next step requiring no new formation premise is consequently
the actual empty-preparation two-time response of the already specified full
parent. It must retain its age dependence and cannot borrow the stationary
ground susceptibility. The exact controls below give it a useful analytical
starting point without a selected favorable age. A dissipative or growing-layer
formation claim would instead be a clearly declared new completion, with
incoming state, retained factors, energy destination and source work supplied.

## What is already an actual native mechanism

BS08 and BS10 define writer/carrier-to-record copies, not just copies out of
records. Take H_h to contain the canonical BS incidence, carrier, feedback
and onsite terms, with ALL copy couplings and other ports off. For
K_v=K_(w_v to r_v), direct commutation of BS10--11 gives

    [H_h,K_v]=i (epsilon_r-lambda_R d_v) Y_(w_v to r_v),
    d<H_h>/dt=(g_v/hbar)<(epsilon_r-lambda_R d_v)Y_v>

when that single copy g_v K_v is active. Here Y is exactly the Hermitian
current in `NATIVE_TEMPORAL_KERNEL.md` equation (1). Other copies add their
own commutators. The displayed simple formula is for writer copying;
carrier-controlled copying also requires its carrier-hopping commutators.
The binding energy is retained. The copy therefore can change held-energy
populations, while complete fixed-H energy remains conserved. With a prescribed
envelope, the complete external work is integral dot(g_v)<K_v> dt; changing
other coefficients adds their corresponding work. This does not prove cooling.

## Actual empty state controls without a new formation rule

Use exactly the reviewed tie parent and empty-edge initial state, with
Hbar=H-C_N and T=2U(C_L-C_R). Define T_H(t)=exp(iHt/hbar)T exp(-iHt/hbar).
For the PLUS source H+theta T use the causal convention

    chi(t,s)=(i/hbar)<empty|[T_H(t),T_H(s)]|empty>, t>=s,
    delta<T(t)>=-integral_0^t chi(t,s) delta theta(s) ds.

Since T|empty>=0, the exact preparation-time impulse response is

    chi(t,0)=0 for every t.                              (1)

This is not zero response to a source held on for an interval, or to an
impulse applied at a later age. Those histories sample s>0. Ground-state
positivity cannot replace this actual initial-state result.

There is a small exact hand-algebra control for delayed responses. Hbar first
creates one-edge states, all annihilated by T. In Hbar^2|empty>, each unordered
two-edge graph has amplitude 2h^2. Only the two-edge stars survive T; there
are `2N binom(N,2)=N^2(N-1)` of them, with T=+/-2U. Thus, for each fixed N,

    W_T(t):=||T exp(-iHbar t/hbar)|empty>||^2
      =4U^2 h^4 N^2(N-1)(t/hbar)^4+O_N((t/hbar)^6).     (2)

The complete state remains even under layer exchange, so its T mean is zero.
Reality of Hbar, T^2 and the initial basis state makes W_T even in t; no
fifth-order term is hidden. This is a fixed-N Taylor coefficient with an
unproved uniform remainder, not an all-time or large-N response bound.
`critical_path_native` independently checked the star count and coefficient
by hand; no machine polynomial calculation was performed.

For arbitrary ages an exact Cauchy--Schwarz control is

    |chi(t,s)| <= 2 sqrt[W_T(t) W_T(s)]/hbar.             (3)

Equation (3) follows by applying Cauchy--Schwarz to T_H(t)|empty> and
T_H(s)|empty>. Positive W_T is a fluctuation weight, not a passivity sign
or static susceptibility. A useful continuation is therefore control of the
complete two-time kernel and its finite-window source work for this actual
preparation, retaining contacts and any source dependence of the preparation.
It should derive its allowed age/window rather than select one after its sign.

## Existing constraints that must not be rerun as new discoveries

The reviewed empty-preparation ground bound `p_ground<=8/(N+8)` remains exact
under the fixed closed H. Neither (1)--(3) nor local apparent equilibration
changes it. A genuine same-H ground endpoint needs the separately accounted
energy decrease identified in that bound.

D/`NATIVE_INCIDENCE_FORMATION_SPEED_BOUND_V001.md` already proves, for every
joint state with `[H_rest(t),M]=0`,

    |sqrt(<M>_t)-sqrt(<M>_s)| <= (Omega/hbar)|t-s|.

Its empty-start consequence is `<M>_t<=(Omega t/hbar)^2` and a necessary
time at least `(hbar/Omega)sqrt(N d_target)` for mean degree d_target.
Native BS10 record writers commute with M and do not evade this bound even
though they change held-energy populations. Incidence-changing ports or a
changed flip scaling require a new analysis; nonempty initial edges require
their explicit initial term. This older result is not newly established by
specializing it to the tie parent. It also does not bound the energy-weighted
W_T or the inverse-gap susceptibility without further moment control.

No physical time, position, volume, density, cooling temperature, negative
pressure or astronomical normalization is supplied by this decision. All
eleven scientific lanes remain open and every physical promotion remains
false. The useful new work is actual-state source response, unless and until
the specific additional formation inputs above are independently supplied.
