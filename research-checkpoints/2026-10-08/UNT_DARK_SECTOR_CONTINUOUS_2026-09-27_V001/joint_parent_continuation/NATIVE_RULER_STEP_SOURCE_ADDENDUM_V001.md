For the actual inherited rectangular source H_f=H+fK, define the derivative
of the carrier arrival projector at fixed duration tau:

    R_step(tau)=d[exp(iH_f tau/hbar) P exp(-iH_f tau/hbar)]/df|0.

Because [P,K]=[ad_H P,K]=[ad_H^2 P,K]=0, the fourth-order expansion has only
one surviving source insertion, ad_K ad_H^3 P. With conventional carrier Y_C
and the saved minus-Pauli record Y_rec,

    R_step(tau)=lambda_R t h X_a Y_C Y_rec
                           *tau^4/(24 hbar^4)+O(tau^5).

This has the same operator coefficient as the cubic ideal-impulse response,
but a DIFFERENT time power and source dimension. A finite step is not obtained
by replacing the impulse strength eta by a source amplitude f. The full
nonstationary Kubo integral, or exact source derivative of the propagator,
supplies the response beyond the leading term.

The old ages0,.25,.5,1,2, durations.08,.04,.02,.01 and plus/minus amplitudes
.01,.005 already specify this source family. Reading carrier P on those
same exact trials adds an operational readout without fitting a new source.
The derivative can be arbitrarily small or zero for a particular actual
state. No sign, nonzero response or monotone delay is prescribed as success.

For a finite step the source-work ledger is unchanged:

    W_on=f<K>_initial, W_off=-f<K>_after,
    Delta<H>=W_on+W_off.

At zero amplitude its derivative is <K>_initial-<K(tau)>_unperturbed. The
impulse work derivative instead is (i/hbar)<[K,H]>; keep the two distinct.
The R-dephased baseline/null and exact feedback-off null apply to the step's
LINEAR response as well. Finite quadratic effects need not vanish.

Finally the compact native action I is conserved during each held sector's
phase rotation. Thus its measured source moment can change with held age at
fixed I, without amplitude hardening or a changing Hamiltonian. This is a
specific retained phase variable, not noise to delete from the native source
mapping before comparing it with an effective model.
