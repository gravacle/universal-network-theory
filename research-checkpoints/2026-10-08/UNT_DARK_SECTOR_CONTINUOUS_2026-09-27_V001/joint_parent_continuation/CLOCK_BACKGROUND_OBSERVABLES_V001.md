## Acoustic and diffusion clocks distinguish the normalization

At fixed endpoint z_e and sound-speed history, r_s scales by sqrt(F).
Consequently D_M/r_s, H r_s and theta_s=r_s/D_M are unchanged. This is a
useful analytic degeneracy, not permission to treat the ruler as independent.

Actual recombination and drag are not fixed endpoints. Atomic rates are per
proper time, dt/dz=-1/[(1+z)H] and d tau_gamma/dz=c sigma_T n_e/[(1+z)H].
Ionization evolution and the visibility/drag criteria change when H does.
At fixed ionization history and endpoint, the photon diffusion integral has
r_D^2 proportional to 1/H, so r_D scales as F^(1/4) and

    r_D/r_s scales as F^(-1/4).

This is a distinct provisional prediction to retain for a viable common
model; an actual damping-tail test needs self-consistent atomic evolution,
mode driving, projection and the measured estimator. Do not rerun the old
conditional CMB histories as if they belonged to this changed action.

## A physical observer map must change more than a coordinate label

With ordinary matter and photons coupled to the same metric, monotonic
relabeling T->f(T) leaves the normalized preferred normal unchanged and
does not alter spectral redshift, arrival-time dilation or atomic time.

For comparison, a NEW universal conformal matter metric
g_tilde=C(xi)^2 g would have d tau_tilde=C dt, a_tilde=C a and
H_tilde=(H+C_dot/C)/C. With conserved photons it retains distance duality
and spectroscopic arrival-time dilation. It is a changed matter coupling,
not a postprocessing choice: the state equation, stress transfer and metric
equations must be varied together. Choosing C(t) to reproduce desired
distances just adds an arbitrary fitted history.

Its weak-field potentials obey Phi_tilde=Phi+c^2 delta ln C and
Psi_tilde=Psi-c^2 delta ln C. Thus a pure conformal extra force does not
add to the Weyl lensing sum. A map depending only on filtered homogeneous
acceleration does nothing, since that acceleration vanishes in FLRW.

An inhomogeneous, source-selected light cone is a different open route:
regional expansion/clock geometry need not equal a volume-average scale
factor. It must predict distances AND redshift drift from the same source
state, without independently chosen lapse profiles. Neither a new matter
metric nor an inhomogeneous geometry is adopted as a fit here. Their actual
shared dynamical source, optical coupling and stable initial-value problem
must be supplied. The next task is a constrained ordinary-source evolution
and optical diagnostic, not another homogeneous normalization scan.
