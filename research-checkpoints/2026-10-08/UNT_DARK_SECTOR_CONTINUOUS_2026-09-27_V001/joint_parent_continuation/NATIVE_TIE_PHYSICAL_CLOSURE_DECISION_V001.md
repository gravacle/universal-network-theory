worker, model import, coefficient choice, new growth/reset/bath rule or edit
to frozen science/URM. This is an identifiability audit of the specified

## Decision

The existing native action does **not uniquely fix** this tie family's
physical clock/ruler/volume assignment or initial/formation state. The
remaining shared-universe step is not just an unevaluated matrix element.
It needs a specified physical-source and preparation completion, or an
independent derivation that actually supplies those currently open slots.
Further bare finite-N response calculations cannot select among the
inequivalent completions exhibited below.

This does not require predicting an SI second or metre without calibration.
Universal calibration and environmental initial/boundary data are legitimate
model inputs. The important distinction is between calibrating one declared
law and choosing its functional source, volume or preparation law after
seeing a desired galaxy/expansion response. No such choice is made here.

## Three concrete non-uniqueness witnesses

### 1. Same reference parent, different physical pressure

Specialize the already written operational alternatives A and B to this
blank-carrier tie sector. The complete carrier transport operator annihilates
this sector. Put E_b=2N U_d d_*^2 and retain every other onsite constant in H.
Under their additional three-dimensional convention V=V_N s^3, they give

    E_A(s)=s^(-1)H,
    E_B(s)=H-E_b I+E_b s^3 I,
    E_A(1)=E_B(1)=H.                                    (1)

For the SAME fixed native state at reference, the complete energy-conjugate
volume derivatives are

    p_A=<H>/(3V_N),       p_B=-E_b/V_N.                  (2)

No new fitted numerical coefficient is needed for this counterexample.
Which existing term changes with physical volume is the additional physical
postulate. The frozen reference Hamiltonian, its eigenvectors, source-free
histories and opposite-layer response cannot determine that deformation.
Neither A nor B is adopted by this memo. They are different models away
from reference, not two coordinate descriptions of one completed model.

More generally, an unbound family H(V,theta)+c(V)I with c(V_N)=0 preserves
all normalized dynamics at prescribed classical V histories while shifting
pressure by -c'(V). A complete actuator or coherent controller can detect
it; c of a dynamical volume is not an irrelevant identity on the full
apparatus. Once a full deformation law is frozen this is NOT permission
to change it. Inside the separately adopted metric-only zero/two-derivative
endpoint, the corresponding allowed vacuum freedom is one matched constant,
not an arbitrary fitted expansion history. This is the existing vacuum
identifiability result, applied here rather than claimed as a new theorem.

### 2. A bare response does not set its physical normalization

The tie's adopted opposite-layer affine source is theta T, with
T=2U(C_L-C_R). If calibrated endpoint locations are +/-ell_N/2, the later
weak-lapse dictionary additionally sets

    theta=a_ext ell_N/(2c^2),   Z=ell_N T/(2c^2),
    chi_Z(0)/V_N=ell_N^2 chi_T(0)/(4c^4 V_N).            (3)

Neither ell_N nor V_N, nor their size dependence, is fixed by BS06 or by
the tie's degree count. The recently proved fixed-age bare-response bounds
therefore cannot simply be renamed physical susceptibility-per-volume bounds.
No size-dependent ruler/volume is chosen to defeat or enhance those bounds.

For a dimensionless native H_hat, an independently calibrated energy E_*
gives H_phys=E_* H_hat and t_phys=(hbar/E_*)tau_hat. With external physical
standards held fixed, assigning different E_*, ell_N and V_N leaves the same
dimensionless computation but changes its time, energy density and (3).
For static response the conversion is

    rho_E=E_*<H_hat>/V_N,
    chi_Z/V_N=E_* ell_N^2 chi_That/(4c^4 V_N).            (4)

Once those physical standards are measured, their calibrations cannot be
varied without changing predictions. Equation (4) identifies missing input,
not a new symmetry of a fully calibrated physical theory. Specifying an
energy clock alone still does not supply a metric/volume law. The known
state-dependent record-phase frequency is likewise not an independent
universal lapse unless its feedback correction and reference are retained.

### 3. Same H and symmetries, different states and responses

Both the empty-edge density matrix and the unique finite-N ground projector
are covariant admissible preparations of this held family. The BS mission
contract does not choose between them. Their consequences already differ:
the ground has strictly positive finite-N static opposite-layer response;
the empty state has exactly chi(t,0)=0, a genuinely two-time delayed kernel,
and a non-passive weak cyclic source-work direction. It is not stationary.

For fixed unforced H the empty preparation's ground population stays fixed
and is bounded by 8/(N+8). Merely waiting or energy-basis dephasing cannot
replace it by the ground. Native exchange/formation operators exist, but
turning them on requires their actual initial registers, correlations,

The source-only formation audit already distinguishes these mechanisms from
an owned preparation of this same tie family. No universal failure of other

## What is a calculation, and what must first be supplied?

Given one complete source family and initial/port protocol, its native
two-time responses, currents, work, finite-size limits and possible emergent
localization are mathematical questions. A specified relational construction
could in principle derive useful geometry and a scaling window; the BS seed
does not forbid that. But its unfilled physical-source derivatives and
initial-state choice cannot be determined by calculating more consequences
of H at one reference source.

The shortest next native-to-universe step is one explicit completion record:
the physical energy/clock calibration; the operational localization/ruler
and volume map with their scale range; the complete same-energy lapse,
volume and metric/source variations with contacts and absolute-work reference;
and one preparation/boundary rule applied to all tested environments with
every energy transfer counted. Each entry must be marked derived, adopted,
calibrated or environmental input. The existing A/B readout and shared-work
identities can then reject that proposed completion independently. They do
not select it by themselves. No new sweep is justified until a specific
unresolved consequence of such an owned completion changes the decision.

If the intended next route is an effective spacetime model, it may be
adopted and compared honestly without first proving a unique BS continuum.
Its postulates and calibrated freedoms must then be counted in the comparison
with DM/DE models, not relabeled native predictions. Conditional tests of
the already adopted cosmological action remain useful and are not blocked
merely because the native dictionary is incomplete. Passing them would not
retroactively derive that dictionary or its galaxy formation state.
