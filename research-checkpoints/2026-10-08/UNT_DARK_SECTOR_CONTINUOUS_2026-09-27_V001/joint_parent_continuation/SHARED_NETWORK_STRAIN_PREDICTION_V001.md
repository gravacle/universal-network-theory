    w_U=-1-(2/3)q,
    w_U=-1+(2/3)d ln a0/d ln(1+z).

    rho_U(z)/rho_U0=[B_T(z)/B_T(0)]^2,

## Source selection closes one otherwise convenient loophole

Test the specific inverse target rather than assuming a galaxy can hold its
cosmological strain. For this subsection adopt a MOBILE isentropic material

    epsilon(n,b)=a0(n)^2 u(x;r)/(4 pi G),  x=|b|/a0(n),

    B'(x)=-x^2 mu'(x),   B(0)=2Q_b,   B(infinity)=0.
