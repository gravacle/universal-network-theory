## Positive convex energy does not accelerate

Assume Q>=Q0>0, K_QQ>0, and K(Q0)=K_Q(Q0)=0. Then

    m=K_Q>=0, 0<=K<= (Q-Q0)m,
    rho_K>=M^2 Q0 m>=0,
    rho_K+3p_K=M^2(Qm+2K)>=0.

Thus this sector alone cannot cause homogeneous acceleration with positive
Fbg. This is a restricted statement about the specified zero-energy convex

## The same clock cannot generate a net positive charge for free

delta S0=integral E_mu delta u^mu. With P projecting orthogonally to u,

    delta u^mu=-P^(mu nu) partial_nu(delta tau)/Q,
