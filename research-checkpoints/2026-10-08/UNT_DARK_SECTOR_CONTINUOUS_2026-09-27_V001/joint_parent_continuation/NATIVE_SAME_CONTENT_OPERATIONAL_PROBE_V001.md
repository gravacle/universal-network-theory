## The writer preparation distinguishes the two existing contents

At fixed local degree let delta=epsilon_r-lambda_R*d and keep a writer in
content 0. The record qutrit Hamiltonians for an empty carrier site, a
content-0 carrier, and a content-1 carrier have coupling vectors

    v_empty=(g_w,0),  v_0=(g_w+g_p,0),  v_1=(g_w,g_p),
    h_s=delta*q^r+v_s,0*X_0+v_s,1*X_1.

For A_s=||v_s||>0 and Delta_s=sqrt(delta^2+4 A_s^2), the exact spectrum is

    (delta-Delta_s)/2,   delta,   (delta+Delta_s)/2.           (1)

The middle state is the content vector orthogonal to v_s. The normalized
lower eigenvector has blank amplitude c_s and content amplitude -s_s*v_s/A_s,
where c_s^2=(1+delta/Delta_s)/2 and s_s^2=(1-delta/Delta_s)/2.
Lower-state overlaps are therefore

    <g_s|g_t>=c_s*c_t+s_s*s_t*(v_s dot v_t)/(A_s*A_t).        (2)

At inherited g_w=g_p=1 and delta=0, the lower energies are -1,-2,-sqrt(2).
The empty/content-0 overlap is 1, whereas empty/content-1 is
(1+1/sqrt(2))/2. A two-endpoint lower-record compression would give hopping
t for content 0 and t*(3+2sqrt(2))/8 for content 1.

These are CONDITIONAL spectral/compression results. The actual blank records
are not lower eigenstates, so these numbers are not their measured transport
coefficients or a permissible equilibrium replacement. The full native
action remains content-exchange symmetric when writers are exchanged too;
the chosen writer state is not invariant under that exchange. This ordinary
medium dependence is neither extra mass nor a violation of gravitational
universality. It shows why that universality cannot be inferred merely
from using two labels with the same bare carrier energy. A universal
energy-source test must retain their internal dressing and energy.

There is also an exact ACTUAL-BLANK control on a fixed path segment at
delta=0. Its overlap for two conditional carrier labels is

    <B|exp(i h_s x)exp(-i h_t x)|B>
      =cos(A_s*x)cos(A_t*x)
        +(v_s dot v_t)/(A_s*A_t)*sin(A_s*x)sin(A_t*x).

For empty versus content 0 at the inherited coefficients this is cos(x),
although the two lower eigenvectors coincide. Conditional lower-state
overlap is therefore not a substitute for the actual blank-state memory.
The complete hopping, current and absolute addition energies remain in
either calculation; independently shifting each species' energy zero
would corrupt the shared energy-source comparison.

## Two local channels define source and probe operationally

Use the existing one-content parent with uniform fixed-Q carrier mixture
sigma_0 on V eligible bulk vertices, blank records and content-0 writers.
A supplied regular bipartite bulk with the isolated clock pair of V116
has V=2N-2 here; the clocks are excluded from carrier eligibility. This is
a declared diagnostic preparation, not a claimed native formation outcome.

Choose four distinct bulk sites a,b,c,d. On source pair A=(a,b) and probe
pair B=(c,d), use the native-content transfer operators

    A=|0,B><B,0|_(a,b),  B=|0,B><B,0|_(c,d),
    M_A=p_a-p_b,         M_B=p_c-p_d.

For either transfer T, the same physical channel as earlier has
K_1=sqrt(z)T, K_0=sqrt(I-z T^dagger T), 0<=z<=1. The disjoint channels
commute and are trace preserving without postselection. Exactly on this
initial diagonal family,

    sigma_eta,xi=sigma_0(1+eta M_A)(1+xi M_B).                (3)

The map is NOT generally Id+z D_T on arbitrary off-diagonal states; its
tangent at zero is D_T rho=T rho T^dagger-{T^dagger T,rho}/2.
Equation (3) does not apply unchanged if the channels act on an aged state.
Their exact action on the specified initial diagonal state proves (3),
even though the fixed-Q background does not factor across sites.

For any fixed readout O and the common native evolution, define

    o_B(x)=Tr[sigma_0 M_B O(x)],
    o_AB(x)=Tr[sigma_0 M_A M_B O(x)]
           =<D_A^dagger D_B^dagger O(x)>.                   (4)

The mixed response measures the change caused by preparation A in the
response to preparation B. It does not identify individual identical
particles, and it does not require a pure tagged-particle state. Local
occupations, currents and both quadratures of one-content coherence can
all be retained as readouts. Those signed responses are not normalized
one-particle density matrices. A direct source-only signal is removed by
the mixed derivative, not renamed as probe acceleration.

At fixed generator age the four-arm contrast is exactly eta*xi*o_AB:
subtract the source-only and probe-only arms from the joint arm and add
the untouched arm. No small-parameter extrapolation is required for this
particular bilinear state family. That statement does not automatically
extend to a parameter-dependent actual-clock readout.

If O initially has no support on A, then D_A^dagger O=0 and the mixed
signal initially vanishes. More generally, locality and complete positivity
preclude a distant instantaneous preparation signal. Fixed-Q correlations
alone do not permit signaling. A later nonzero response can still be
ordinary interaction, exclusion or record memory rather than geometry.
A global product of readouts in two separate regions is not a propagation
witness: its mixed response can reflect the two local actions without
communication. Use a declared local output near the probe region.

## The costs and response normalization are fixed

For V>=4 define

    s=Q(V-Q)/[V(V-1)],
    kappa=Q(Q-1)(V-Q)(V-Q-1)/[V(V-1)(V-2)(V-3)].

Kappa is the probability that specified sites in both pairs are occupied
while the other two are blank. Fixed-Q counting gives

    ||sigma_0 M_A M_B||_1=4 kappa.                          (5)

The positive and negative parts each have trace 2 kappa. The mixed signal
is identically zero at Q=0,1,V-1,V. These are protocol-null sectors, not
evidence against any physical force. At fixed interior filling the norm
is nonzero. The earlier local full/reference comparison consequently bounds
the fixed-generator-time mixed readout error by 4 kappa E_O.

Let m_a=1,m_b=-1 and n_c=1,n_d=-1. Every mixed one-site occupation tangent
is zero, as is each mixed pair tangent not joining A to B. For a cross pair,
delta_AB<p_i p_j>=kappa m_i n_j. Blank-record and diagonal-preparation
means then give the COMPLETE initial mixed energy coefficient

    delta_AB E[L]=-j kappa sum_(i in A,j in B)
                          Adj_ij (L_i+L_j)m_i n_j.          (6)

All hopping/copy means vanish initially, but their operators remain in H.
At L=1 the coefficient is -2j kappa times the signed cross adjacency.
It is exactly zero if the two pair supports have no connecting edge.
Single-channel mean-energy changes also vanish on the regular bulk at
uniform lapse. For nonuniform L retain their previous full spatial-energy
formulas; they are not generally zero.

The initial entropy change is exactly

    Delta S=-s[f(eta)+f(xi)],
    f(z)=(1+z)ln(1+z)+(1-z)ln(1-z).                        (7)

Disjoint trace-preserving channels preserve the other pair's marginal.
Taking the logarithm of the likelihood factors in (3) proves additivity
here; it is not a general entropy additivity assumption for correlated
states. Zero initial mean energy does not make either apparatus free.
Their entropy, controller correlations, reset/readout and source-work
obligations remain, as in the original transfer protocol.

## A mixed response needs its own clock correction

Subscripts A,B,AB denote parameter derivatives at fixed generator age x,
with no factorial in the eta*xi coefficient. For a nonzero clock amplitude
beta and rate nu=dTheta/dx, put

    phi_A=Im(beta_A/beta),  phi_B=Im(beta_B/beta),
    phi_AB=Im(beta_AB/beta-beta_A*beta_B/beta^2),
    u_A=phi_A/nu,           u_B=phi_B/nu.

At common phase the exact mixed derivative is

    o_AB|Theta=o_AB-u_B dot(o_A)-u_A dot(o_B)+u_A*u_B ddot(o)
      -(dot(o)/nu)*[phi_AB-u_B dot(phi_A)-u_A dot(phi_B)
                                      +u_A*u_B dot(nu)].   (8)

It follows by differentiating the implicit fixed-phase age twice. The
phase origin is common and parameter independent here. An explicitly
elapsed clock with a parameter-dependent initial phase requires subtracting
its initial derivatives; an unowned reset is not allowed. Applying only
the first-order clock subtraction to sigma_0 M_A M_B misses terms in (8).

In the isolated-clock NODE reference, both channels leave the clock
entirely unchanged, so beta_A=beta_B=beta_AB=0 and the native mixed response
equals (4) at x=Theta/Delta_c. In the full parent retain (8) and actual
clock bounds. Fixed-time error (5) alone does not justify interchanging
mixed clock derivatives and a large-size limit.

## The withheld prediction includes the entire interacting medium

To test a proposed source representation, replace A by a declared small
complete lapse L_v=1+eta ell_v(x), while retaining preparation B. Its
profile must be fixed from source-side evidence before these probe outputs.
Write the interaction-picture source V_ell(s)=sum_v ell_v(s)h_v(s), using
the full allocated energy, and a_c(x)=integral_0^x ell_c(s) ds.
For a profile initially independent of xi, the isolated-clock reference
predicts

    o_AB^lapse|Theta = i integral_0^x
           Tr[sigma_0 M_B [V_ell(s),O(x)]] ds
                             -a_c(x) dot(o_B(x)),           (9)

plus any actual readout/preparation contacts. This predicts the change in
the prepared probe response. It is not the ordinary baseline source kernel
and must not be replaced by a fitted one-body force. Native interactions,
record memory and source-particle recoil remain on BOTH sides.

Uniform ell provides an exact check: the raw term is a_c dot(o_B), so
it cancels at the internal clock, also for the nonstationary preparation.
The clock correction in (9) uses the baseline clock state, not its
trace-zero M_B-weighted state; the latter would incorrectly erase it.

If source-side matching gives coefficients c_mu(xi)=c_mu^0+xi*c_mu^1,
the mixed prediction additionally contains sum_mu c_mu^1 R_O,mu^Theta,
with the ordinary complete relational source kernel. Those derivatives
must come from the source law or source-side evidence, not the withheld
probe. Disjoint preparation supports do not establish negligible physical
backreaction. Preparation boundaries must also be retained if a source
acts during either channel.

For a fixed finite profile basis this gives a concrete source-side inverse
problem that RETAINS first probe backreaction. Let R_E,R_P be ordinary
relational source columns on source-side and withheld probe readouts, and
K_E^B,K_P^B their probe-preparation derivatives from (9). Stack unknowns
c=(c^0,c^1) and solve only the source-side system

    [ R_E     0   ] [c^0] = [b_A^E ],
    [ K_E^B   R_E ] [c^1]   [b_AB^E].                     (10)

The withheld mixed prediction is [K_P^B  R_P]c. If (10) is consistent,
it is unique exactly when the null space of the source-side block matrix
is contained in that of [K_P^B  R_P]. This is the earlier identifiability
criterion applied to the actual two-preparation experiment, not a license
to fit c^1 on probe outputs. Include any owned preparation/readout contacts
in both row sets. Source-side rows must be physically appropriate to this
representation: matching an entire prepared source state to a Hamiltonian
intervention is neither required nor generally possible.

The next useful comparison is therefore a SOURCE-DETERMINED joint response,
