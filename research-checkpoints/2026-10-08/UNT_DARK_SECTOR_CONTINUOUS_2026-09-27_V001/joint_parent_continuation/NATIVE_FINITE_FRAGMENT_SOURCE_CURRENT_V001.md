The smallest source-aware extension of the actual apparatus attaches one

    F_v = nu q_bv + lambda q_v X_bv,

They remain symbolic. Neither is fixed by the current BS input or selected

The original four blank preparations are retained exactly, tensor two blank

The endpoints remain Q=+/-ln(2)/3, not physical density measurements. Fragment

## 3. Exact current identities, not equality of the response

Write J_X=[H_X,Z_X]; the real energy-dipole current is i J_X/hbar. The endpoint
partition has the property [h_v,F_w]=0 for v!=w. Its edge flip, symmetric

cancel in the complete local energy difference. Also [F_Sigma,F_Delta]=0.
Direct expansion of (3) therefore gives

    [H'_A,Z'_A] = J_A tensor I_b,
    [H'_B,Z'_B] = J_B tensor I_b
                     +gamma0 [HQ,s] F_Delta.           (6)

The B remainder is the Q-controller current of added spatially located
energy. It is not a new gravitational law. It vanishes only under additional
conditions, for example a clamped Q, which are NOT imposed here. C-dependent
copying would add HC commutators and would not obey (6).

Equation (6) is a Schrödinger-picture operator identity. Even A's current
has different subsequent Heisenberg evolution under H'_A. It does not make
the full two-time response, spectrum, state or signed first moment equal.
Define the signed-moment operator

    M_X=[Z_X,[H_X,Z_X]]/hbar^2,
    M'_X=[Z'_X,[H'_X,Z'_X]]/hbar^2.                    (7)

For A the difference is gamma0[F_Delta,J_A]/hbar^2. In the actual cell the
native/edge part of J_A commutes with both record projectors. With

    M'_A-M_A tensor I_b

Thus the finite gap is not an automatically positive extra A source contact;

the formation controller would incorrectly erase the actual correction (8).

For B let D=gamma0[HQ,s]F_Delta and W=gamma0 s F_Delta. Then

    M'_B-M_B tensor I_b

Equations (8)-(9), including all signs, are a smaller discriminator than a

<0_b|X_b|0_b>=0 and q_b|0_b>=0. The terms linear in F in (8)-(9) have zero
blank-fragment expectation. The remaining B term is proportional to
F_Delta^2 and annihilates the original record blank. Hence

    <M'_A>_0=<M'_B>_0=0.                              (10)

Copying commutes with R=q_L+q_R, so the exact R-current operator is unchanged;
its later expectation is not. With u=t/hbar, f_j=s_j^-1 for A and f_j=1 for
B, the first nonzero populations are

    <R>(t)=2 g^2 f_j^2 u^2+O(u^4),
    <q_bL+q_bR>(t)=g^2 lambda^2 f_j^4 u^4/2+O(u^6).   (11)

The two-step record-then-copy amplitude at either endpoint is
-g lambda f_j^2 u^2/2. Thus the fragment starts exchanging energy only after
the original blank record formation has begun. No I/2 cooling coefficient
can be transplanted to (2).

   it contains one record and its fragment excited, and is orthogonal to

## 5. Paid finite energy, not a free bath or absolute closure

For fixed parameters H'_X is a finite closed Hamiltonian. Its exactly
conserved expectation is the sum of the original native/controller energies,
the two fragment gaps and both copy interactions, with the same A/B factors
as in (3). Fragment energy is neither dropped after an apparent cooling
event nor equated to escaping heat. There is no supplied irreversible sink.

If a deliberate external protocol changes lambda or nu, their work is
included by integrating <dH'/dlambda> dlambda and <dH'/dnu> dnu. If both copy
ports are removed suddenly, their switching work is

    W_copy_off=-lambda <a_X(Q)(q_L X_bL+q_R X_bR)>,
    a_A=s^-1, a_B=1.                                  (13)

Although initial mean switch-on work is zero in (2), that is not a statement
that preparation/support is free. If g is also removed, the existing owned
formation-port work must be counted as well. In general the exact external
work is the integral of <partial H'/partial theta> d theta for every changed
parameter theta. At the background L0=1,a=eta=0 its source/volume terms are
<Z'> da-<B'_eta> d eta, and its uniform-lapse term is <H'> dL0. Mixed
contacts from (4)-(5) are retained away from that background. All controller
interaction energies remain.
An autonomous switch would need a further explicitly specified finite
controller, initial state and interaction ledger. Those are not silently
provided by the notation lambda(t).

This closes the finite displayed energy balance conditional on its boundary
