## 2. State and source conditions for one conservative work function

Use eta for an EXTERNAL logarithmic length strain, distinct from the internal
quantum Q, and define alpha:=a_ext, the signed physical acceleration source

Let W(eta,alpha;I) be the complete per-cell work energy for one specified
material/preparation branch. I denotes its retained invariants, not a fitted
radial age or a freely reselected population at every source value.

One explicit finite-system realization, when justified, is

    W=sum_r p_r E_r(eta,alpha)+E_support(eta,alpha),         (1)

with fixed populations p_r transported adiabatically on a specified analytic
spectral branch. All source/controller and archive energies are included.
This does not require p_r to be passive, and does not prove stability. Gap,
adiabatic-error and preparation conditions must be checked; degeneracies
require the complete retained block, not an arbitrary eigenvector choice.
A genuine equilibrium free energy is another possible branch, but its bath,
entropy and heat-work prescription would be additional owned input.

Define the complete conjugate readouts

    D=partial_alpha W,
    B=-partial_eta W=3V*p,
    K_alpha=partial_alpha^2 W.                             (2)

Pressure is per calibrated branch volume. No ratio of separately averaged
quantum-volume readouts is substituted. The PLUS-source convention matters:

    K_alpha=<H_alphaalpha>-chi_ZZ(0),                      (3)

not chi_ZZ alone. Support/source contacts belong in (3). The affine native
lapse insertion has H_alphaalpha=0 within its own chart; the uninstantiated
physical metric or source-field contacts cannot be silently supplied by a
positive oscillator fit.

Commuting derivatives of one C3 work function give the new joint condition

    partial_alpha B=-partial_eta D,
    partial_alpha^2(3V*p)=-partial_eta K_alpha.             (4)

Eta and alpha are varied independently; V is independent of alpha in this
declared chart. Equivalently, for a reversible closed source loop with the
same final state/support branch,

    integral_loop [D d(alpha)-B d(eta)]=0.                 (5)

This is a compulsory cross-observable prediction. Computing both sides by
differentiating one arbitrarily chosen W would only check algebra; an
independent native/controller derivation or measured conjugate readouts are
needed to validate the proposed physical assignment.

## 3. Reflection and a concrete scalar-volume relaxation calculation

On a reflection-invariant branch, eta is even and alpha is odd. Therefore
W is even in alpha. This applies to a justified reflection-invariant branch,
NOT automatically to the original left-carrier blank, its finite-age state
or a selected numerical dephased comparator. Write locally

    W(eta,alpha)=U(eta)+K(eta)*alpha^2/2
                          +L(eta)*alpha^4/4+O(alpha^6).  (6)

Equation (4) becomes

    3V*[p(eta,alpha)-p(eta,0)]
        =-K'(eta)*alpha^2/2+O(alpha^4).                    (7)

Thus a claimed density dependence of the dipole curvature predicts a
field-induced pressure correction. It cannot be omitted while using the
same work function to calculate cosmological stress.

Now make an additional, explicit mechanical postulate: promote this eta to
a slow classical scalar volume coordinate with an owned kinetic term and
support energy, and consider its quasistatic minimum at fixed alpha. The
mechanism that reaches or reads that minimum must be owned independently;
positive stiffness alone does not create an autonomous attractor. This is NOT
the same as the already quantized three-site Q controller, nor a cosmological
metric equation. Include the support energy in U. If

    U'(eta0)=0,  K_eta=U''(eta0)>0,

the implicit-function expansion is

    eta_star(alpha)=eta0-K'(eta0)*alpha^2/(2K_eta)
                              +O(alpha^4),
    W_relaxed(alpha)=U(eta0)+K(eta0)*alpha^2/2
        +[L(eta0)/4-K'(eta0)^2/(8K_eta)]*alpha^4
        +O(alpha^6).                                      (8)

Reflection removes linear alpha--eta mixing, so stable scalar-volume
relaxation does NOT independently alter the linear dipole curvature. Its
first mediated change is the specified nonpositive quartic contribution.
For several stable even scalar coordinates, replace K'^2/K_eta by
(grad K)^T U_hessian^(-1)(grad K). If symmetry is broken, a permitted mixed
Hessian c instead gives the usual curvature Schur correction -c^2/K_eta;
that breaking and its state must be supplied, not selected after a sign test.

A useful elementary check is the clamped, frozen-state B expression
U=C+tau*exp(-eta)+E_b*exp(3eta). For tau>0 its free stationary point satisfies
tau*exp(-eta)=3E_b*exp(3eta) and U''=12E_b*exp(3eta)>0.
Its TOTAL mechanical pressure is zero there, even though the isolated
baseline has negative pressure. For tau<=0 it has no finite stationary
point of this form. This is a frozen-state example only: relaxed state
susceptibility, controller/support energy and actual formation cannot be
deleted when applying it to a real candidate. A homogeneous universe is
not obtained by setting every local pressure to zero through a free-piston
minimization.

## 5. One finite two-environment discriminator when the premises are ready

Prepare two densities using one independently specified material loading /
volume and formation rule, with the same calibrated sources and boundary
inventory. Ordinary mass divided by calibrated volume may label these
environments; archive count is not silently substituted for either factor.
Retain every population and supplied-work difference. Locally transport
each resulting branch at fixed I as required for (1), rather than resetting
its state independently at each source point.

For EACH environment, the small predeclared test is:

1. At eta0 and eta0+/-d, apply alpha=0,+/-e and measure D, B and complete
   controller work independently. Keep the weak-positive-lapse/source domain.
2. Define K_e(eta)=[D(eta,+e)-D(eta,-e)]/(2e). Compare
   [K_e(eta0+d)-K_e(eta0-d)]/(2d) with the NEGATIVE of
   [B(eta0,+e)+B(eta0,-e)-2B(eta0,0)]/e^2. These estimate the opposite
   sides of (4) after moving its minus sign to the right-hand comparison,
   with respective O(d^2+e^2) and O(e^2) truncation for a smooth branch.
3. Run the source rectangle (eta0,0)->(eta0+d,0)->(eta0+d,e)
   ->(eta0,e)->(eta0,0), and its reverse, with the SAME retained state branch.
   Record the work in (5), every support/supply term and final-state change.
4. If a free scalar-volume closure is proposed, independently determine its
   stable K_eta and compare its displacement and quartic work correction to
   (8). Do not infer K_eta from the correction it is intended to predict.

Before any run fix e,d from the calibrated source window, not the desired
outcome. Use only the predeclared e,e/2,e/4 and d,d/2,d/4 derivative controls,
not an open-ended search for a passing step. Readout, Taylor and adiabatic
errors must have independently owned bounds; finite-difference agreement
alone is not such a bound. No numerical tolerance or duration is invented
here before the source, spectral window and measurement scale are known.
An unbounded or nonconvergent error is an unqualified test, not permission
to loosen the model criterion.

For truly nonstationary states there need not be a scalar W, reciprocal
static derivatives or zero reversible-loop work. The exact two-time response,
source-dependent formation, memory, work and final-state energy must then
replace (1)--(8). Dissipated/stored work is not automatically a violation of
the complete microscopic energy ledger. The current original blank states
remain in that nonstationary category unless separately justified otherwise.

## 6. Minimal independent datum and actual next decision

Mixed response tests do not determine the source-independent volume term:
W->W+c(eta) leaves D, K_alpha and (4) unchanged while shifting pressure and
scalar-volume stiffness. A genuinely dynamical volume experiment can see
that change, but cannot call it an irrelevant phase. In the already restricted
metric-only zero/two-derivative endpoint, the remaining vacuum ambiguity is
one absolute matched constant; one independently calibrated absolute volume
work/contact datum suffices after the other terms are owned. For an arbitrary
undeclared c(eta), one number is not enough. B fixes a functional assignment
only by its explicit postulate; its SI normalization and physical matching
still require independent calibration. A galaxy dipole fit cannot supply it.

Consequently no single added density number closes both lanes. The minimal
new physical object is one owned source/volume/clock and formation reduction
that can predict the joint work derivatives; its independent absolute-volume
datum is a separate calibration unless actually derived. Equations (4), (7)
and (8) are a finite falsifiable bridge for that object. Equation (9) gives a
constructive candidate class, while (10)--(11) specify what must be earned
before claiming its MOND-like window. This narrows the next source/state
derivation; it does not authorize another finite-cell age or parameter sweep.
