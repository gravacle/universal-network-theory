## The restriction is wider than a particular interpolation function

For a flat homogeneous action with integrand R3+Kij Kij+U(K), constant
M^-2=8pi G_N and minimally coupled conserved ordinary matter, put
f(K)=K^2/3+U(K), K=3H. Vary the lapse BEFORE fixing proper time:

    rho = (M^2/2) [f-K f_K],
    lambda_K = -U_KK/2,
    (lambda_K-1/3)(q+1) = sum_i (1+w_i) Omega_i.

This follows by differentiating the constraint, using Kdot=-3H^2(q+1)
and ordinary energy conservation. It requires no special F, odd continuation
or value of the force constants. Retuning U or adding its constant does not
break this background/Hessian identity.

IMPORTANT: that identity alone is NOT a universal scalar-instability theorem.
One must count the actual constraints and demonstrate a propagating scalar.
Removing lapse-gradient operators may change that count; the nonprojectable
lambda-R literature provides a pertinent warning. The saved UNT effective
action does have its positive lapse-gradient sector and explicitly assumed
short-wavelength window, so its earlier constrained sign screen still applies.
See [Bellorin and Restuccia](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.0055).

## An independent clock state must do more than rename the old charge

For homogeneous f(K,Q), where Q is an independent physical clock rate,

    rho = (M^2/2) [f-K f_K-Q f_Q],    a^3 f_Q = I.

The integration constant I is a state freedom unless its formation is
predicted. This is not new merely because the state is called a network clock:
the existing K(X)+J+compression branch already contains an adopted charge
excess and vacuum normalization. Its shared-scale audit showed that changing
the galaxy a0 leaves its homogeneous/linear sector unchanged. Preserve that
identifiability result and its unresolved galaxy-forming nonlinear state;
existing qualified short-time nonlinear source results remain preserved.

A zero-current branch is a distinct possibility. If f_Q(K,Q*)=0 and f_QQ is
nonzero, then

    Q*' = -f_KQ/f_QQ,
    f_eff(K) = f(K,Q*(K)),
    f_eff'' = f_KK-f_KQ^2/f_QQ,
    lambda_red = 1/3-f_eff''/2.

The reduced background satisfies the SAME trace identity. Thus merely
eliminating Q algebraically and choosing a favorable f_eff is not an escape
in a regime where that local reduction and the scalar constraint argument
are valid. Retained finite-frequency and spatial-current dynamics can change
the question, but must be specified and tested, not dropped when inconvenient.

The late-time relation Q-Q* approximately I/(a^3 f_QQ) is only a conditional
attractor. It neither fixes today's finite dust-like normalization nor proves
the background/perturbations have forgotten their initial state. In existing
K(X), setting I=0 removes the adopted charge-dust excess; compression is not
a source of that missing charge.

Nor does a constant derivative coupling to conserved baryon current create it:
integral sqrt(-g) d_mu(phi) J_b^mu is a boundary term when div J_b=0. A
canonical-charge offset is not energy or physical charge production. For

    ndot+3H n = S_form,
    rhodot+3H(rho+P) = Q S_form.

The donor or boundary work must supply that energy. Fitting the final source

## Ordinary clock mixing is not an invisible repair

The familiar EFT terms -(m3^3/2) delta g00 delta K and
(M2^4/2)(delta g00)^2 leave the selected FLRW background unchanged at first
variation and retain its tensor propagation speed. But restoring the clock
fluctuation pi yields quasistatic metric-clock mixing and generally changes
the inferred Newton response. Their coefficients and the reference clock
history are additional functions, not consequences of the old galaxy fit.
See [Gubitosi, Piazza and Vernizzi, sections 3.3--3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/1210.0201v2).

There is a sharper action-specific check. With n=delta N, b=Delta beta and
gamma n(3 zetadot-b)+M_n n^2 added to the old principal scalar action, shift
elimination leaves

    C_zeta = 2(3lambda-1)/(lambda-1),
    L contains C_zeta zetadot^2 + [2gamma/(1-lambda)] n zetadot
                 + [2F' k_phys^2+O(1)] n^2.

For finite coefficients, lambda!=1 and F'>0, lapse elimination produces
corrections suppressed by k_phys^-2, including braiding and background-mixing
terms. The inherited negative high-k limit
survives in the same admitted effective-theory window. This is OUR inference
for the detuned-trace action, not a theorem excluding all scalar-tensor
braiding. A different constraint structure needs its own full variation.

Similarly, an operator's zero value at K=0 does not prove static invisibility:
B(Q)K and a_i D^i K generally have nonzero static first variations. A genuine
replacement must check the equations for the galaxy, not just its background
Lagrangian. A clock scalar P(X)-G3(X) box(phi) is a known different starting
point, but supplies unspecified functions and state data; it is not a derived
UNT completion. [Deffayet, Pujolas, Sawicki and Vikman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.0048).
