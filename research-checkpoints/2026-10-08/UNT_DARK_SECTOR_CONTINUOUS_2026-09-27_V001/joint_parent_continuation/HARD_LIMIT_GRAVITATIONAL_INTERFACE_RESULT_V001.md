# Stored network-mode energy now has a conditional gravitational interface

2026-09-29. Completed analytical derivation and independent reviews. This is
a result of a declared continuum parent and state, not a native-network
derivation or an observational validation.

The key gain is stronger than an averaged equation of state. For the new
balanced hard-mode sector, we retained the preferred clock, phase, lapse,
shift, soft memory and hard static contact and derived what sources gravity.
The sum of stored excitation and signed polarization obeys exactly the
linear scalar stress/conservation equations of pressureless matter. This
continues to hold when ordinary radiation, baryon pressure and anisotropic
stress are included. The matched vacuum remains the same constant.

Thus this candidate can give network-mode excitation the gravitational role
usually assigned to cold matter; it does not merely store energy without
explaining its metric response. The density normalization remains the one
supplied by the shared state/calibration. There is no extra fitted abundance
or replacement G/mu0 in this derivation.

The cancellation is physical bookkeeping, not deletion. If V is the total
velocity potential and pi the preferred-clock perturbation, the conserved
hard current follows pi while its stress momentum follows V. The signed
polarization density supplies precisely the difference in their continuity
equations. The total obeys delta_dot=3Psi_dot-(k/a)^2 V and V_dot=Phi, with
zero linear pressure and anisotropic stress. Ordinary species retain their
own pressure and anisotropy; Phi=Psi is not imposed when they forbid it.

## The additional mode is part of the result

There is also an independent relative clock/polarization sector. Its reduced
kinetic matrix is positive in the stated finite band, but its potential has
a negative direction. The slow-response matter-era solution contains a
compensated growing physical tilt while its total metric perturbation is zero.

The exact finite-band temporal equations have no wave-number-dependent
growth rate after a field rescaling, within their fixed-wave-number limiting
domain. That is useful, but not a UV completion or full hyperbolicity proof.
For the regular slow branch in a hard-matter-plus-vacuum background, physical

Ordinary matter changes the exponent; arbitrary finite-band initial transients
are NOT bounded by that slow-branch estimate. The next numerical calculation
therefore tests actual finite-time internal evolution rather than substituting
this approximation for it.

## Which assumptions carry this result

- The covariant mechanical parent, physical metric coupling and matched
  vacuum are explicitly adopted; their derivation from native BS mechanics
  remains open.
- The hard gap is taken large at finite owned energy, with controlled phase
  averaging and fixed physical wave number. Finite-gap and fast-geometry
  errors are not silently zero in the real theory.
- A newly counted two-copy, balanced state removes free coherent forcing.
  It needs separate preparation/initial data. The old common pulse is not
  retroactively converted into it.
- Soft background excitation is zero in this limiting branch. Soft
  perturbations and their finite-time memory remain in the equations.
- Ordinary photon/recombination/scattering physics would be imported
  explicitly for an acoustic calculation. This does not derive a continuous
  network-origin CMB or prove that the old radiation history is unnecessary.

If background densities, ordinary microphysics and perturbation initial data
are chosen identical to a pressureless reference cosmology, the ideal-limit
linear metric/ordinary equations coincide. That is a conditional equivalence,
not an independently predicted CMB spectrum, measured preference or novelty
claim for the general polarization idea. Distinguishing predictions require
the finite corrections, prepared internal mode and nonlinear behavior.
