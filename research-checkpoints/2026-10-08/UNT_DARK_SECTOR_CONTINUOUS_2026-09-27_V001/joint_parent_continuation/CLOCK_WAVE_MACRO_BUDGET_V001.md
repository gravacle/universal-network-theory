# Can source-generated clock waves close the cosmic budget?

2026-10-05 UTC. Analytical action-level test, independently checked by an
internal reviewer. This is not a numerical cosmological fit or an exclusion
of every nonadiabatic, coherent, or environment-dependent network state.

## Conserved wave action and stress

For a stable positive-energy mode with p=k/a and action

    S2=(1/2) integral dt N a^3 Z(p)
          [|q_dot/N|^2-omega(p)^2 |q|^2],

the adiabatic invariant is I_k=E_k/omega_k. Averaged lapse/scale variation gives

    rho_wave=a^(-3) integral I_k omega(p),
    P_wave=(a^(-3)/3) integral I_k p partial_p omega(p).

The p-dependent positive normalization Z cancels from leading averaged
stress by kinetic/potential equipartition; it does not secretly add dust.
No temperature, thermal equilibrium, or Planck occupation is assumed.
Positive mixtures lie between their constituent w values.

For constant unfiltered trace, the flat vacuum symbol is

    omega=c sqrt[beta/(2+3beta)] ell p^2 sqrt(2+ell^2 p^2),
    w_mode=(1/3)[2+q^2/(2+q^2)], q=ell p.

Thus 2/3<=w_mode<1; freely redshifting populations dilute between a^(-5)
and a^(-6). For the common-filter flat symbol, y=1+q^2 gives

    w_mode=(1/3)[1+2y(2+3beta)/((y+1)(2y^2+3beta))].

For 0<beta<=2 this lies between 1/3 and 2/3, corresponding to dilution
between a^(-4) and a^(-5). These are neither dust nor negative pressure.

## Essential applicability restrictions

Use these flat symbols in cosmology only where the ACTUAL constrained
FLRW eigenmode is stable, has positive energy, controlled species mixing
and uniformly small background corrections. Merely omega>>H is insufficient.
ORDINARY_MATTER_CLOCK_ACTION_V001 explicitly finds a common-filter matter
instability despite its healthy flat symbol; that unstable domain is NOT a
healthy wave gas. Background-dependent omega or Z can require additional
metric-variation terms not included in this simple action.

Modes with omega comparable to H, condensates, coherent forced near-fields,
source binding and nonadiabatic production are outside this result. They
are possible separate hypotheses, not successes furnished by this test.

## Production is a work obligation, not a free abundance

For rho_wave_dot+3H(rho_wave+P_wave)=Q, sustaining dust-like dilution
requires Q=3H w_wave rho_wave. Sustaining constant density requires
Q=3H(1+w_wave)rho_wave. The donor stress, source work and depletion must be
retained in total conservation. A source-dependent population cannot be
assigned an independent favorable abundance without counting that freedom.

In particular, the donor obeys rho_o_dot+3H(rho_o+P_o)=-Q, so Q cancels
from the total continuity equation. At constant positive F_beta,
apart from a separately retained vacuum term,

    a_ddot/a=-(rho_o+3P_o+rho_wave+3P_wave)/(6 M^2 F_beta).

Nonnegative ordinary/wave densities and pressures cannot accelerate this
background. An effective creation pressure assigned to the waves while
omitting the donor's depletion would double-count the proposed benefit.

Conclusion: a passive, stable adiabatic wave population in the stated
approximation does not close the missing homogeneous matter or accelerating
budget. A genuinely different clock/geometry observable map or explicitly
conserved nonadiabatic state is needed before another expansion-data sweep.
