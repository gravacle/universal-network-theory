## Decision

**Transparent shared calibration is appropriate now. A full native derivation
of every constant, a complete CMB likelihood and proof beyond doubt are not
prerequisites for a provisional favorable assessment of a conditional model.**
A coherent quantitative account of known phenomena with less genuinely
independent freedom can earn such an assessment. An unused risky prediction
would strengthen it, but is not its sole possible basis.

The immediate obstruction is more specific: the state supplying the new
cosmological matter-like energy is not the state used to compute the existing
baryon-only galaxy fields. Their successful pieces cannot yet be counted as
one joint prediction. Closing that macroscopic source/state join is more
decisive than another finite-cell mechanism example or another fit of the
old curve.

## An exact joint-consistency result available without another run

Consider the declared cold alpha=1 branch with zero polarization entropy
and ordinary baryons during a pressureless, separately conserved regime.
Both populations follow the same metric. In the conventions of the existing
linear interface, for i=b,x and p=k/a,

    delta_i_dot=3Psi_dot-p^2 V_i,    V_i_dot=Phi.

Thus the gauge-invariant relative perturbations obey

    DeltaV_dot=0,
    (delta_x-delta_b)_dot=-k^2 DeltaV/a^2,
    delta_x-delta_b=Delta_i-k^2 DeltaV_i integral(dt/a^2). (1)

The physical relative peculiar velocity scales as a^-1. Initially equal
density contrasts and velocities remain equal under **any** common metric
forcing. In a matter-era example the relative density consists of constant
and decaying pieces, whereas the common growing mode grows. This does not
prove a particular nonlinear halo profile, but it rules out treating one
component as automatically nonclustering merely because its homogeneous
density is small on a galactic volume. The initial relative/isocurvature
mode is a real additional datum, not an unrecorded suppression switch.

There is also a strict stationary check. An occupied harmonic hard mode has
e(I)=omega0 I. On a static zero-flow branch, the phase equation is
N omega0=mu with constant mu. Therefore N is constant wherever that branch
is occupied. Pressureless material cannot sit at rest in a nonconstant
lapse. Nonzero orbital/streaming motion, finite pressure, exchange or another
state can change this conclusion; none is supplied by the old baryon-only
PDE. Infall, crossing streams and a supported galaxy state need their actual
stress/current closure.

Equations (1) and the static condition reject the **silent splice** of the
ideal growing cosmological excitation onto the old galaxy calculation while
omitting its galactic stress. They do not reject finite-gap alternatives,
all possible network states or UNT. Baryonic pressure/feedback, radiation-era
effects and nonlinear multistreaming are not approximated as dust outside
the specified regime.

## Recommended bounded next study

**One hard-stress-retaining, shared-boundary galaxy calculation**, not a
new galaxy survey or another native toy census. Select one explicit finite-gap
alpha-one effective completion and one supported state prescription, derive
its weak-field stationary equations from that same action, and solve them
for the already retained NGC3198 baryonic sources. The existing static
scalar BVP is a starting reduction, not permission to label a spherical
annulus as an axisymmetric disk or an isotropic vector state.

Use the cosmological calibration to fix the outer hard state and matched
vacuum; allow shared parameters to be inferred, but do not fit an independent
galactic excitation profile. Retain all source variants and all observed
radii (with the old all-radius/R>=2 sensitivity distinguished). The output
must include hard density/current/support and polarization separately,
their sum in the metric constraints, and the resulting rotation and light
potential. Nonexistence of the chosen branch, an excessive hard contribution,
or a materially wrong radial shape is a decisive result for that candidate,
not a reason to omit its energy or change the branch after looking.

This is bounded by one model and one galaxy; no claim about resource size is
inferred from the much simpler completed workers. Before a fit, its chosen
finite-pressure/flow state must actually define the field problem. If that
definition is not available, the immediate deliverable is the explicit
macroscopic closure, not a nominal joint score from spliced equations.

If a viable joint forward map results, calibrate the same shared parameters
to the exposed galaxy/expansion data with their real nuisance freedom, then
compare quantitative coverage and remaining assumptions with the halo
benchmark. DDO154 is already exposed: it may test cross-system consistency
of the new rule but cannot be relabeled unused. A meaningful improvement on
this known-data comparison could already support a **limited provisional**
explanatory advantage; it need not await all of Stage3.
