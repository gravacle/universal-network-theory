## Decision value: HOLD for this objective

There is an additional decisive scope limitation, directly visible without evaluating the proposed test. Every original psi, r, w and b register starts blank and remains exactly blank: its onsite occupation commutes with H_0, the carrier/copy/feedback couplings are zero, and V_p acts only on edges and newly added battery registers. The proposed route therefore forms edge/battery correlations, not the joint original record/activity mechanism. Calling it a completion of that mechanism would be incorrect.

The proposal does make formerly unspecified assumptions explicit and supplies a falsifiable example with an honest resource ledger. That is a limited analytic advance. It does not independently motivate those assumptions, derive the vacuum-like term or physical volume, provide an operational gravitational source, or show why this battery protocol is relevant to the native model sought. A positive finite outcome cannot repair these omissions; rejection would eliminate only this particular, independently unmotivated preparation. The necessary-condition screen is not a general no-go test for native formation.

Accordingly, the shortest useful disposition is to retain this note as a nonadopted illustrative construction and HOLD the proposed N=2 worker. A numerical run would become decision-relevant only if there were an independent reason to select this specific mechanism and a stated scientific decision that its rejection could change. That reason is not supplied here. This is prioritization, not an added numerical threshold or a demand for a new proof campaign. It creates no blocker for the separate already-agreed conditional perturbation diagnostic, and no new native implementation or experiment is requested by this review.

No source correction is required for the proposal's explicitly conditional claims. No model/API/release status, physical qualification, native-gravity/dark-energy conclusion or existing criterion is changed. All eleven program lanes remain OPEN.
