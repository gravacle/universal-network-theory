## Intended result and present boundary

The target is one network dynamics and formation law whose predictions make
independent dark-matter and dark-energy components unnecessary in the tested
domains. It is not a proof that no conceivable dark particle exists. A
successful local numerical check cannot establish this broad result.

The same model need not put every galaxy and the universe in an identical
microstate. It must use one physical source/clock/volume map, one formation
rule, explicit environmental initial/boundary data, and a counted universal
parameter/calibration ledger. Force, energy, pressure, and creation work must
be calculated from the same resulting state. Independently selected halo
populations, radius-dependent preparations, or an independently adjusted
vacuum offset cannot be reported as predictions eliminating those freedoms.
Universal calibration is allowed, but must not be called independent prediction.

The current native record mechanics, conditional nonlinear gravity response,
prepared-action cosmology, and finite two-current spatial tests are distinct
constructions. They have **not** yet been joined into this shared realization.

## Density-at-the-edge hypothesis: a restriction, not a dismissal

Distinguish ordinary activity density, retained-structure density, physical
volume, local acceleration, normalized mode spectrum, and actual prepared
state. Lower visible density may accompany changes in several of these; it
does not by itself specify their dynamical relationship.

There is an exact restriction in the existing conditional radial parent.
For a uniform positive multiplier A, take nu_A=A nu_0, K_b=A K_0 and
B_A=A B_0. Hold the per-mode equations, coefficients, cutoffs, source history,
and per-mode initial coordinates/velocities fixed. The equations are then
A-independent and

    D_A(g,t) = g - h'(g) B_A(t)/K_b = g - h'(g) B_0(t)/K_0.

This cancellation applies also to nonlinear finite-age evolution, positive
gaps and finite quadratures where the solutions exist. Total energy scales
with A; fixing total energy instead changes the stipulated comparison.
Consequently, merely changing the number of identical modes and their
compulsory contact together does not change this normalized local force law.
Changes in normalized spectral shape, cutoffs, inertia, interactions, state,
or physical source coupling are not excluded.

For the qualified alpha=1 relaxed continuum window,

    a0 = 8/(3 pi) sqrt(kappa/u4) Lambda^2,
    d ln a0 = (1/2) d ln kappa - (1/2) d ln u4 + 2 d ln Lambda.

This conditional scale is independent of the amplitude A, not a native
derivation of the observed acceleration scale. Inertia changes finite-age
response without changing this relaxed expression.

A different construction fixes the contact and rescales only polarization
by a source-independent fraction f. It gives D_f=(1-f)g+f D_1 exactly. If
D_1=o(g) on the qualified relaxed/held-mean branch and 0<=f<1, its conditional
isolated spherical algebraic interface has a Keplerian far limit. This is
**not** an all-age theorem: at finite age the linear coefficient is
1-f+f mu_age, which conservative ringing may make positive, zero or negative.
A finite lower gap also limits any flat-curve continuum window at f=1.

Neither argument licenses substituting A(r) or f(g,rho) after variation.
For example, varying an energy integral A(x) W_0(grad Phi) gives
div[A F_0], including grad A dot F_0. Dynamical density and source-dependent
coefficients require their full variations and energy/stress terms. The
complete action must decide whether any such terms cancel.

This algebra was independently checked by a separate reviewer before this
checkpoint. It narrows the experiment to changes of interactions, normalized
response or actual state; it does not refute a density-dependent network law.

## Why physical coupling and formation are the immediate priorities

Adding c g^2 I/2 + alpha(V-V0) I to a Hamiltonian leaves normalized prescribed-
background native histories unchanged while changing source contact and
volume work. A coherent source/controller can distinguish supplied choices;
decoding their effects does not derive which coupling nature uses. Under the
adopted metric-only two-derivative endpoint the vacuum freedom includes a
constant C0; this is not permission to fit an arbitrary expansion function.

The existing clock-polarization equations also admit an unexcited geodesic
branch that ordinary common-source evolution does not automatically leave.
The relaxed galaxy state, returned-action cosmology and antisymmetric hard
preparations cannot be equated by naming them all records. The needed state
must form under a stated rule with its preparation energy retained.

The next new bounded test is an autonomous finite formation/volume controller
for the already declared alternatives A and B. Its protocol must be fixed
before execution, compare both compressed and expanded initial environments,
retain all controller and native energy, and test differing recoil and record-
formation predictions. The physical-volume assignment and controller ratios
are new apparatus premises. An internal pass would validate those finite
conditional dynamics, not identify volume with spacetime or select gravity.
No outcome of that unexecuted experiment is asserted in this snapshot.
