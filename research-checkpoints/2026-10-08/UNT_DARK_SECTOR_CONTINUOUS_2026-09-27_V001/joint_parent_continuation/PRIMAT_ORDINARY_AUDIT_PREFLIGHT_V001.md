only formats a path. Valid table arithmetic is unchanged; finite monotone-grid
checks retain signed thermal corrections. Physical weak-rate accessors retain
the original library's normalization/clamp convention, not a new rate law.

The adapter's direct initialization order and units agree with the pinned APIs:
T background in MeV, weak accessor temperature in K, time/rates in seconds,
rhoB in g/cm³, and the published atomic versus nucleon helium distinction.
Mass-number history checks use the actual species order. All history and
background rows remain, including repeated era endpoints and raw signed
abundances. Above10MeV rate nulls are expressly distinguished from nonfinite
output failure. Direct in-memory assembly avoids the stock output driver;
disabled write flags and required fixed paths are consistent with that scope.

For the fixed two-state HT generator, the difference obeys
deltaYn' = -(Gamma_np+Gamma_pn)deltaYn. Consequently the sign, log-time Jacobian
t, nested Simpson weights and reported log contraction are correct. This is an
exact identity with an approximately integrated coefficient, not an error bound
on later nonlinear nuclear evolution or a native thermalization result.

background, NEVO or cache-systematic convergence. The baseline intentionally
retains upstream's baryon-rest omission in its H density sum and fixed standard
