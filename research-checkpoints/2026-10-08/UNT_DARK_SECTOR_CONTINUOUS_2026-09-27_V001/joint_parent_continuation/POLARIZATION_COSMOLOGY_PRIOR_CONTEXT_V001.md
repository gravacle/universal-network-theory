# Prior context for the conditional polarization/cosmology parent

2026-09-29. Primary-source check, not an import of their empirical success or
an alteration of the adopted action. These are distinct models.

[Blanchet and Le Tiec,2008](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.3518) already construct
a relativistic polarizable medium that reproduces pressureless matter plus a
cosmological constant at linear cosmological order. Their galactic MOND result
also invokes weak clustering. Thus the GENERAL coexistence of polarization,
MOND-like response and matter-like cosmological stress is not a new UNT claim.
The present finite-mode mechanism, preparation and native derivation must be
established on their own; that paper's results do not validate them.

[Blanchet and Skordis,2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.00912v1) summarize a
khronon-tensor construction with separate functions J(Y) and K(Q). Their
K(Q) supplies the cosmological sector; a simple quadratic choice has an
early-history/galaxy tension, while a different proposed function improves
that model's behavior. This reinforces the need to derive the whole time
response rather than infer it from a static galaxy law. It does not justify
adding an arbitrary K to our action or borrowing their CMB agreement.

The present conditional construction explicitly keeps positive-inertia
response modes and their paid excitation, together with the matched endpoint
constant. Its preferred-foliation interface was already attributed to
[Blanchet and Marsat,2011](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.5264) in
EXPLICIT_MODE_COSMOLOGY_PATHWAY.md. It additionally retains a separate phase
and a normalized clock in the proposed high-gap average. Its extra internal
perturbations and their initial data require direct calculation. No assertion
of equivalence to either prior cosmology or of a new native continuum theorem
follows from the shared vocabulary.
