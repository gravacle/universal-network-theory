One derivation keeps the record dynamics exactly along a single hop.

    Im <B...B| exp(i*D_a*x)*exp(-i*D_b*u)*exp(-i*D_a*s)|B...B>

## The full incidence background closes the leading clock contrast

For this SAME uniform fixed-Q mixture on the complete potential K_(N,N)

    A_remove=Delta+2*U_d*(2*d-1-2*d_*).

removed configuration. Initially all records are blank, so no occupied
record-feedback energy enters these particular coefficients. Writers and

The copying-off native kinetic expectation is

    <K>_0(x)=B_N*x^4+O_N(x^6),
    B_N=-c*h_N^2*t^2/6 * [(N^2-N*d)*(A_add+j)
                                        -N*d*(A_remove+j)]. (11)

One can prove the cancellation of other-edge terms without assuming that
conditional carrier Hamiltonians commute. For a single flipped incidence
bit, call those blocks A,B and put D_op=B-A. The flip off-diagonal is
v*I, v=-h_N; the initial carrier density is I/D_Q in the A block. Its
diagonal second derivatives are -2*v^2*I/D_Q and +2*v^2*I/D_Q, and its
diagonal fourth derivatives are respectively
+2*v^2*(D_op^2+4*v^2*I)/D_Q and its negative. Therefore the kinetic
coefficient from this bit is

    v^2*Tr[(K_A-K_B)*(D_op^2+4*v^2*I)]/(12*D_Q).

Here sigma=1-2*n_e(G), D_op=sigma*(A_g*I+j*C_e-t*T_e),
K_A-K_B=sigma*t*T_e, and A_g is A_add or A_remove as appropriate.
