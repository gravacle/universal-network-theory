## 1. Stationarity must first come from the phase and clock equations

For the scalar/radial action sector, the reviewed averaged parent is

    S_mode=C integral sqrt(-g) {sum_j w_j[I_j u.partial theta_j
                         -e_j(I_j,h(a))]-DeltaU}.

Here the preferred u is the normalized gradient of the clock T. It is not an
independently varied ordinary-fluid velocity. Variation gives

    nabla_mu(I_j u^mu)=0,       u.partial theta_j=omega_j=e_j,I.

In a static spherical patch with aligned clock, shift zero and lapse N(r),

    theta_j=N(r) omega_j(I_j,h) t+theta_j0(r).

The averaged radial momentum is proportional to sum w_j I_j theta'_j. Thus
zero initial radial momentum and no secular momentum generation require

    sum_j w_j I_j (N omega_j)'=0.                         (1)

A sufficient boundary-determined branch is

    N(r) omega_j(I_j(r),h(r))=mu_j,
    theta_j=mu_j t+constant_j,
    mu_j=N_b omega_j(I_b,j,h_b).                         (2)

These mu_j are inherited boundary-state quantities, not per-radius halo
parameters. On this branch the relative phase gradients D_i theta_j vanish,
so the density-phase contribution to the preferred-clock energy flux vanishes.
This is a substantive state/phase choice. Random local phase averaging does
not alone enforce (1) or (2). An actual flow or compensating matter/geometry
current would require its own retained momentum and work equations.

At zero source define per-mode Pi=I omega-e. Equations (2) and de=omega dI
give exactly

    Pi'=-(e+Pi) N'/N.                                   (3)

Thus ordinary hydrostatic balance is DERIVED for the intrinsic zero-source
stress on this branch, rather than inferred from w approximately zero. At
nonzero source the identity is instead

    Pi'=-(e+Pi) N'/N+b <y> h_r,       b=kappa delta.      (4)

Moreover the full static stress is not just e,Pi. In c=1 units, with
E=sum w e, Pi=sum w(I omega-e), B=sum w b<y>, F=B h'(a),

    rho_mode=C[E+DeltaU+aF+div F],
    p_r=C[Pi-DeltaU-aF],       p_t=C[Pi-DeltaU],
    a=N'/(N L),       div F=(r^2 F)'/(L r^2).             (5)

Equations (4)-(5) obey the static radial conservation equation exactly. The
same stress determines the mass/lapse and lensing equations; a separate halo
mass or light-coupling profile is not added.

There is a useful exact limiting obstruction. If e(I,0)=omega_H I is strictly
harmonic, omega_H is independent of I. Equation (2) then forces N constant
