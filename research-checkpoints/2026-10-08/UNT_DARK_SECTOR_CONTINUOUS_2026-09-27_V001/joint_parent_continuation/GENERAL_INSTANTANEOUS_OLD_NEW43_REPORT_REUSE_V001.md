# HELD old/new43 general instantaneous reporting reuse

The existing all-43 formatter is reused. V003 changes only its allowed scope literal to include `general_instantaneous_radial_balance_diagnostic`; residual, percentage, quoted-error, missing-row, summary and Markdown formulas are unchanged. No data or reporting function was evaluated during preparation.

The HELD recipe pins genuine unchanged baseline c389 and its producer790/custody3c9, and future changed receiver83fa. Root must bind and independently authenticate the actual changed source, naturally closed RESULT/RECEIPT/full-custody proof and changed-field lineage before calling the formatter. Every future terminal binding remains None; this recipe is not an executable release or a custody certificate.

Use the producer's declared orientation=1 and its unique `selected_index`, verified to name a timelike DECLARED_ORIENTATION candidate. Branch index is not chosen from observed agreement. A missing, ambiguous or refused selection yields None and its row remains among all43. Do not score an incomplete-row subset.

All catalogue radii, observed velocities and absolute quoted errors are copied unchanged from authenticated c389 rows, tied to the original baseline catalogue and the changed receiver's same direct-reference event/baseline lineage. The old speed is c389's selected general baseline speed, not its earlier Q1 scalar comparator. Retain exact producer790 and changed rational endpoints, all candidates/refusals and every source qualification alongside presentation midpoints; no future number is fabricated.

Percent residual is 100*(speed−Vobs)/Vobs, undefined at Vobs=0. Quoted-error units are (speed−Vobs)/absolute quoted error. The formatter's legacy `sigma` and `chi_square` fields are descriptive quoted-error units and their squared sum: no Gaussian, covariance, model-uncertainty or degrees-of-freedom inference. The point-display ±error comparison is distinct from exact interval/error relations.

The report compares unchanged versus changed instantaneous radial-balance diagnostics at the documented left radial/upper sheet events. Preserve time/phi, vertical and angular-drift diagnostics and all nine False qualification flags. It does not establish a full circle, sheet tracer motion, stability/history, accepted finite constraints, installed rounding accuracy or a qualified galaxy prediction. Original circle132 and producer/operator actions are not called.
