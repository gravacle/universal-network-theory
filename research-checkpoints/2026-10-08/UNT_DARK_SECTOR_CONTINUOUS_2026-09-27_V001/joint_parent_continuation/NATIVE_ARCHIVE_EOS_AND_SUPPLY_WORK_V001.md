# Archive growth is an energy transfer, not automatically dark pressure

2026-09-30. Analytical extension of the exact archive-conditioned finite-prefix
candidate. No cosmological promotion, new volume law, fitted creation rate,
numerical run or selected state is introduced. The result is a sharp test of
what the EXISTING A/B archive energy and source assignments would mean under
an explicitly conditional FLRW-like calibration.

## 1. Mechanical pressure from the same owned energy

At a fixed archive count m=(m_L,m_R), define M=m_L+m_R, D=m_R-m_L.
At a calibrated native Q branch with linear scale s>0, the added archive
energy and physical energy dipole are already fixed:

    E_A=nu*M/s,       Z_A,arch=gamma0*nu*D,
    E_B=nu*M,         Z_B,arch=gamma0*s*nu*D,                 (1)

where nu>=0 is held fixed during the derivative and gamma0=ell0/(2*c_phys^2).
The external length strain eta sends s to exp(eta)*s and the calibrated
branch volume to V=V0*s^3. Pressure is the mechanical source readout

    B_eta=-partial_eta E at fixed count and native state,
    p=B_eta/(3V),    rho=E/V.                               (2)

Here rho is ENERGY density, so p and rho have the same units. Equations
(1)--(2), not a separately chosen equation of state, give exactly

    A: rho_A=nu*M/(V0*s^4),  p_A=rho_A/3,
    B: rho_B=nu*M/(V0*s^3),  p_B=0.                         (3)

The result concerns the added archive gap term, not every term in H_A/B.
The native/controller/interaction terms retain their own source derivatives.
In particular, no independently chosen constant vacuum energy is included
or inferred from (3).

The existing B parent already assigns its degree baseline the separate term
E_b*s^3, with E_b=2 in the frozen dimensionless benchmark. Under this same
volume calibration that term alone has rho=E_b/V0 and p=-rho. This is the
PRE-EXISTING B volume-assignment premise, not energy generated by archive
growth and not an independently derived physical vacuum normalization.
Keeping its provenance separate is essential when assessing a shared model.

For quantum Q, (2)--(3) apply per calibrated branch volume. If weights
w_(Q,m)=Tr[Pi_Q sigma_m] are used to construct macroscopic readouts, the
averaging and volume convention must be declared. A common positive linear
average of the branch rho and p preserves p_A=rho_A/3 and p_B=0; so does a
consistently volume-weighted total. Mixing <E>/<V> with a separately weighted
<p> does not. No replacement of quantum Q by a single evolving cosmic scale
factor has been derived here.

The same energy fixes the dipole, because ell=ell0*s and

    Z_arch=ell*(E_R-E_L)/(2*c_phys^2),
    E_R-E_L=nu*a_X*D,   a_A=s^-1, a_B=1.                   (4)

Thus a second halo or vacuum normalization cannot be inserted without
changing this candidate. D=0 makes this particular first dipole vanish even
when M>0 and the archive energy is positive; it does not make uniform-lapse
energy, pressure, fluctuations or every gravitational response vanish.

## 2. The finite-prefix identity prevents counting supplied energy twice

The preceding exact construction keeps unnormalized native blocks sigma_m.
At a copy-off boundary its inclusive energy is

    E_total,j=E_native,j+E_archive,j
       =sum_m Tr[sigma_m*(H_X+a_X*nu*M)].                    (5)

For one square copy pulse with independent fresh blanks, no varying external
sources and the declared constant pulse Hamiltonian, W_on=0 and

    Delta E_native+Delta E_archive=W_port,
    W_port=-lambda*<a_X*sum_v q_v X_bv>_exit.               (6)

For a smooth pulse from f=0 back to f=0, the right side is
integral dt lambda*dot(f)*<a_X*sum_v q_v X_bv>. The full source family gives
any additional strain/lapse work through <partial_t H>. Native H_Q and H_C
are already part of E_native and are not charged again as an external work
device. If the copy switching is made autonomous, its work device must be
included: its energy supplies the corresponding W_port with the opposite
sign, together with any separately specified supply/support terms.

Equation (6) is not Delta E_archive=W_port in general. Native energy can
also change. Nor does an initially zero assigned gap energy prove that pure
blank preparation, supporting the endpoints or producing the pulse schedule
has zero physical cost. All such missing apparatus remains explicit input.

In A, even between pulses, the operator a_X(Q) changes under H_Q. Its exact
archive energy current contains

    d<E_archive>/dt=(i/hbar)<[H_X,a_X*nu*M]>

at fixed external sources, since the old occupations are conserved. This is
internal energy exchange with the retained native controller, not evidence
that a changing mean Q already obeys a FLRW expansion equation. During a
copy pulse, the newly active fragment occupations and interaction work must
also be included. Equation (6) is the inclusive boundary balance.

## 3. Conditional expanding-cell bookkeeping

Only for this section, ASSUME an independently justified coarse-grained
proper-time and homogeneous comoving-cell mapping: a positive smooth linear
scale b(t), V(t)=V0*b(t)^3, H=dot(b)/b, and a smoothed count M(t) from a finite
prefix or an explicitly supplied extension of it. Identify the strain in
(2) with this calibrated geometric change, not with record count. Hold nu
and all dimensional calibrations fixed. These are additional mapping
premises, not consequences of the native finite controller.

Write kappa=1 for A and kappa=0 for B. Then

    E_archive=nu*M*b^(-kappa),
    rho_archive=nu*M/(V0*b^(3+kappa)),
    p_archive=(kappa/3)*rho_archive.

Direct differentiation gives

    dot(E_archive)+p_archive*dot(V)=nu*b^(-kappa)*dot(M),
    dot(rho_archive)+3H*(rho_archive+p_archive)=Q_archive,
    Q_archive=nu*b^(-kappa)*dot(M)/V.                       (7)

Equation (7) is an energy-transfer/source term. If M is a discrete prefix
count, use the boundary balance (6) instead; a differential creation rate
requires its own coarse-graining justification. If nu or the clock/length
calibration is varied, their explicit work terms must be added, not hidden
inside dot(M). For example, varying nu adds M*b^(-kappa)*dot(nu)/V to (7).

The source Q_archive is supplied by the other retained/native/controller
and supply sectors. It is not a source of total energy from nothing. In a
closed, consistently coarse-grained two-part ledger, with interaction stress
assigned once and no external agent,

    dot(rho_rest)+3H*(rho_rest+p_rest)=-Q_archive.           (8)

Adding (7) and (8) gives the total conservation equation. With an external
work source, its power and stress must instead appear explicitly. A universe
model cannot leave the external experimental switch operator outside its
total stress tensor and still claim an absolute closed explanation.

The exact pulse ledger (6) is the necessary microscopic consistency check
for this interpretation: energy given to archive growth comes from changes
in native energy and from the work device. Q_archive is not an extra copy of
W_port and must not be charged twice. Fixed-gap count growth and geometric
redshifting are distinct terms.

## 4. Why an effective negative pressure is not yet a physical one

For H!=0 one can move the right side of (7) to the left and DEFINE

    p_effective=p_archive-Q_archive/(3H),
    w_effective=kappa/3-dot(M)/(3H*M)                      (9)

when M>0. This is a convenient one-component description of a subsystem
with energy transfer. It is not the mechanical derivative (2). At H=0 or
M=0 the displayed divisions are unavailable; (7) remains the definitionally
safe equation. No singular effective pressure should be inferred there.

For an expanding calibrated cell and constant archive density, (7) would
require

    A: dot(M)=4H*M,  Q_archive=4H*rho_archive,
    B: dot(M)=3H*M,  Q_archive=3H*rho_archive.                (10)

Both yield w_effective=-1 in (9), while their mechanical pressures remain
rho/3 and zero. These rates have not been derived. Choosing them just to
keep rho constant would supply the desired dark-energy history by hand.
Even M proportional to volume is an added formation law, not the identity
V=M; for A it still gives w_effective=-2/3, not a pressure-source derivation.

Energy transfer alone does not change which COMPLETE stress tensor sources
an adopted gravitational field equation. In an Einstein-FLRW comparison
with energy-density units and no separate inserted cosmological constant,

    double_dot(b)/b=-4*pi*G*(rho_total+3*p_total)/(3*c_phys^2).

The added archive contribution to rho+3p is 2*rho_A for A or rho_B for B,
both nonnegative. To accelerate in that comparison, the remaining complete
native/controller/supply/interaction stress must satisfy

    rho_rest+3*p_rest < -(rho_archive+3*p_archive).          (11)

If other positive components are listed separately, include them on the
right too. Omitting the donor sector and inserting p_effective as if it were
the total mechanical pressure is not this same model. A different emergent
gravity equation could change the criterion, but would require its own
derived field/source matching; it is not established by archive growth.

## 5. The shortest remaining physical decision

The useful new conclusion is exact within the adopted energy/volume law:
retained record-like fragment counts can carry cumulative energy and memory,
but their present A/B gap terms are not intrinsically negative-pressure
energy. The complete ledger and same-energy dipole prevent promoting a
chosen count-growth curve into both a dark halo and dark energy for free.

A constructive continuation must supply, from one declared native model:

- The finite inventory or limiting supply, pulse/clock law and formed count
  rate, retaining the original preparation and the energy balance (6).
- The physical source, volume and proper-time calibration, and the stress of
  the work/supply/support terms that could satisfy (11), if acceleration is
  claimed under this gravitational mapping.
- A covariant or otherwise complete large-scale transfer prescription,
  including perturbations of the source and donor, not just an assumed
  homogeneous Q_archive(t). Lensing, growth and early-universe responses
  cannot be inferred from w_effective alone.
- Independent calibration of the shared energy/source parameters. An
  environment-specific count law, an independently tuned vacuum offset or
  two normalizations for (1) and (4) would be a new model choice, not a result
  of this finite-prefix construction.

No finite-prefix numerical sweep is needed to establish (3), (7), (9) or
(11). This note does not select the missing law or declare either branch
cosmologically excluded in every completion. It identifies exactly what
the current archive term cannot supply alone and where a proposed completion
would have to earn its acceleration claim. All physical promotion flags
remain false.
