# A photon reference for the same-action density coefficient

2026-09-29. Prospective dimensional reference, separate from the exact mode
arithmetic. It introduces no fit, state, gap, abundance or acceptance threshold.

For an ordinary blackbody photon field of present temperature T, the measured
present energy density is

    u_gamma=(8*pi^5*k_B^4/(15*h_P^3*c^3))*T^4,
    Omega_gamma=8*pi*G*u_gamma/(3*H0^2*c^2).

Use the same inherited H0 of each existing calibration reference, converted
with its saved Mpc convention. Fixsen's combined temperature determination is
T=2.72548 K with stated uncertainty .00057 K. This is the combined estimate,
not the separately reported WMAP-recalibrated FIRAS value 2.7260±.0013 K.
Primary source: [Fixsen 2009](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.1955).

Use the 2022 CODATA central G=6.67430e-11 m^3 kg^-1 s^-2, uncertainty
.00015e-11, and exact SI k_B=1.380649e-23 J/K, h_P=6.62607015e-34 J s,
c=299792458 m/s. Primary sources:
[NIST CODATA table](https://www.physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/wall_2022.pdf) and
[NIST SI definitions](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-330/sp-330-section-2).
The arithmetic may retain the independent linearized T/G fractional reference
uncertainty sqrt((4*sigma_T/T)^2+(sigma_G/G)^2); this does not propagate the
uncertainties/covariance in the inherited cosmological calibration.

The comparison is R_gamma=Omega_extra_rad,asympt/Omega_gamma, where the numerator
is already fixed by the saved state's Q/E1 and present excitation matching.
Retain every original and explicitly conditional extended-spectrum reference.
At any saved A, also report the finite coefficient E(A)/A^4 and its exact
Q-to-Q+D/A^2 interval, divided by the same E1 and photon reference. No new

This ratio compares density scales. It is not an N_eff likelihood, a
free-streaming-neutrino identification, a CMB acoustic calculation or a claim
that the network modes are photons. Its interpretation as an early-epoch
ratio additionally assumes ordinary photons redshift as A^4 and the stated
network action/volume/clock conditions hold there. A continuous photon-source
hypothesis must supply its own temperature/redshift and energy ledger instead;
it cannot silently borrow that scaling. The adopted late-time references and
standard-BBN baryon alternatives are not independent confirmations of a changed
early expansion law. A large new early density requires their self-consistent
reanalysis, not relabeling a temperature comparison as an exclusion of UNT.
