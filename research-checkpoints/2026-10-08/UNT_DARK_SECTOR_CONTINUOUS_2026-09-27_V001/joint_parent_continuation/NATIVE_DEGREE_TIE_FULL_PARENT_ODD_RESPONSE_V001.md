# Opposite layer energy response in the complete degree tie parent

2026-09-30. Exact source-only continuation of the reviewed full-parent
coupling note. No numerical run, new Hamiltonian term, coefficient scaling
with N, physical-volume assignment or formation-state adoption.

## Result and its limit

The full first-degree-tie parent has a strictly positive static response to
the opposite-layer energy source for every finite N>=2 and U,Omega>0. That
source is identically zero in the matching projection. This establishes an
actual source-visible parent contribution beyond PHP, not just a failure to
bound a discarded remainder.

An exact parity-resolved Feshbach formula locates the contribution in the
second source derivative of the interacting self-energy. Its first source
derivative vanishes inside the invariant matching block. The raw parent
source is affine, so this effective second contact is generated by exact
elimination, not by inserting a new raw source term.

Finite-N positivity supplies neither a uniform lower bound nor a nonzero
response per calibrated physical volume. It also does not lift the projected
anchored static bound: that source belongs to different permutation sectors.
The explicit unresolved quantity is an energy-weighted, layer-odd interacting
resolvent, rather than a radial matching gap alone.

## Parent source and symmetry sectors

Keep the exact held uniform-record, blank-carrier, ports-off family of
`NATIVE_DEGREE_TIE_FULL_PARENT_COUPLING_V001.md`. With h=Omega/N,

    H=C_N+2U(C_L+C_R)-h sum_e X_e,
    C_L=(1/2)sum_L d_v(d_v-1),
    C_R=(1/2)sum_R d_v(d_v-1),
    h>0, U>0, N>=2.                                   (1)

The complete local energies are h_v=c0+U d_v(d_v-1)-(h/2)S_v, with
C_N=2N c0. In the inherited affine lapse chart use u_L=+1 and u_R=-1:

    H(theta)=H+theta T,
    T=sum_L h_v-sum_R h_v=2U(C_L-C_R),
    |theta|<1.                                         (2)

The restriction gives positive node lapses; no physical ruler is inferred
from it. Uniform constants and the two allocations of each flip term cancel
in T. They have not been deleted from H or the other source families.
The parent has H_theta,theta=0 in this explicitly adopted affine chart.

Let G=S_N(left) x S_N(right). A G singlet means invariant under both separate
permutation groups. Layer exchange R is a further involution; it commutes
with H, normalizes G, and sends T to -T. The words even and odd below refer
only to R within the G-singlet space. They do not mean a new G representation.

P projects onto all matchings and Q=1-P. At each matching size m the G action
is transitive, so P has exactly one G singlet there, the uniform matching
vector. Layer exchange preserves that vector. Consequently

    P_s=P restricted to G singlets is entirely even,
    no odd G singlet lies in P,
    TP=PT=0.                                          (3)

The full G-singlet space does have odd vectors. For example, the uniform
orbit sums of a length-two path centered on the left versus on the right
give a nonzero odd difference. Such graphs exist already at N=2.

## Exact finite size positivity

In the graph basis the full H has negative off-diagonal entries -h on every
single-edge flip. That incidence hypercube is connected. The finite parent
therefore has a unique strictly positive ground psi0. Uniqueness and the
permutation and layer symmetries put it in the even G-singlet sector.

T is diagonal, and it is nonzero on the length-two-path graphs just described.
Since psi0 has positive amplitude on every graph,

    <psi0|T|psi0>=0,
    f:=T psi0 is a nonzero odd G singlet,
    W_T=||f||^2>0.                                    (4)

For the PLUS source in (2), define the positive static susceptibility by
delta<T>=-chi_T(0) delta theta. If H_o is H restricted to odd G singlets,

    chi_T(0)=2 <f|(H_o-E0)^(-1)|f> >0,
    E0''(0)=-chi_T(0).                                (5)

H_o-E0 is positive definite at finite N because the unique ground is even.
Thus (5) needs neither a selected numerical age nor an assumed sign of a
response residue. It is a ground-state statement. It is not a statement
about the evolving original blank preparation, and it does not prove that a
retarded kernel is positive at every time.

## The source generated second contact

Restrict henceforth to G singlets. Decompose that space into P_s, Q_e and Q_o,
where Q_e is its even nonmatching part and Q_o its odd part. Q_o is the entire
odd G-singlet sector by (3). Define

    B=Q_e H P_s,
    H_e=Q_e H Q_e,   H_o=Q_o H Q_o,
    R_e(E)=(E-H_e)^(-1),   R_o(E)=(E-H_o)^(-1),
    Sigma(E)=B^dagger R_e(E) B.                        (6)

At E=E0 both resolvents are strictly negative. In particular E0 is strictly
below the Q_e compression ground: equality in the variational principle
would place the unique full ground wholly in Q_e, contradicting its positive
matching amplitudes. None of these finite-N strict gaps is an N-uniform
estimate.

Choose any nonzero solution p in P_s of the ground Feshbach equation
[P_s H P_s+Sigma(E0)]p=E0 p. Its scale is immaterial. Then

    psi0=(p+R_e Bp)/sqrt(D_p),
    D_p=p^dagger[I+B^dagger R_e^2 B]p
       =p^dagger[I-partial_E Sigma(E0)]p>0.             (7)

The sign follows from partial_E R_e=-R_e^2. In particular the normalization
factor is not I+partial_E Sigma and must not be omitted.

At fixed E, use the full source-dependent Q resolvent in

    H_eff(E,theta)=P_s H P_s
                     +P_s H Q(E-QH(theta)Q)^(-1)QH P_s.

B and P_s H P_s have no theta derivative because TP=0. One T insertion
changes even into odd and cannot return to P_s. Two can return. Therefore,
with resolvents evaluated at theta=0,

    partial_theta H_eff(E,0)=0 on P_s,
    partial_theta^2 H_eff(E,0)
       =2 B^dagger R_e T R_o T R_e B.                  (8)

The displayed second derivative is negative semidefinite. This is the
effective contact in the fixed-P chart, not a raw parent seagull. It includes
the interacting Q dynamics exactly; no bare denominator was substituted.

Since the first effective derivative is the zero operator for all E, there
is no first-order P_s state-relaxation contribution to E0'' at theta=0.
Implicit differentiation of the energy-dependent eigenproblem, or direct
substitution of (7) in (5), gives the identical exact result

    chi_T(0)=
      -2 p^dagger B^dagger R_e T R_o T R_e Bp / D_p.    (9)

This equality checks the source sign, the normalization metric and the
contact ownership together. Keeping only PTP would incorrectly give zero.
Keeping the first dressed source but dropping the second contact would
also incorrectly give zero in this particular symmetry sector.

## Exact bound and the missing uniform estimate

Let g_o=inf spectrum(H_o)-E0>0. Equations (4), (7) and (9) imply

    W_T=||T R_e Bp||^2/D_p,
    0<chi_T(0)<=2 W_T/g_o.                             (10)

The prior norm ||B||<=||QHP||<2Omega/sqrt(3) is useful but insufficient here.
It does not bound T R_e B on the actual state uniformly in N, nor give an
N-uniform g_o. T is an excluded-degree energy insertion, not a bounded
coefficient times the identity on Q. The existing O(sqrt(N)) bound on
<C_L+C_R> is a first-moment bound; it cannot silently replace the second
moment in W_T or the inverse-gap weighted measure in (9).

Thus the precise additional analytic target is a uniform bound on the
quadratic form of R_e T(-R_o)T R_e between the coupled matching vectors,
including the metric (7). A gap histogram in PHP cannot evaluate it because
PHP has no odd G-singlet sector at all. Finite-N positivity in (5) does not
answer whether this quantity vanishes, stays finite or grows per physical
volume at fixed Omega/U.

## A fixed size coefficient check

There is an independent path-counting check, useful without claiming a
uniform expansion. For fixed N, expand at h/U tending to zero from h>0,
using the unique positive ground of -P sum_e X_e P to select the first-order
matching state. Let w_m be its squared normalized amplitude on the uniform
size-m matching vector, so sum_m w_m=1 and each w_m>0.

Illegal additions centered on the left have bare cost 2U(1+theta), those
centered on the right cost 2U(1-theta). On a size-m singlet their Gram row
sums are separately 2h^2 m(N-m). The length-three outputs have one violation
on each layer, cost 4U independent of theta, and Gram row sum h^2 m(m-1).
The exact second-order bare singlet coefficient is therefore

    K2_s(m,theta)=
      -(h^2/U)m(N-m)[1/(1+theta)+1/(1-theta)]
      -h^2 m(m-1)/(4U).                               (11)

At theta=0 this is precisely the prior parent note's second-order singlet
coefficient. Differentiating this fully sourced coefficient yields

    chi_T(0)=(4h^2/U)sum_m w_m m(N-m)
                           +O_N(h^3/U^2),
    W_T=4h^2 sum_m w_m m(N-m)+O_N(h^3/U).              (12)

The W coefficient follows independently because T/(minus the bare energy
cost) is -1 on the left-centered length-two outputs, +1 on the right-centered
ones, and zero on the length-three outputs. Squaring and summing the two
cost-2U Gram forms gives the second line. Hence the leading chi coefficient
is the leading W coefficient divided by U, as required for transitions of
gap 2U. The leading response is a defect-scale contribution, not evidence of
a soft continuum.

The coefficient is positive for N>=2 because some 0<m<N have w_m>0, and
its displayed leading value is at most h^2 N^2/U=Omega^2/U. This is a bound
on that coefficient only. The O_N constants and their source derivatives
are not established uniformly in N. Equation (12) cannot be used at fixed
Omega/U while taking N to infinity without a new remainder theorem. The
auxiliary fixed-N expansion is not a recommendation to retune Omega/U with
N or a substitute for the exact expression (9).

## Consequence for the shared physical explanation

There is now a proved full-parent response omitted by the matching model,
but not a proved galaxy-scale mechanism. If the two layers are independently
assigned physical positions +/-ell/2 in the same weak-lapse convention, then
Z=ell T/(2c^2), theta=a_ext ell/(2c^2), and

    chi_Z(0)/V_N=ell^2 chi_T(0)/(4c^4 V_N).            (13)

Neither ell nor V_N is fixed by the graph count. A positive finite-N chi_T
does not establish a nonvanishing limit of (13), its acceleration dependence,
the baryonic mass normalization, or selection of this stationary state.
The within-layer anchored source transforms in standard G representations;
the positive odd singlet channel proved here does not by itself change its
separate projected bound or settle its full-parent correction.

The absolute onsite energy still participates in uniform clock and volume
work even though it cancels from (2). No volume deformation law or c(V)
normalization has been derived by this opposite-layer measurement. Any
claimed shared expansion mechanism must keep that same complete energy,
its volume derivatives and the formation or boundary work. The present
result supplies a concrete interacting source channel for that future
matching; it supplies neither negative-pressure closure nor an autonomous
preparation law.
