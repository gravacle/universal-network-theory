# Shared spatial length, physical clocks, and slow-mode energy

2026-10-04. Conditional analytic screen of the changed gradient parent. No
new expansion fit, numerical universe evolution or native clock claim.

For constant positive inertia m and J_delta=kappa delta^2 ell^2,

    omega_delta^2=kappa delta^2/m,
    c_delta^2=J_delta/m=ell^2 omega_delta^2.

Here c_delta is the high-wave-number speed in this declared mode sector,
not automatically the photon speed. omega_max=sqrt(kappa/m)Lambda is a free
physical clock normalization until it is derived or independently calibrated.
The equality omega_max=c_light/ell would be an ADOPTED saturation of the
fastest-mode causal speed. Causality alone permits smaller values and does
not prove that equality. A delta-dependent inertia is a changed functional
input; the simple spectral bound below assumes constant m.

## A deliberately explicit slow-curvature subset

Define the subset with longitudinal potential curvature

    kappa delta^2+3u4 |q_delta|^2 <= m Omega^2,  Omega=chi H.

chi=3 will be used as a permissive Hubble-timescale illustration. It is not
an exact universal condition for cosmic acceleration or overdamping in an
evolving background. In this subset, let x=kappa delta^2/(m Omega^2). Then

    |q|^2 <= m Omega^2(1-x)/(3u4),
    V2+V4 <= m^2 Omega^4/u4 * (1+4x-5x^2)/36.

Using nu=A delta, Kb=kappa A Lambda^2/2, gc^2=kappa Lambda^4/u4, set
alpha=Omega/omega_max and b=min(1,alpha^-2). Integration gives

    U_slow/(Kb gc^2) <= alpha^6 [b+2b^2-(5/3)b^3]/36.

For alpha<=1 this is alpha^6/27. It is a bound on slow-mode POTENTIAL energy,
not total excitation energy. Positive kinetic energy does not help sustained
negative pressure: its contribution to rho+3p is positive. The bound applies
even if every permitted slow mode is filled up to its curvature ceiling;
no such occupied state has been derived or prepared. Sparse occupation lowers
the result. Rapidly oscillating modes instead have averaged0<=w<=1/3 in
the already varied homogeneous action. A specially synchronized turning point
is not automatically sustained acceleration over a Hubble timescale.

If the whole finite band is intrinsically very slow, alpha can exceed one
and the small-alpha suppression no longer applies. That is a possible
physical alternative, not excluded by this screen, but has consequences for
galaxy formation/relaxation and energy preparation. For modes whose clocks
are of order H, ell-scale propagation takes order H^-1. The earlier static
galaxy state therefore cannot simply be assumed realized. With no damping
specified, an inverse frequency is an oscillation/response scale, not a
proved relaxation time.

## Interpretation ceiling

A small bound excludes only this proposed population as the source of an
order-Kb gc^2 slow potential budget under the stated clock choice. It does
not exclude all network acceleration, alternative mode spectra, variable
inertia, a different covariant action, or a genuinely derived formation
mechanism. It also does not establish the absolute cosmological normalization
from a static galaxy calibration. Such changes must be counted and tested.
Keeping the old finite DeltaU after removing bounded sourcing remains an
independent vacuum choice. Neither graph growth nor a time-coordinate change
alone supplies an energy/current production law or observed acceleration.
