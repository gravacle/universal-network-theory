## 1. Retained history cannot be both universally blind and interacting

Let F be a finite active-front quantum factor and B a fixed finite archive.
For U_t=exp(-iHt/hbar), suppose the reduced future on F depends only on its
reduced input for EVERY joint state, with arbitrary reference correlations,
and every t near zero. Heisenberg duality and differentiation imply

    [H,O_F tensor I] belongs to B(F) tensor I, for every O_F.

Expand the interaction in independent traceless archive operators:

    H=H_F tensor I+I tensor H_B+sum_mu A_mu tensor B_mu.

Closure requires [A_mu,O_F]=0 for every O_F, so every A_mu is scalar and can
be absorbed into H_B. Thus exact all-state, all-time future blindness on this
FIXED factorization permits no interacting archive-writing term. This does
not exclude a prepared blank-input/code sector, growing output factors,
restricted classical observable algebra or a subsequent quarantine stage.

There is an immediate native witness. If a record occupation Q is classified
as blind while incidence X is an active observable, BS feedback gives

    H_fb=-lambda_R n tensor Q,
    [H_fb,X tensor I]=i lambda_R Y tensor Q,
    Delta(d<X>/dt)=-lambda_R <Y> Delta<Q>/hbar.

Identical front states can therefore have different futures when retained
occupation differs. Such a record is part of the active causal state, not
universally invisible history. This is a native environmental effect already
present in the action, not a derived force law or a new dark component.

The existing constructions respect the distinction. BQ4's EQ12 uses
stage-dependent factorized isometries, growing archive spaces and an explicitly
admitted chronological future family. EQ12 is reference-stable for every state
on its stagewise active/archive factors, not merely one prepared code state.
Its escape from the screen is the changing isometry/output and future family,
not a claim of the strong autonomous fixed-factor hypothesis above. FPMH
writes first; its later quarantine G has identity dynamics/query, while the
pair memory K that gates links stays active. Neither theorem is invalidated.
Reusing their coefficients alone does not supply autonomous endpoint capacity,
binding/release, fresh resources or the physical selection of coalesced fronts.

## 2. Could a quantum matrix current supply the missing smooth cone?

There is a mathematically attractive alternative to four independent count
streams: a positive 2-by-2 Hermitian matrix

    J=t I+x sigma_x+y sigma_y+z sigma_z >=0

has t>=sqrt(x^2+y^2+z^2) and determinant t^2-x^2-y^2-z^2. Its positivity
cone is exactly the future Lorentz cone; sums of positive rank-one spinor
matrices stay in it. This elementary identity does not identify a physical
current, seconds, spatial intervals, boosts or a spacetime metric. Applying
it to native retained records is a NEW proposed dictionary, screened here
before any simulation or adoption.

In particular, the native qutrit's two occupied contents are not automatically
an arbitrary coherent spinor that can be copied. BS08 supplies

    K_(w->r)=sum_(x=0,1) |x><x|_w tensor
                         (|x><B|_r+|B><x|_r).

It copies the orthogonal alphabet and is content-exchange covariant, not a
universal SU(2)-covariant cloning operation. In the existing writer-only
active-clock preparation, records begin blank and carrier-record/fragment
copying is off. Every writer projector |x><x| commutes with the Hamiltonian.
Conditioned on that label, its record stays in span{B,x}. Tracing the retained
writer removes cross-label sectors. Therefore its occupied record block is
diagonal in the declared alphabet, for arbitrary coherent or entangled writer
input. Incidence correlations and occupation-only feedback do not restore
the missing occupied off-diagonal entries.

Consequently a proposed J made only by positively summing these occupied
record blocks spans I and sigma_z, not all Herm(2). In the saved fixed-content
states it is rank at most one (zero when no occupied record is present). It
cannot supply the proposed full 3+1 cone. For an
isolated completed ideal copy, writer |0> gives record |0><0|, whereas writer
|+> gives I/2 after retaining but tracing the writer. Equal occupied trace
has determinants 0 and1/4, respectively: the copying channel does not intertwine
arbitrary spinor rotations. The joint writer+record state still contains the
coherence; no physical erasure was performed or permitted by this argument.

FPMH instead transfers arbitrary quantum content without cloning it, but its
retained handoff bit is content-independent. A matrix CURRENT made from that
transferred content would require an additional identification. Likewise,
with carrier-record copying off, the prepared one-carrier generator has the
position/environment action tensor identity on carrier content. Changing an
internal spinor alone changes no transport direction. An internal Bloch cone
is not thereby the operational propagation cone.

These restrictions concern the specified protocols and raw reduced-record
dictionary. They do not forbid a different coherently prepared record state,
an encoded joint register, a derived link frame or spin-dependent collective
coupling. Such a proposal must define its observable/current and show that
its determinant tracks the SAME operational clock and transport; the matrix
