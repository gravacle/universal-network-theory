stability or transfer test. Conversely, a negative result for one precise
construct does not close every other lane or exclude all network models.

that is still undefined, a comparison not attempted, or mere absence of a
large worker is not completion. The goal is evidence, not a guaranteed
positive outcome. Continue routine authorized work without new milestone
