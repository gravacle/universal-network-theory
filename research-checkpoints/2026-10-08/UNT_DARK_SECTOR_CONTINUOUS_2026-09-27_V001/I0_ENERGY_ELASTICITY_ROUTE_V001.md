# I0 energy elasticity: a constructive target, not a fitted dark fluid

2026-09-27. Root analytical derivation. No new numerical experiment, empirical
fit, remote action, A0 freeze, or change to the primary active/maintenance rule.

## Result and status

A same-parent network energy law could generate matter-like and vacuum-like
background behavior from two different responses to physical expansion:

- A retained-record excess energy independent of volume gives zero isotropic
  pressure and dilutes as inverse volume.
- A complete energy proportional to physical volume gives negative pressure
  equal in magnitude to its energy density.

These are **conditional mechanical identities**. Neither follows from record
permanence or causal capacity alone. Their value is that they specify what a
microscopic allocation/energy law must deliver, without inserting a separate
dark fluid. The present finite record Hamiltonians do not yet deliver the
required physical deformation family. This note complements, not replaces,
the primary active/maintenance derivation.

## 1. Declare the interface before differentiating

Adopt, for this conditional screen, a physical isotropic volume V and a
reversible work convention dE = -p dV at fixed retained-record number R and
fixed other adiabatic state labels xi. A thermodynamic energy E(V,R,xi), if
used, must describe a controlled equilibrium or adiabatic branch. The actual
formed quantum state is not automatically its ground state.
Here fixed R means a fixed sector or a specified fixed distribution of sectors
and other state labels, not merely a matching mean count. In a mixture apply
the appropriate branch relation sector by sector; replacing nonlinear moments
by powers of the mean requires a separate justified approximation.

For a closed quantum state with Hamiltonian H(V) on a specified common state
space, driven only through the external parameter V, unitary evolution gives
the exact identity

    d<E>/dt = Tr[rho (partial H/partial V)] dV/dt,
    p_mech = -Tr[rho (partial H/partial V)].

This follows from Tr[H [H,rho]]=0. It is thermally isolated in this model but
not isolated from parameter work when V changes. Extra driven parameters add
their own work terms. An open subsystem has exchange terms; its density slope cannot replace
this mechanical derivative. Identification with the gravitational isotropic
stress remains an adopted or separately derived interface, not a consequence
of the trace identity.

Keep the energy ledger and the gravitational source assignment distinct. If
geometry is itself dynamical, derive the source/geometry split by variation
of the complete adopted action or equivalent parent, with its constraints.
Do not put a geometric contribution into a matter stress and also retain it
as the same geometric force. A controller's work cost belongs in the ledger,
but is not automatically an additional cosmological matter source.

For a smooth adiabatic energy branch the corresponding expression is
p = -(partial E/partial V)_(R,xi). For a general chosen state family rho(V),
the total derivative also contains Tr[H partial_V rho]; it must not be called
mechanical pressure without justifying the state evolution and its exchanges.

This use of a complete ledger, rather than treating interacting subsystem
energies as separately additive, is consistent with the issue analyzed by
[Alipour et al., 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5073246/).
Their particular division into heat, work and binding energy is not imported
as a UNT law. Our trace identity and subsequent differentiations are explicit
and do not require their subsystem partition.

## 2. Conditional homogeneous-density identity

Suppose one controlled branch admits

    E(V,R) = V e(n),       n = R/V,

with all remaining branch labels held as specified. Boundary, shape and
gradient corrections must be negligible in this declared regime, not simply
dropped. Then differentiation at fixed R gives exactly

    p = n e'(n) - e(n).

If R is conserved and an adopted material volume follows V proportional to
a^3, n follows a^-3. A contribution e_s(n)=c_s n^s has

    p_s = (s-1)e_s,       e_s proportional to a^(-3s).

The ratio p_s/e_s is defined only when that contribution is nonzero. Negative
energy contributions are not automatically admissible fluids. The identity
does not establish a transport law, isotropy, common gravity, or perturbation
stability.

In particular, if the independently derived energy has a regular dilute
expansion

    e(n) = e0 + mu n + B n^2 + O(n^3),

then

    E = e0 V + mu R + B R^2/V + ...,
    p = -e0 + B n^2 + O(n^3).

This use of the remainder requires derivative control as well: if r(n) is the
omitted energy density, take r(n)=O(n^3) and n r'(n)=O(n^3). A bound on its
values alone does not justify the pressure error estimate.

The constant and linear terms have the desired vacuum-like and dust-like
background signatures without assigning separate microscopic species. The
positive-energy versions additionally require e0>0 and mu>0; the algebra
does not guarantee these signs or a stable common gravitational coupling. The
quadratic correction has p_B=e_B and falls as a^-6 under the stated conditions.
An analytic density expansion is an **assumption to test**, not a theorem for
all networks: long-range correlations, gaplessness, topology, non-equilibrium
populations and phase transitions can invalidate it.

### What makes this an explanation rather than a relabelling

The network must determine e0, mu, interactions B, the admissible state branch,
the record formation history R, and their deformation law. Taking e0 and mu R
as independently fitted present-day dark densities simply reproduces the
unexplained ingredients. In the leading conditional regime

    rho_record/rho_background = mu R/(e0 V).

This varies as a^-3, not as a constant coincidence ratio. No preferred present
ratio, baryon relation or acceleration epoch has been derived here. A common
network origin is a worthwhile target, but it does not itself predict the
normalizations or initial conditions.

## 3. Do not hide cell-creation energy inside pressure

Let M denote independently countable microscopic cells, distinct from R, and
let a proposed network construction have V=v0 M. Suppose its energy is

    E = epsilon M + mu R.

If V and M are independent thermodynamic controls before imposing that path,
the correct differential is

    dE = -p dV + mu_M dM + mu_R dR,
    p = -(partial E/partial V)_(M,R) = 0,
    mu_M = epsilon.

Along dM=dV/v0 the path derivative is epsilon/v0, but it is chemical or
creation work, not a proof that p=-epsilon/v0. A material subsystem can have
constant density through creation with zero pressure.

Alternatively, M may be a constrained geometric degree of freedom of a
complete closed parent, not an independently exchanged population. In that
case the reduced geometry force may include its variation. One must derive
that constraint, its energy accounting and gravitational coupling from the
parent; inserting V=v0 M alone cannot choose between these interpretations.

This is especially important for the maintenance proposal. A number of
updates per time is a rate; multiplying by an event energy gives power, not
stored energy. Multiplying again by a selected timescale supplies an extra
constitutive assumption. Its volume derivative must include the clock,
update rule and supplying system, not only the number of records serviced.

For comparison, emergent-vacuum approaches also discuss thermodynamic
adjustment rather than identifying an arbitrary raw background energy with
the cosmological constant; see [Volovik, 2004](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0405012).
That paper's equilibrium condensed-matter analogy is not adopted here and
does not establish the UNT cancellation, residual value or expansion law.

## 4. What existing finite results actually supply

The declared record-coupled finite restriction on its source-off,
record-diagonal hold gives the insertion operator

    H(q_v=1)-H(q_v=0) = epsilon_r I - lambda_R d_v.

It is sensitive to the incident network structure, not just a token count.
It can constrain a candidate mu, but a matched-background expectation is not
identical to a fully relaxed formation energy. All background changes must
be included when using E_1-E_0. The current hold retains record occupations
without ongoing external maintenance power.

An energy offset independent of physical V cancels from this pressure
derivative. A term e0 V does not: it changes the physical force on geometry.
It cannot be installed or removed as an unobservable scalar phase after
claiming a gravitational stress. Fixed-geometry evolution alone cannot
determine that term or its magnitude.

The finite source-off state also has nonzero upper-band population. Replacing
it by a ground branch requires an actual preparation/relaxation mechanism and
its energy ledger. The two-band results rule out treating that replacement as
an innocuous change of basis.

## 5. Smallest decisive next test once the deformation is specified

The next useful calculation is a **same-parent energy/strain response**, not
a larger record-count demonstration. Before outputs, specify the parent,
physical or explicitly adopted V, deformation path, full couplings along it,
state/preparation, and which populations are fixed versus exchanged.
Specify boundaries and physical versus comoving separations for pair tests.

On that same path obtain:

1. E0(V): background energy and its complete geometry/controller work ledger,
   with the gravitational source assignment separately justified as above.
2. E1(V)-E0(V): complete one-record excess and its pressure derivative.
3. E2(V; separation)-E1a(V)-E1b(V)+E0(V): interaction and arrangement
   dependence, not an automatically density-only B coefficient.

The small-system results must then support, or refute, a controlled bulk and
dilute limit; three finite energies alone do not prove it. Independent force
and energy-difference calculations provide a direct conservation check.

For the restricted barotropic truncation e=e0+mu n+B n^2, a further exact
necessary screen is

    dp/de = 2 B n/(mu+2 B n).

The ratio excludes a zero denominator. It may be interpreted as squared
adiabatic sound speed in units c=1 only
after a compatible local fluid perturbation closure. For mu>0 and small n,
B<0 gives negative derivative and fails that simple stable-barotropic branch;
B>0 gives nonzero pressure corrections whose observational size is still
unknown. This does not rule out a nonlocal, multicomponent or non-equilibrium
network mechanism using a different derived perturbation law.

## Decision

Keep I0 open. The constructive target is now more specific: derive the
physical energy response of background, retained records and interactions
from the allocation rule, including any cell-creation or maintenance source.
Do not fit the constant and linear coefficients to dark densities and call
the result derived. A successful energy/strain test would support a limited
mechanism; transport, abundance, gravitational response and observations
would still be required for the joint dark-sector explanation.
