## Native dynamics supplies a correlation mechanism

The previous turn showed that graph correlations can change carrier return
despite identical mean links and initial modeled energy. The new question was
whether the native Hamiltonian can generate such correlations, rather than
requiring a new microscopic loop interaction.

In a declared fixed-record, carrier-free restriction of the BS action, native

    -k = -5h^4/(2U^3).

The diagonal fourth-order correction is constant on the fixed-degree manifold.
The remaining operator is therefore a switch adjacency, not a classical
relaxation generator. No microscopic loop term was inserted.

There is an exact all-N combinatorial identity on d-regular bipartite graphs:

    number of allowed switches = R0 + 2 C4,
    R0 = (Nd/2)[(N+2)d - 2d^2 - 1].

Consequently the derived fourth-order operator can be written as
`k L_switch - 2k C4_hat - k R0 I`. This exhibits an effective square-count
contribution, but it is inseparable from the mixing operator. Their coefficients
are fixed together. L_switch acts on whole graph configurations, not physical
vertices or spacetime; it supplies neither a metric nor stochastic cooling.
