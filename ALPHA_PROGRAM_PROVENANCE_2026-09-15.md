## 1. Executive disposition

For Alpha's role inside Record Formation, numerical derivation is not the
governing category. Alpha is constitutive identity data of an electromagnetic
parent. A different alpha is a different parent, just as a different atomic
number identifies a different element; it is not another adjustable value
inside the same fixed parent. The current work does not rule out a deeper
parent-selection law, but such a law would answer a separate `SELECT` question.

The program established three propositions that a traditional “derive alpha”
objective conflates:

1. **Active-EM coupling:** once a nontrivial charged visible-
   \(U(1)\) parent is supplied, canonical normalization gives a base coupling
   \(e=q_0/\sqrt{Z_F}\) and \(\alpha=e^2/(4\pi)\).  In the frozen cavity
   witness, setting \(e=0\) removes the nominated photon-record contrast.
   This is a parent premise plus a mechanism-specific ablation result, not a
   derivation of electromagnetism or of \(\alpha_{\rm obs}\).
2. **Same-sector inheritance:** every record independently shown to be an
   ordinary coefficient-preserving subsystem of one fixed visible-
   \(U(1)\) parent inherits that parent's base coupling and RG ancestry.  In
   the actual world, measurement anchors the parent value; SAI/AWAI then
   transports it conditionally to every `ACTVIS` record.
3. **Parent allowance and selection:** for the declared active-EM packet the
   exact `ALLOW` set is
   (I_\chi\cap(-\ln(1-\delta)/(4\pi B^2),\infty)), reducing at zero relevance
   floor to every positive alpha in (I_\chi). Bare record formation therefore
   does not choose the observed numerical value. A complete-universe singleton
   theorem and a law selecting the realized parent remain open and logically
   distinct.

The durable result is a correction of explanatory type, not a failed numerical
exercise: portable recordhood **ALLOWS** parent values on the exact declared
interval; one realized visible-EM parent **REQUIRES** its common inherited value
for same-sector records; and a dynamical **SELECT** law for the parent is neither
supplied nor presumed to exist.

## 2. The historical route

### 2.1 Early record-level coupling program

The early repository charter asked for the roles of EM, gravity, and alpha in
record formation.  At that stage, “alpha” was represented by a generic local
perturbation strength \(\lambda\), not by the measured electromagnetic
fine-structure constant.

Two results established the boundary of that route:

- `LANE_T10_PARAMS` swept 28 temperature, coupling, bath-size, time, and bath-
  spectrum settings.  Numerical responses changed, but the registered
  structural conclusion did not.  The coupling magnitude affected rates, not
  which interaction class formed the nominated record.
- `LANE_T14_ALPHA` established that the perturbative splitting exponent is
  \(n_*\), the fewest available local terms whose product acts non-scalarly on
  the code space.  The code distance \(d\) is the unrestricted special case,
  not the general law.

The subsequent T-22 disposition made the limitation explicit: this \(\lambda\)
was a generic magnitude, not the physical fine-structure constant; the lane
contained no dimensionful bridge or continuum map from its \(\mathbb Z_2\)
lattice setting to visible electromagnetism.  Therefore it could not calculate
the observed numerical value.

This established that the *traditional objective* was misframed, not that the
calculations were unreliable. The calculations showed that this particular
record-level apparatus carries structural exponents and rates but no
numerical-alpha selector.

`DEVELOPMENT_ALLOW_REQUIRE_SCOPE_REPAIR_V001` later generalized the alpha
ladder into repository-wide definitions:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{ALLOW}_P(v=a)
  &\Longleftrightarrow a\in S_P(v),\\
\operatorname{REQUIRE}_P(\phi)
  &\Longleftrightarrow \forall x\in{\cal A}_P,\ \phi(x),\\
\operatorname{SELECT}_{P,D,\mu_0}(v=a)
  &\Longleftrightarrow \text{a declared dynamical law selects occupancy }a.

For a nonempty numerical domain,
`REQUIRE_P(v=a*)` means `S_P(v)={a*}`.  Actual occupancy alone is not a
selection theorem.  Host-sector `REQUIRE` is a conditional universal inside a
parent that is already fixed; it is not an operator that produces the parent.

The hostile-audited scope repair also preserves a crucial parent distinction:
internal dynamics on a frozen parent changes occupancy without redefining that
parent's admitted set, while an enlarged parent may own explicit transitions
between effective carrier fibers.  The repair adds no new alpha value or
physical parent.

  parent coupling and RG trajectory.”
- “In our universe, measurement identifies the realized electromagnetic parent;
  same-sector ancestry then requires its exact alpha and RG trajectory.”

  coupling removes the photon-record contrast.”

  law are separate open questions.”

### Formulations the present custody does not support

- “Record formation derives \(1/137\ldots\).”
- “Electromagnetism was derived from recordhood.”
- “Every possible record requires visible electromagnetism.”
- “SAI proves a nonzero coupling without assuming an active charged parent.”
- “The ideal cavity construction proves two complete alternative universes.”
- “Same-sector inheritance explains why nature selected this parent.”
- “Actualization of one value proves a dynamical `SELECT` law.”
- “The older T-14 alpha variable was already the physical fine-structure
  constant.”

The phrase “EM requires alpha” is safe only after its domain is stated.  The
repository supports: **a supplied nontrivial charged visible-\(U(1)\) action has
one base coupling; the nominated active-EM write requires it to be nonzero; and
same-sector records require the realized parent's value.**  It does not support
the unqualified implication `REC -> visible U(1) -> alpha_obs`.

## 6. Empirical, conditional, and open inputs

### Empirical inputs

- The measured actual visible-sector value at a declared reference context,
  including uncertainty, scale, scheme, and observable.
- Experimental evidence for the tested universality of the visible coupling.
- For any concrete record to which AWAI is applied, independently established
  ancestry to the same visible photon eigenmode and parent action.  Agreement
  with alpha cannot itself be used to establish that ancestry.

These empirical anchors must receive external literature/data citations in the
paper.  The executable verifier does not measure them.

### Exact conditional results

- Canonical field-coordinate invariance and same-parent coefficient
  inheritance under SAI1--SAI8.
- One common RG ancestry when the RG initial-value problem and threshold maps
  are supplied and unique.
- Non-embedding of a genuinely inequivalent aligned alpha as an ordinary
  subsystem of the same frozen parent.
- The fixed-control ideal cavity contrasts and the non-singleton finite
  active-EM allowance set, within its declared validity domain.
- The formal `ALLOW/REQUIRE/SELECT` relations and frozen/enlarged-parent type
  distinctions.

### Open physics

- Emergence of compact \(U(1)\) and the visible electromagnetic action from
  record formation.
- Completeness of the actual gauge sector and the independently established
  `ACTVIS` ancestry of arbitrary records.
- Viability and cardinality of complete alternative matter/gravity universes.
- A first-principles numerical prediction of \(\alpha_{\rm obs}\).
- A physical process, if any, selecting the realized parent, vacuum, phase, or
  RG boundary condition.
- Quantum/UV stability of the illustrative phase-lock EFT and any universal
  interface or foreign-sector stability law.
