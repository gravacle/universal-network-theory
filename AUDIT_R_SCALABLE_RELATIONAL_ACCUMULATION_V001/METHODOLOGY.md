# Independent seed method: Scalable Relational Accumulation V001

The blind seed implementation does not import the target module, basis, or
matrices. It reverses the tensor-bit layout: retained carrier modes occupy the
low `2L` bits and genesis cells the high `L` bits. It independently enumerates
the exact fixed-total-content basis, constructs all prism and admission maps,
and uses an order-14 Taylor propagator with 96/192 Simpson steps rather than
the target's order-12 64/128 pair.

The comparison opens only completed L4/L6/L8 JSON records. It compares every
write, `ALLOW`/blocked probability, sector weight, connector differential,
late-history interval, owner ledger, and Boolean classification. Target/blind
agreement must be at most `1e-8`; every method must retain its own numerical
guards. The sealed L4 trace is an external control.

The seed can authorize only a frozen L10/L12 history-method stage. It cannot
authorize spectra directly and cannot claim a phase, continuum, emergence, or
gravity.
