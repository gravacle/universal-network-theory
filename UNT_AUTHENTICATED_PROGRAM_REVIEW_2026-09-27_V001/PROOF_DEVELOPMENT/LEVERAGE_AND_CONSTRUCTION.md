# Where to construct the theory next

The goal is not to defend every historical formulation. It is to construct a
small number of explicit mechanisms, prove what follows from them, and learn
which can support the broader theory. High leverage means that an answer
settles an important dependency with relatively little avoidable ambiguity,
not simply that its potential headline is dramatic.

## Recommended parallel research fronts

| Front | Why it has leverage now | Next concrete object | What success would and would not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operational lineage response | Starts from independently reproduced finite evidence and admits exact linear algebra | A pulled-back observable that detects the actual retained correlation; an analytic size-uniform bound or a rigorous decay/annihilation result | Establishes necessary dynamical information in the stated family, not gravity. |
| Dark-sector construction and identifiability | Can rapidly distinguish a predictive microscopic rule from adjustable bookkeeping | Record lifecycle, energy/work map, conserved budget and exchange law; derive the resulting background and parameter/initial-data dependence | Establishes a well-posed candidate or eliminates it before a fit; not dark matter/energy from counts alone. |

| Prospective finite curvature | Provides an independently controlled discriminator for a specific relational-object proposal | Fixed operator-incidence graph joined to authenticated all-event L8 response | Can support or reject that finite association, not prove continuum Ricci curvature. |
| Cosmological likelihoods | Useful only after the model predicts enough to be wrong | Frozen evolution, perturbations, priors, parameter count, data and prediction custody | Tests the specified effective model; cannot identify UNT ontology solely from a good fit. |

This is a dependency-based recommendation, not a numerical ranking or a claim
that the dark sector must wait for a full microscopic gravity proof. A
conditional effective-GR branch can test a record-inspired dark-sector rule
in parallel, provided GR and the continuum/fluid map are explicitly external
premises. Its outcome must not be described as deriving GR from UNT.

## Construct components before asking them to explain everything

For each new component, distinguish four layers:

1. **Definition:** What object exists in the model? A record, lineage, active
   write, retained state, maintenance operation, spatial relation, and physical
   energy are different objects until a rule connects them.
2. **Mechanism:** What state-transition, Hamiltonian, stochastic generator,
   constitutive law, or action determines its evolution? Supply units and
   conserved quantities. A metaphor does not determine an evolution law.
3. **Deduction:** Which quantities follow uniquely, conditional on specified
   parameters and initial data? Prove invariances, bounds, stability, and
   nonidentifiability, including explicit counterexamples.
4. **Physical identification:** Why should that mathematical quantity be
   identified with observed mass, pressure, a metric, or a dark component?
   State the independently testable consequences of that identification.

The theory may be built in stages. An assumption is permissible when declared;
its downstream result stays conditional. The useful task is then to replace
important assumptions with derivations or independent tests, not hide them.

## Dark-sector focus: two especially valuable questions

**Does maintenance determine pressure?** Maintenance is initially a rate of
operations or an expenditure of power. Cosmological density is energy per
physical volume; pressure is mechanical/metric response. They have different
units and roles. A stored-energy functional and a source of maintenance work
must connect them. Constant density can result from negative pressure or from
energy injection into pressureless material. The background density alone
does not distinguish them.

**Does the rule select an abundance or preserve a free normalization?** A
count per baryon is not predictive if its conversion factor or primordial
record count remains free. Conversely, a derived attractive ratio from a
closed production/destruction law would be a genuine mathematical advance,
even before its value is compared with data. One must show that the attraction
is stable, reached in the relevant history, compatible with energy
conservation, and not installed through target-dependent rate constants.

## What would count as meaningful progress

A complete explanatory theory is not the only valuable outcome. Examples of
rigorous progress are: proving which correlations a continuation can detect;
deriving a uniform local response bound; proving a proposed maintenance rule
cannot yield a distinct negative-pressure component; proving a stable ratio
is or is not selected; identifying a missing source term; or exhibiting two
probes that do not share the proposed metric. Each narrows the space of
possible theories and changes what should be calculated next.

Neither a failed candidate nor a successful conditional lemma should be
inflated into a verdict on all of UNT. Track exactly which construct it tests.
The existing finite results remain intact unless their own evidence is
challenged. The broader physical claims must earn their additional links.
