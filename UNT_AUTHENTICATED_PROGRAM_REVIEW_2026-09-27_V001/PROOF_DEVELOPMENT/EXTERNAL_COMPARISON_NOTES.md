# Optional comparisons, not imported UNT proofs

These primary-author arXiv records were checked on 2026-09-27 at title/abstract
level. This is a short literature triage, not a full-paper mathematical audit,
a claim of current observational viability, or a transfer of their results to
UNT. Any actual import needs a fresh domain/hypothesis comparison.

- **Matter and gauge structure:** Levin and Wen construct specific string-net
  models with topological phases and discuss emergent gauge bosons and
  fermions in higher-dimensional constructions. This is a concrete comparison
  for the ambition that collective network dynamics can support matter-like
  structure; it does not identify UNT's parent with their Hamiltonians.
  [String-net condensation](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0404617).
- **Discrete spacetime:** Sorkin's causal-set notes provide a distinct
  discrete-gravity research framework. Its definitions and dynamical choices
  must not be equated with an arbitrary graph or UNT's retained-lineage
  construction. [Causal Sets: Discrete Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0309009).
- **Influence bounds:** Nachtergaele and Sims review Lieb–Robinson bounds and
  their uses in quantum many-body models. This is a possible tool family for
  a concrete locality proof, after checking the actual interaction, geometry,
  state-space and boundedness hypotheses. It is not automatically a physical
  relativistic light cone. [Lieb–Robinson Bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.2086).
- **Dark-energy alternatives:** Ahmed and collaborators propose an
  “Everpresent Lambda” ansatz motivated by causal-set and unimodular-gravity
  ideas. It is an explicit alternative construction to compare with a future
  UNT rule, not a generic consequence of networks or a solution inherited by
  UNT. [Everpresent Lambda](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0209274).

Use these to ask sharper questions and identify independently developed
mechanisms or countermodels. Their existence is neither proof that UNT is
correct nor a reason that UNT must conform to their assumptions.
