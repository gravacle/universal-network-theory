# Network-first research stance

The user's foundational working hypothesis is that the universe is a network.
The research program will construct consequences from that premise seriously,
not treat conventional spacetime or particle descriptions as automatically
fundamental. The premise remains a hypothesis whose physical content must be
specified and tested; absolute certainty is not an evidential status.

The immediate question is not merely whether known physics can be represented
as a network. A representation can be very general. The explanatory question
is which network structure and dynamics produce the observed regularities,
what follows without being inserted, and what could have turned out otherwise.

## Elements to construct explicitly

| Element | Question | Hidden import to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Primitive objects | Are nodes physical subsystems, events, record degrees of freedom, or another specified object? What is a physical relation? | Assuming objects already occupy a physical spacetime whose emergence is the target. |
| State and admissibility | Which configurations or operator states exist, and which distinctions are gauge versus physical? | Treating a chosen representation as a derived ontology. |
| Dynamics | What local or nonlocal update law is allowed? What determines transition amplitudes/rates and causal order? | Importing a preferred clock, dimensional scale, locality, or conservation law without declaring it. |
| Record and retention | Which physical correlations constitute a record, persist, and affect future dynamics? | Inferring physical memory from any mathematical correlation or counting the same energy twice. |
| Operational readout | Which observable procedure extracts distance, duration, energy, pressure, or a propagation law? | Calling a graph metric, information metric, or computational budget its physical counterpart by definition. |
| Scale correspondence | How are small constructions related to larger domains and observations? | Assuming a thermodynamic/continuum law applies at the microscopic scale or vice versa. |

These choices need not all be fixed before useful work begins. Each candidate
can state its own domain, expose its assumptions, and face small tests. The
existing owner-once model supplies one concrete finite family; it does not yet
select the unique fundamental network dynamics.

## What would explain "why"

A persuasive mechanism would show that a structural constraint on the native
network forces a result across a defined family, rather than reproducing it
only after a favorable graph, coefficient, or initial state is selected. A
useful test then changes the proposed causal ingredient while holding the
relevant alternatives fixed. If the effect changes as predicted, the mechanism
earns support. If it does not, the failed link is localized.

For dark-sector research this means deriving how the network's actual
transition/retention rule fixes energy, stress, transport and populations.
For the current lineage work it means identifying the correlation component
that the continuation reads and proving or testing its size dependence. For
geometry it means showing that independently defined physical probes agree on
one relational structure without imposing that agreement.

The working commitment is to build the strongest testable version of the
network idea and actively seek its decisive checks. Neither conventional
authority nor attachment to UNT determines the result. Successful, negative,
and unresolved outcomes all remain part of the authenticated picture.

## When evidence points another way: ask why

Treat a discrepancy as a question to investigate, not automatically a verdict
on the entire framework or an excuse to protect it. Trace the chain from raw
observation through calibration, model-dependent extraction, theoretical
assumptions, scale correspondence, numerical implementation and predicted
observable. Ask which link actually generated the disagreement.

Separate direct measurements from quantities inferred under a particular
model. Challenge an inherited assumption when its domain is unearned, but
also challenge new UNT assumptions by the same rule. Preserve the original
failed prediction and its inputs. For each proposed repair, explain why it is
independently motivated, identify what changes and what stays fixed, expose
new parameters, and derive a further test. A repair that merely accommodates
the already-seen discrepancy is a candidate explanation, not independent
confirmation.

This protocol supports persistence: a failed implementation, analogy,
continuum import or constitutive rule need not defeat the network hypothesis.
It also preserves the possibility of a real counterexample to a concrete UNT
claim. The task is to learn which case we actually have.
