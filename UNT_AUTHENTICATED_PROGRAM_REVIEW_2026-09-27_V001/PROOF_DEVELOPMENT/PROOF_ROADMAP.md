# From finite lineage response to a testable physical theory

Status: prospective proof-development program, not a claim that its open
theorems have been proved. This document selects a concrete route through
the current UNT obligations; it does not assert that every conceivable route
to gravity must take exactly these steps.

## The question worth proving

Can the frozen record-forming dynamics generate retained information that
has a persistent, local, quantitatively identifiable effect on future
propagation, and can that same response be shown to define a common physical
geometry with the required reciprocal stress response?

This is stronger and more useful than asking whether one can write an
Einstein-shaped equation after adopting a physical metric and its response
class. The existing conditional theorem answers the latter question. The
new program must earn its antecedents from the declared microscopic model.

The L4 result is an excellent starting point because it is operational: two
states with equal separate marginals respond differently under the same
continuation. However, correlations affecting reduced dynamics is compatible
with ordinary quantum mechanics. By itself it is not a distinctive proof of
UNT, new gravitational physics, or failure of quantum mechanics. The added
scientific burden is to derive the specific persistent structure, common
metric, coefficients, or prediction that the theory proposes.

## 1. Identify exactly what information is dynamically necessary

**New finite mathematical target:** characterize when a carrier response is
determined by sector marginals and when joint information is necessary.
`LINEAGE_RESPONSE_LEMMA_V001.md` supplies a self-contained kernel criterion
and its observable-space dual, rather than treating the product replacement
as a physical quantum channel. This is a general finite-dimensional lemma,
not a claim of new fundamental physics or historical priority.

**Application to UNT:** authenticate the frozen joint state, q-sector
dephasing, per-sector product comparator, and common continuation. Establish
that a measured difference is a difference in joint information, not in
sector weights, starting marginals, transport, or readout. The existing L4
controls already supply one such application. Its independent numerical
agreement is not an all-size theorem.

**Deliverable:** an exact model-specific witness identifying which pulled-back
carrier observable accesses the retained correlation. This explains the
mechanism; a trace-distance number alone does not identify it.

**Failure conditions:** the claimed difference vanishes after fixing a
comparator mismatch; changed marginal/sector weights explain it; or the
observable's pulled-back component lies wholly in the separate-marginal
observable space.

## 2. Prove a response lower bound that survives increasing size

**Finite numerical screen:** run independently frozen L6, then L8, with
durable checkpoints and prospectively fixed observables, tolerances, and
interpretation. Report increases, declines, sign reversals, and nulls without
changing the criterion after inspection. These computations diagnose the
next lemma; three sizes do not prove a limit.

**Actual theorem target:** choose a physically justified common local
observable family `O_L`, an explicitly comparable time/continuation convention,
and the actual frozen reachable states. Prove constants `c>0`, `t0>0`, and
`L0`, independent of L, such that a specified response diagnostic is at least
`c` (or has an explicitly stated scaling law) for every admissible even
`L>=L0` at the stated times. If this is false, a decay upper bound is an
equally valuable outcome. Do not assume the desired lower bound as a premise
and call that persistence derived.

`LOCAL_RESPONSE_BOUND_V001.md` reduces one possible short-time route to two
concrete obligations: a nonvanishing correlation–commutator pairing and a
uniform remainder bound. It is a new conditional mathematical tool, not a
proof that the actual UNT sequence satisfies either obligation. The actual
discrete admission mechanism must first be shown to admit the specified
continuous interpolation; this cannot be silently substituted for the frozen
event-zero experiment.

**Missing comparison structure:** L4/6/8/10/12 are independent model domains.
Specify local embeddings or observable identifications, state preparation,
boundary effects, normalization, and the relationship of L to physical size
before invoking a thermodynamic or continuum argument.

## 3. Derive the relational object before testing its curvature

**Target:** construct a relation or propagation operator solely from the same
parent dynamics and an independently fixed readout rule. State which changes
are coordinate conventions, which are physical interventions, and which
parameters are measured or arbitrary. Derive locality, stable positivity or
the appropriate hyperbolicity, and an identifiable long-wavelength response.

The unweighted formation-order path already gave a controlled null. The new
operator-incidence graph is a separate prospective candidate; it must not be
chosen or reweighted to match the desired outcome. Authenticate its conductance
and all-event L8 response independently before joining them.

**Finite test deliverable:** target/hostile agreement for the fixed response
and the two independent transport-distance solvers, complete permutation null,
effect size, and sensitivity analysis specified before unblinding. A pass
would support a finite model association, not establish spacetime curvature.

**Theorem deliverable:** prove that the chosen discrete object approximates a
specified continuum quantity under a defined refinement, with controlled
errors. The refinement theorem is required if this finite object is used as a
continuum antecedent; it need not block unrelated microscopic lemmas.

## 4. Earn one physical metric, not several compatible-looking tensors

PMICS demonstrates local curvature-symbol capacity; PMSR gives a bounded
commuting-parent reciprocity result; GL6AV provides partial collective
response. A matching number of components does not identify their tensor
types or make their underlying parents the same.

**Target:** construct the same metric object from independent operational
probes in the same state and parent, with controlled agreement of proper-time,
transport/principal propagation, and variational response. Explain signature,
causal cones, units, gauge redundancies, and how spatial data is completed
temporally. Separate an information/Fisher metric from a physical spacetime
metric until a theorem identifies them.

**Decisive obstruction:** different probes require incompatible metrics or
cones that cannot be accounted for by controlled corrections. Such an outcome
would constrain or refute the proposed common-metric route even if each
individual tensor is well defined.

This directly addresses RGRL-A/B/C and E1 rather than assuming their physical
content under a new name.

## 5. Derive complete sources and close the reciprocal loop

**Target:** obtain the complete stress, currents, ports, contacts, and initial
constraints from the same parent; prove the required conservation/Ward
identity and propagation of constraints. Demonstrate an intervention loop:
records change geometry, the resulting complete stress changes the metric,
and that same metric changes subsequent matter/EM/clock/probe/record dynamics.

The finite commuting PMSR identity and forward-response examples are building
blocks, not a substitute for the noncommuting full-parent join. Do not replace
the complete source by a selected pair-sector conjugate or infer reciprocity
from one forward influence.

**Deliverable:** one source-and-response theorem with a complete term census,
physical assumptions separated from definitions, and limiting cases that
expose cancellation, missing directions, and broken conservation. This earns
E2/E3. Partial exact results remain valuable if the full join fails.

## 6. Apply the conditional endpoint, then test a prediction

Once its antecedents are earned, apply the existing WTC/RIEHB endpoint at its
declared approximation order. Prove the size of omitted terms and the absence
or suppression of competing leading channels in the chosen model. State the
coefficient status plainly:

- an empirically calibrated positive `G` is not a parameter-free prediction;
- the allowed `Lambda` coefficient is not its observed value or radiative
  stability;
- inherited same-domain alpha is not numerical/domain selection;
- a dark-sector abundance is not predicted if its normalization or initial
  condition remains freely chosen.

Choose a quantitative discriminator whose data was not used to fix the model,
graph, coefficients, or analysis. Freeze the prediction, uncertainty, null
model, acceptance rule, and comparison custody before observing its outcome.
Mathematical proof establishes what the model implies; this independent test
addresses whether the implication describes nature.

## Immediate division of work

The compute Mac develops the independently restartable L6 target/hostile
successors and small qualification, then measured production gates. This Mac
develops and checks the operational-sufficiency lemma, a model-specific
witness, the persistence bounds, and the missing common-parent joins. File
repairs preserve evidence needed for those proofs; they are not themselves
proof progress. No resource-heavy calculation is a prerequisite for finding a
logical mismatch or proving a finite-dimensional identity.

Each proposed theorem should travel with: exact domain and quantifiers;
definitions; adopted and empirically anchored premises; lemmas; an inspectable
proof; independent attempts at counterexamples; executable small cases where
appropriate; source/evidence hashes; and the narrowest warranted physical
interpretation. A result enters the established claim ledger only after that
review, not because it appears in this roadmap.
