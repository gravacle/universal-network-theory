# Validation findings and meanings

This record distinguishes the baseline failures, authorized repairs, and
remaining custody limits. A passing byte seal is not a proof of physics; an
aggregate nonzero exit remains a failure even when its cause is understood.

hashes, exit statuses, wall times and complete log hashes. The full URM run
took 335.337 seconds and exited 1. Its 176 model-family checks and the project,
geometry, formation, frontier, U-DCL, GFT, microscopic progress, witness,

The old migration and dark-prerequisite validators refused their exact f597
HEAD precondition when run at e640. Those two scripts are frozen snapshot
checks, not moving-HEAD validators. Their nonzero exits are preserved, not
"fixed" by changing old seals or pretending the current HEAD is f597.

Successful small checks included L4 V002 packet custody, target/hostile
reconciliation, 15 fresh-baseline restart tests, reconciliation mutation
