## 2. Feasibility of measuring gravity

**Direct numerical measurement: not reachable.**
- At h = 1/5 and U_d = 1, light comes from sixth-order ring exchange: g ≈ (63/8)h⁶/U_d⁵ ≈ 5.7e-4, with speed about 7e-4 bonds/tick.
- Carriers hop at t = 1.
- Resolving light needs β ≳ 2000 and real-time windows ≳ 10³. The N16 4-cube is too small for fronts.

**Analytic decomposition: reachable, and decisive.**
- **Universal part.** If local time is counted in Records, a Record-clock rate that depends on density acts as a lapse N(ρ) and rescales every species' speed equally. That is the gravity signature. Derive the clock rate as a function of local Record density.
- **Medium part.** Derive the Record-density dependence of:
  - the light speed (ring exchange with Record-dependent corrections to the vertex and incidence energies);
  - the matter speed (hopping t with λ_J, λ_R and g corrections).
  In general these differ, which is a medium effect.
- **Verdict rule.** The model is gravity-like iff the universal part dominates the medium part at large scales.
- **Null.** A clock rate independent of ρ means no gravity from this model.
- **Positive control.** The same pipeline on a lattice with an explicit lapse multiplying every term must return a universal shift. A lattice with a density-dependent sound speed must return a non-universal one.

Report the clock-rate derivation across the ε_r region, including ε_r = 1, and say whether the clock survives the full dynamics long enough to serve as a time standard.
