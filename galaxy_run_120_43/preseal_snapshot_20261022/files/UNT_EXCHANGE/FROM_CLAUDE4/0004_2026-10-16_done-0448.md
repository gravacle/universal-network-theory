## Findings

1. **Formula and convention.** Gkin = -rho divU - rho_R U_R - rho_z U_z is the Eulerian -div(rho xi). divU carries the only phi term and the only U_R/R term, and the signature has no U_phi, so a duplicate phi term is structurally impossible. Checked with analytic nonconstant rho against a 4th-order finite-difference -div(rho xi), m = 1..6: 4.4e-12.
2. **EOS provenance** (provider L44-46). K_z = 0. P_R and P_z come from the lagged-rotation relations, not from differentiating P. rho_R and rho_z follow from the EOS. With P = K rho^gamma, the 0439 PM computed with G = Gkin is exactly -gamma P divU - xi.grad(P) (3.3e-16 through the frozen 0439 module). Gkin certifies continuity relative to these supplied gradients, not the consistency of the stored columns.
3. **Pointwise vs weak.** Exact pointwise continuity does not imply the finite weak identity V^H W G = b, and the reverse fails too. The difference between them is the dropped boundary flux plus quadrature inexactness: non-polynomial rho, regularity powers, sqrt(s) factors, and the old spline breakpoints inside Gauss-Legendre groups (root's radial defect of 0.79). Even with the weak identity satisfied, the 0438 G equals only the projection Pi_V(Gkin). The 6.339 discrepancy is therefore a representation and resolution diagnostic, not a coding error, and it does not show which representation is right.
4. **Hermiticity** (diagnostic, not a repair). With G = Gkin and the provider gradients, A - A^H = U_R^H D(q) U_z - U_z^H D(q) U_R with q = w rho[R d_z(Omega^2) + (K_R/K) P_z/(gamma rho)] (6.6e-15). A is Hermitian if and only if the fixed-K thermal wind R d_z(Omega^2) = -(K_R/K) P_z/(gamma rho) holds pointwise (4.7e-16 when imposed). This is consistent with root's "fixed symmetry". Any residual anti-Hermitian part measures input thermal-wind inconsistency. Do not symmetrize it, and do not read Hermiticity as an equilibrium certificate.
5. **Implementation.**
