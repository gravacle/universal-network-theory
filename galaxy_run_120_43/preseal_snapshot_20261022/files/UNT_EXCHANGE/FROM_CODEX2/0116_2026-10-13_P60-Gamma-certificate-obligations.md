## 1. What can be proved from the diagonal native coefficients

On a loopless q4 graph write s_v=d_v-2. At fixed p/q/cell words the incidence diagonal energy relative to its ice value is exactly

 Delta E_D = sum_v [Ud s_v² + mu_v s_v],

This follows by expanding the degree penalty and using sum_v s_v=2(sum_e n_e-Nv). For integer s_v, s_v²>=|s_v|. Therefore if

 delta=min_v (Ud-|mu_v|)>0,

then Delta E_D>=delta sum_v |s_v|. Off ice the sum of the s_v is even. A nonzero configuration cannot have sum |s_v|=1, so Delta E_D>=2delta. The assertion presumes that ice configurations exist; it is a diagonal fixed-word comparison, not a quantum many-body spectral gap.

For all binary p,q words on the degree-lock ray, a sufficient word-uniform diagonal condition is

 Ud > max(0, |lambda_J|, |lambda_R|, |lambda_J-lambda_R|).

For nonnegative lambda_J and lambda_R the right side is max(lambda_J,lambda_R). In the p=0,q=1 word, it reduces to Ud>lambda_R (for positive lambda_R), consistent with the written carrier-free edge cost 2(Ud-lambda_R). A zero/nonpositive sufficient bound is INCONCLUSIVE, not proof of no gap: graph constraints can prevent simultaneous minimizers.
