## What was added (one paragraph)

v005 is v004 plus ONLY an explicit OPTIONAL extended reference-cut sector. Sector0 is the ordinary closed path (the v004 measure). Sector2 is an open thermal path x -> z plus an ordered marked closing pair z -> y -> x weighted by xi*beta^2*W_a(y<-z)*W_b(x<-y). The pair is kept outside the thermal event-time array. Sector switches are the W2 SPEC's unconditioned full-duration proposals with its exact log MH ratios. The bridge A(x,z) is the intersection of COMPLETE primitive_channels target-key maps at x and z. y is drawn by the products, marks by their rates, and the x -> y mark is converted into its exact y -> x adjoint and verified. Ordinary estimates become conditional on sector0. The two-hop numerator sum_sector2 g / (xi beta^2 N0) is formed from joint sector counts, with joint block sums, a ratio jackknife and extended-chain mixing. The extension is reached only through a NEW public entry. The v004 public entry keeps its signature and semantics.

## LIMITATIONS of this delta (ambiguities recorded, not resolved by inventing physics)

1. **O, the "actual adapter read vector".**
   - The W2 SPEC and W2 HELPER use a 9-vector O without saying which reads form it: one cell's whitened R'/S', a cell sum, or a mode projection.
   - v005 takes 9 caller labels in order: operator labels, callback whitened labels, or the L23 uniform labels when active.
   - It applies no Mandel or multiplicity factor (L33).
2. **"Full thermal path refresh with endpoint z rejected unless matching is also valid."**
   - Implemented as: the whole-path refresh must end at z, else reject.
   - Re-matching the marked pair at a new endpoint is not implemented. Other endpoints are reached by switching (L30).
3. **Sector2 interval density.**
   - The W2 SPEC fixes only "endpoints fixed, no rotation across the cut".
   - v005 reuses the L10 density. Its "whole" draw is the whole open path, and other intervals are clipped at beta, never wrapped.
   - This choice is state independent and symmetric, so it is valid for any target. It is an implementation choice, not physics.
4. **Palette move in sector2.** The W2 SPEC says blocked regrowth "may" be reused. v005 does not offer the palette move in sector2 (sector0 keeps it).
5. **Adjoint rate tolerance.**
   - The x -> y versus y -> x rate is verified within `reverse_rate`, and the exact term log(W_b/W_b') is carried in every ratio.
   - The target therefore uses the verified y -> x rate exactly. This differs from the W2 SPEC's literal ratio only when the rates are not bitwise equal; with operator v002 they are, so the term is 0.
   - Reviewers should confirm this reading.
6. **Mixing rule and columns.**
   - The extended-chain primary columns are my diagnostic choice: the indicator, sector2-masked g, sector0-masked score/contact/reads, and events.
   - So is the rule that a chain with zero accepted 0 -> 2 or zero accepted 2 -> 0 switches in measurement fails mixing.
   - The thresholds are the caller's v004 error_parameters.
7. **NaN placeholders.**
   - W2 rows of the other sector are NaN (score/contact/reads in sector2 rows, g in sector0 rows), identified by the sector array.
   - This is a deliberate fail-closed placeholder, never zero padding. It differs from v004 prefixes, which never contain placeholders.
8. **New resource input.** `max_bridge_channels` is a per-endpoint resource cap that the caller must choose. Bridge maps are held in memory, not streamed.
9. **Unit-weight gate.**
   - Requires the declared string and every lapse factor exactly 1.0.
   - That the requested E is this unit-lapse H is the caller's/root's declaration (L28).
   - Lapse and mode-direction derivative coordinates stay derivatives at n = 0 of sector0-conditional quantities.
10. **Not done here:**
    - b_onehop, hbar and the combination b = mean b_onehop - numerator / hbar^2 (the consumer is owned_cosmic_worldline_dae.complete_b per the W2 HELPER; not read);
    - the eta = 0 stationarity comparison;
    - DAE time contacts and normal-constraint consistency.
11. **Output-level differences on the original entry.**
    - The result has the extra key `"twohop"` (always None) and `conventions["twohop"]`.
    - Progress records name module `..._v005`.
    - No RNG draw, counter, array, admission rule or analysis step changed. This is by static AST comparison, not measured.
12. **Cost.**
    - Every 0 -> 2 proposal enumerates x and z completely and computes one canonical key per positive-rate channel there.
    - A sector2 state holds its bridge.
    - No scalable runtime, acceptance rate or mixing time is claimed. Full-duration switching can accept rarely at large beta or domain (L30).
