## 1. Mass-advection identity, cylindrical convention, no duplicate phi term

delta_rho = -div(rho xi) = -rho div(xi) - xi.grad(rho). The background rho(R,z) is axisymmetric, so grad(rho) = (rho_R, 0, rho_z). divU = d_R U_R + U_R/R + i m U_phi/R + d_z U_z (helicity L64) already carries the only phi term and the only U_R/R term. Therefore Gkin = -rho divU - rho_R U_R - rho_z U_z has **no duplicate phi or 1/R term**. The signature has no U_phi argument, so duplication is structurally impossible.

This is the **Eulerian** perturbation that the 0439 PM requires, not the Lagrangian -rho div(xi).

Verified with analytic nonconstant rho = rho0 (1 + a R^2) exp(-z^2/h^2) and complex xi for m = 1..6. Module output vs a 4th-order finite-difference -div(rho xi): **4.4e-12**, which is finite-difference accuracy.

## 2. Provenance of the EOS gradients (provider L44-46)

The provider defines:

- K_R = K d_R ln K, from the saved entropy derivative.
- K_z = 0, the fixed-K column law.
- P_R = rho * rotation_pressure_R_over_rho and P_z = rho * rotation_pressure_z_over_rho. These are saved analytic lagged-rotation relations, **not derivatives of the stored P**.
- rho_R = (P_R - rho^gamma K_R)/(gamma P/rho) and rho_z = P_z/(gamma P/rho).

Consequences:

- **(a) PM becomes the exact Eulerian adiabatic delta P.** With these gradients and P = K rho^gamma on the supplied arrays, the 0439 PM evaluated with G = Gkin reduces exactly to -gamma P divU - (P_R U_R + P_z U_z). Checked through the frozen 0439 module: **3.3e-16**.
- **(b) What Gkin certifies.** Gkin is exactly -div(rho xi) for the density field whose gradient is the EOS-implied (rho_R, rho_z) of the lagged-rotation pressure relations. It equals the true gradient of the stored rho columns only as far as those columns satisfy the lagged relations. So Gkin certifies pointwise continuity relative to the supplied gradients. It does not certify hydrostatic or lagged consistency of the stored state.
- **(c) EOS consistency is root's responsibility.** If the supplied P differs from K rho^gamma, PM no longer reduces to the adiabatic form. 0447 cannot check this, because P and K are not its inputs.

## 3. Pointwise continuity and the finite weak identity are different claims

- **Claim P (pointwise).** Gkin(x_p) = -div(rho xi)(x_p) at every 32q node, given exact U, divU and gradients. This is an algebraic identity. It is independent of quadrature, trial space and boundary, and it holds here (section 1).
- **Claim W (finite weak).** V^H W G = b, with b = VR^H W rho xi_R - i m V^H W rho xi_phi/R + VZ^H W rho xi_z for test functions in span(V). The 0438 projected G satisfies W exactly by construction.

**P does not imply W.** V^H W Gkin - b is the discrete integration-by-parts defect. It equals the dropped boundary flux -oint conj(psi) rho xi.n plus the quadrature error on the two integrands. It vanishes only if both of the following hold:

1. The quadrature integrates both integrands exactly. This fails for non-polynomial rho, its gradients, the regularity powers (R/L)^e, and the sqrt(s) factor in d_R. On the old 72-knot radial maps it also fails because the spline breakpoints fall inside the Gauss-Legendre groups. That is the radial scalar defect of 0.79 that root measured in 0451.
2. rho xi.n vanishes on the boundary. Physical vacuum at the free surfaces is assumed, not verified on the discrete fields. Outer xi_R = 0 is imposed as a polynomial trace. The axis relies on regularity.

**Projection.** Even if W held, the 0438 G would equal only Pi_V(Gkin), the L2(W) projection onto 8 Legendre functions per column. Pointwise equality would additionally need Gkin to lie in span(V). That generically fails, because rho and grad(rho) multiply polynomial xi.

**W does not imply P.** The projected G matches the weak functional exactly, but its pointwise values are those of a projection. They can deviate most where rho' and the vertical displacement are large, near the surfaces.

**Consequence.** The 6.339 strong-vs-weak density difference is a representation and resolution diagnostic. It is not evidence of a coding error in either map, and it does not tell which representation is "right". The two should converge only as the quadrature becomes exact for the integrands, the boundary flux vanishes and resolution increases. This is consistent with root's 0451 radial-defect finding.

## 4. Hermiticity: a diagnostic, never a repair

This reuses the 0440 thermal-wind derivation. Suppose A is assembled with G = Gkin, using the provider's EOS gradients and K_z = 0. Then exactly:

A - A^H = U_R^H D(q) U_z - U_z^H D(q) U_R, with q = w[2 rho R Omega Omega_z + (K_R/K) P_z/gamma] = w rho [R d_z(Omega^2) + (K_R/K) P_z/(gamma rho)].

Checked through the frozen 0439 module: **6.6e-15**.

A is exactly Hermitian if and only if the fixed-K-column thermal wind R d_z(Omega^2) = -(K_R/K) P_z/(gamma rho) holds at every node. When that relation is imposed, A is Hermitian to **4.7e-16**. This is consistent with root's report that Gkin "fixed symmetry".

Any remaining anti-Hermitian part measures thermal-wind inconsistency among the supplied Omega_z, P_z and K_R. The Omega_R spline approximation does not enter q. That part must not be symmetrized. Hermiticity is not an equilibrium certificate.
