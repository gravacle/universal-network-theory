**F2 (blocking) — adopting the cube alone is not permitted by the pre-registered decision order as written, and the pre-named recovery was not applied.**
- Decision rule 1: "Any registered case invalid or missing → INCOMPLETE (name each case and failed gate, with that gate's pre-named next step; no threshold or rule change)." S2 and Y2 are registered cases and are invalid (FAILED_AT_STAGE). Rule 5 ("ADOPT A*" among passing shapes, which may be a single shape) is reached only when rule 1 does not fire, i.e. when every registered case is valid. The amendment's sentence "the prereg already allows a single passing shape" conflates the two: one *passing* shape among *valid* cases is allowed; proceeding with cube while sphere/cylinder cases are invalid is not.
- "BRACKET_PRECONDITION_FAILED" is not a pre-named outcome. The pre-named outcome for exactly this failure is gate V1 (ii), "FAILED_AT_STAGE with a bracket miss in a column pass: the D7 recovery", and P6's `declared_recovery`: widen only the missed columns' override entries with the failed call's column enthalpies as evidence, write a corrected base spec, rerun the failed call admitting its own prefix, and regenerate every case with the corrected brackets block. That recovery was not attempted for S2/Y2 (6 and 1 missed columns respectively, all "supply a resolved compact-support bracket").
- Withdrawing the sphere and cylinder shapes also makes rule 4 (shape disagreement at K=4, 2·τ_i) vacuous and reduces the matrix to one shape, i.e. it weakens the pre-registered convergence evidence. That is a decision-rule change and must be recorded as one, with its reason.
- Fix (V005), one of: (a) run the D7 recovery for S2/Y2 as pre-registered (the amendment's "registered results" stand as the record of the first attempt); or (b) add a new pre-named outcome and rule — "a shape whose G2 case fails the bracket precondition after the D7 recovery (or with the recovery declared not applicable, with the reason) is WITHDRAWN; withdrawn shapes are removed from rules 1, 3, 4 and 5; the adoption is labelled SINGLE_SHAPE and the shape-agreement term of the carried uncertainty u_i (rule 6) is reported as not evaluated" — sealed before any v005 result exists.
