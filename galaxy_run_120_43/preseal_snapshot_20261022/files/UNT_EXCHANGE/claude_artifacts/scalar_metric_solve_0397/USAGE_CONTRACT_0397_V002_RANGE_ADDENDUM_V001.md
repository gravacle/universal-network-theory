This addendum records the numerical-range warning from the two distinct V002 checkers: REVIEW_0397_V002_SOURCE_DELTA.md (sha256 5df6d6966c4b8c72ca2e1b71d90a9a53763814d57b6e10494a1bf394c5e89eda, finding N1) and REVIEW_0397_V002_ADVERSARIAL.md (sha256 3b086bcf00984afb781095d588307ec6c81c2c66ed091108c2c5304c5d56ea6b, findings N1, N2 and N3). Finite admission does not mean the module is reliable at every scale. An admitted, finite input can still produce a misleading finite zero while `ok` is True, and the reviewers record three such paths. (i) Norm underflow (source-delta N1; adversarial N3): the sum-of-squares norms lose precision for components below about 1.5e-154 and square to 0 below about 1.6e-162. A nonzero residual can then give `residual_norm` 0.0 and `relative_residual_to_F` 0.0, a spuriously perfect value. A nonzero F_eff or s_max·q_norm can give 0.0, which makes the ratio None. The contract's phrase "denominator … exactly 0" then describes the computed value, not the input. Outside a component-magnitude window of roughly 1e-154 to 1e154, a 0.0 residual or a None ratio is not evidence of exactness. (ii) K0 underflow (adversarial N2): if k² or k²/(4πG_EH) underflows to 0.0 (for example k ≲ 1.6e-162, or k ≲ 5.6e-12 at G_EH = 1e300), the off-diagonals of K0 become exactly 0. The result is the k = 0 operator, which the scope excludes, and it is still packaged with `ok` True. numpy's default `under='ignore'` means no warning is issued, even when warnings are escalated. (iii) Prefactor overflow when warnings are not escalated (adversarial N1): if G_EH > ~1.43e307, then 4πG_EH overflows to inf, the prefactor becomes 0, and K0 is exactly zero. Under root's fatal-warning policy the numpy overflow warning ends the call. Without escalation, the zero K0 is packaged with `ok` True. Together these paths qualify the contract's "no value is replaced by zero" (V002 L60) and its ratio semantics (L37) at extreme magnitudes. They do not reopen any closed 0398 item. According to 0399, root's reviewed controls use ordinary magnitudes and fatal warnings, and both checkers find that these paths require extreme magnitudes. V002 therefore stays qualified to its tested scope. This addendum makes no all-scale, physical or prediction claim, and it makes no code change or new numerical case.
