**Computation (verbatim):**
- M = VᴴWV
- b = VRᴴW(ρξ_R) − i·mode·VᴴW(ρξ_φ/R) + VZᴴW(ρξ_z)

- per-block singular-value ranges and condition numbers, as diagnostics only
- the residual Ma − b
- dot(W, δρ) as a complex value, not forced to zero

**Failure behaviour:** a singular or non-finite block fails closed, keeping the completed-block count and the coefficients already solved. There is no acceptance flag.
