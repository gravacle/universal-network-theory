## Answers to the three questions (with the 0367 qualification)

1. **Populated e = 1.0 is not necessarily a limit marker.** The CDS writes an ordinary printed ±1.0 as a populated value with e = 1.0, the same literal Note (3) calls "upper limit".
   - The only electronic limit form observed is blank + 1.0, in e_FUV+24, a column the ReadMe does not attach Note (3) to.
   - NGC 628 prints no HI or stellar limit, so there is no example of a limit in those columns. Blank + 1.0 cannot occur for stars, because Sigma* carries no "?".
   - **NGC 3198 stars row 2 stays UNRESOLVED.** Its form matches an ordinary ±1.0, but under its e_Sigma* Note (3) it can also be read as a marker. Neither reading is established, and the electronic limitation is preserved.
2. **Printed 0.0 and absent H2.**
   - Printed ±0.0 is transcribed as e = 0.0. It is a published σ printed as 0.0, not a known zero, and its rounding rule is unstated. This covers NGC 3198 rows 16, 18, 19 and 20.
   - Printed H2 "≤" is blank/blank in all 18 of 18 cases, with no value and no marker. An electronic blank is therefore indistinguishable from no-data, and NGC 3198 H2 rows 10–22 cannot be classified as limits. The pixel rule on p.43, "where there are no data, we take ΣHI = 0 and ΣH2 = 0", applies to ring averaging, not to blank cells. Blank is not zero.
3. **Reduction assumptions that stay attached** (named only; none adopted):
   - ring averaging: mean for HI, H2 and SFR, median for stars, 10″ rings, within 60° of the major axis, Walter et al. Table 4 geometry (p.43);
   - zero substitution for no-signal pixels (p.43);
   - σ defined as the RMS scatter within the ring, excluding systematics (pp.43–44);
   - HI helium factor 1.36 (Eq. A1, p.34);
   - H2 constant X_CO and the CO(2→1)/(1→0) line ratio, with X_CO unknown at low metallicity (Eqs. A2–A3, pp.34–35);
   - stars: 3.6 μm→K scaling, fixed K-band M/L, Kroupa IMF; "the major uncertainty … is the mass-to-light ratio" (Eq. C1, p.37);
   - the inclination correction throughout.
