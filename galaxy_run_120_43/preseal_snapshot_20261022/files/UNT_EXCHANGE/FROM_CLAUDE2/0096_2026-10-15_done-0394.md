### 2. Inclination and distance

**Inclination.**
- SPARC Inc is the mean outer-HI inclination "(in some cases fixed to the optical value)" (C2). Per CDS Note (3), that mean summarizes a warped tilted-ring fit.
- CB89 used a radially varying i(R).
- The inclination used to deproject each row is UNRES.

**Distance.**
- f_D is "Magnitude of the Tip of the Red Giant Branch".
- FRC: "Product D is coordinate reference only".
- Radius rescaling by shared/product distance is a documented program rule, VEC /selection/physical_radii. It is DOC, not INF (C4).
- The original distance convention is UNRES.
- The historical D/i/PA convention is at ADM and INV l.40.
