## Answers

### What was fitted (CB89, ApJ pp.764–765, §IV(b) "H i Kinematics")
- **Velocity field:** from a moment analysis; a Gaussian-profile field gave similar results.
- **Fit:** the 45″ data were fitted with a least-squares solution for five parameters (centre, Vsys, i, PA) in 30″ annuli in the plane of the galaxy.
- **Sides:** the approaching and receding sides were also solved separately. The global curve represents both sides.
- **Reading, INFERRED:** this is a global, deprojected tilted-ring rotation velocity. It is not a line-of-sight speed.

### Inclination and position angle (CB89 p.765)
- A two-step procedure:
  1. the inner, unwarped data, with i and PA fixed at their optical values, gave the centre and Vsys;
  2. then "we let i and [PA] vary at all radii".
- Inside the warp, i and PA stay nearly constant near the last optical isophote. Beyond it the disk becomes more face-on.
- The northeast side has too little HI at the last point.
- **NOT FOUND:** any bound or prior on the radial variation, and any uncertainty on i or PA.
- **Be91** (Table 1) gives a single inclination for DDO154 and requires 50°–80° for its sample. Whether it used CB89's i(R) is NOT FOUND.

### Beam and annulus support (CB89 §II(b) p.761; p.764)
- VLA D-configuration, 33.5″×35.3″ synthesized beam (uniform weighting), 10″ pixels.
- Restored to 45″, and a 60″ cube was also made. The curve uses the 45″ data on 30″ annuli.
- **INFERRED:** because the annuli are narrower than the beam, adjacent points are not independent. No per-point kernel is stated.
- **Be91:** only generic statements (Westerbork or VLA; the two sides agree within 10%; gas-extent ratio < 1.2; full 2-D field). Nothing DDO154-specific.

### Pressure and asymmetric drift
- **CB89 p.765**, the last sentence of §IV(b), verbatim: "Corrections for the asymmetric drift have been calculated and found negligible at all radii."
  - "Asymmetric" occurs nowhere else. "Dispersion" appears only in the §V halo fits, which are excluded.
  - No method, dispersion, magnitude, bound or uncertainty is given.
  - **"Negligible" is qualitative and gives no usable numerical bound.**
- **Be91** selection criterion (vii): gas pressure gradients are negligible compared with the centrifugal term. This is an assumption with no DDO154 computation.
  - "Drift" occurs 0 times in Be91, and "pressure" occurs once, in (vii).
  - **INFERRED:** Be91 applies no correction.

### Distance
- **CB89 §III(c):** "we favor a distance of 4 Mpc over 10 Mpc", and "even the small distance is probably an upper limit". The basis is the brightest blue stars and CVn I.
- **Be91** distance code 4 is the "distance to CVn I group". It is not a Hubble-flow distance, and it differs from SPARC's TRGB method. The value is not transcribed.

### What Be91 changed, and the SPARC mapping
- **Be91 adopts the CB89 curve.** Table 1 reference code 4 = Carignan & Beaulieu 1989, for both photometry and rotation curve, and DDO154 is among the four galaxies "added from other sources".
- Its ring re-derivation is described in the paragraph about Begeman's six galaxies.
- **NOT FOUND:** any rescaling, resampling, rebinning, interpolation, beam or smearing treatment for DDO154. The searches for "resampl", "rebin", "interpol", "beam", "smear" and "SPARC" each return 0 hits.
- **SPARC mapping: NOT DOCUMENTED by either primary source.** Neither paper documents how SPARC's 12 rows derive from it: sampling, rescaling to TRGB distance, inclination rescaling or error transfer.
- **INFERRED:** the reference pair "Be91, CB89" fits Be91 being an intermediate compilation of the CB89 curve. Whether SPARC took its rows from Be91's tabulation or directly from CB89 is undocumented.
- **CB89 per-point errors:** not described in the prose. They sit in the p.766 table, which was not opened.

### Raw measurement versus later fits
- **CB89 raw and reduction items:**
  - continuum from line-free end channels;
  - one lower-quality run discarded;
  - VLA flux below single-dish values, which affects the HI mass and not the kinematics directly;
  - side-difference and Gaussian-versus-moment statistics exist on p.765, not transcribed.
- **Excluded:** CB89 §V mass distribution, and Be91 §3 dark haloes and MOND, including Be91's fit setup (constant M/L, helium factor).

## Checker corrections, recorded here

- **Be91 C1 (evidence basis).** "Code 4 for both photometry and rotation curve" is correct. However, the OCR layer contains only the photometry-code column. The checker verified the rotation-curve code from a single 300-dpi crop of that column alone, which contains no data. It reads 1,1,1,1,1,1,2,3,4,6, so DDO154 = 4. The Be91 note says no page was rendered, so it does not state how this was obtained.
- **Be91 C2.** The phrase "excluded Results section" is imprecise, because the paper has no section by that name. The text in question is in §3 "DARK HALOES AND MOND", which starts on p.4.
- **Be91 C3.** Be91 attributes the VLA field to Carignan & Freeman 1988. CB89, however, describes its own new D-configuration observations and only compares its curve with CF88's. Read Be91's statement as Be91's attribution, not as a statement about where CB89's data came from.
- **CB89 CB1.** §IV(c) "Rotation Curve Analysis" begins on PDF p.9 (ApJ 765), not p.10.
