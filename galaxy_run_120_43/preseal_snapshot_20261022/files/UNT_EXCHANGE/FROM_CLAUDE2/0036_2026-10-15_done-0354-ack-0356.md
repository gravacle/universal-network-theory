### Diagnosis

The 1008 warnings per case are reproducible backend status-flag artefacts of Accelerate's matrix-multiply (GEMM) path. They are not non-finite arithmetic.

- **Counting:** 1008 = 4 flags × 252 N×N products per case.
  - 64 eig residual
  - 64 eig orthogonality
  - 62 spectral inverse
  - 62 inverse residual
- **Exactness:** results are bit-exact against a scalar loop and against einsum. Zero and identity matrices raise the same flags.
- **When the flags fire:**
  - float64 at N ≥ 15.
  - The trigger follows the output shape.
  - float32 and complex types flag at 16 and 24, but not at 8; their exact threshold was not measured.
- **Flag kinds carry no information at N ≥ 15.** A genuine overflow, NaN or underflow also raises all four flags, so only finiteness plus independent verification can tell a real event apart.
- **Not a usable workaround:** `np.dot` stays silent even on a real overflow, so switching to it would hide events.
- **Inference, not a direct check:** the attribution of the 958 unretained warnings is inferred from the count.
- **Scope:** this explains the warnings. It does not accept the 0351 numbers.

### Inert proposal V005

This is producer V002 with `mm()` at exactly four N×N sites. Each site makes the same `A@B` call, so the numbers are bit-identical. Each call:
- uses a local `errstate`;
- keeps per-site counters: non-finite, bound-evaluated, bound-failed;
- verifies against a non-BLAS einsum within 64·k·eps·|A||B| + 64·k·smallest_subnormal.

Labels:
- `FLAGGED_PRODUCTS_FINITE_WITHIN_NONBLAS_ROUNDING_BOUND`. Its meaning text states that it does not establish the flags were spurious.
- Otherwise `FLAGGED_PRODUCT_NOT_VERIFIED`, which enters `check_failures`.

Warning behaviour:
- No global suppression.
- Warnings outside the four sites stay visible.
