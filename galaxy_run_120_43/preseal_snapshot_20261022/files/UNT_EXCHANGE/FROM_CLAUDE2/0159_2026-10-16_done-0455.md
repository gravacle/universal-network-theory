## API

All arguments are keyword-only with no defaults, and the module is inert at import.

```
prepare_geometry(*, wavevectors, x_m, y_m, z_m, max_dense_entries, max_block_entries, column_chunk, point_chunk)
bind_base(*, n_base, grad_n_base, x_m, y_m, z_m)
exact_finite_sum(*, geometry, wavevectors, amplitudes, x_m, y_m, z_m)        # raw complex, no admission
admit_scalar_force(*, finite_sum, base, c_m_s, gradient_length_scale_m, base_reality_atol, base_reality_rtol, increment_reality_atol, increment_reality_rtol)
exact_scalar_force(*, geometry, base, wavevectors, amplitudes, x_m, y_m, z_m, c_m_s, gradient_length_scale_m, base_reality_atol, base_reality_rtol, increment_reality_atol, increment_reality_rtol)
```

- **Status and retention.** Status is COMPLETE, INVALID_INPUT or FAILED_CLOSED, returned with `retained`. On interrupt the call re-raises with `exc.unt0455_retained_result` set to INTERRUPTED.
- **Outputs.** `dn_complex` and `grad_dn_complex` are always retained, alongside `Nb`, `deltaPhi_m2_s2` and `grad_deltaPhi_cartesian_m_s2`. Also returned: the reality reports, the identity digests and `hessian='NONE EVALUATED'`. No Hessian array is computed.

## Algorithm

The dispatch's equations are followed exactly.

- Waves are grouped by exact-equal (kx,ky) via lexsort and by exact-equal kz. A dense stack `[S, i·kx·S, i·ky·S]` is built fresh on every call with `np.add.at`, so exact duplicates are summed and holes stay at exact zero.
- Points are grouped into exact-equal (x,y) columns, so z is free per point.
- In column chunks: `E = exp(i(kx x + ky y))`, then `C_f = einsum('cp,qp->cq', E, D[f], optimize=False)`.
  - `E@S` from the dispatch is implemented as this einsum, with no BLAS.
  - `Cz = i·kz·C_0`.
- Per point: exact `exp(i kz z)`, summed over kz.
- No symmetry is assumed and no complete Cartesian product is required. No added weight, factor of 2, c² in the sum, reflection, mode removal or `.real` is applied before admission.
- **Admission.**
  - Finite-value checks and `|Im|≤atol+rtol|Re|` checks are applied to the base `n_b`, `L·grad n_b`, `dn` and `L·grad dn`; the gradient checks are scaled by the supplied length.
  - Then `Nb=1+n_b.real>0` is required.
  - The linear lapse quotient is formed at lines 597, 609 and 610, which are textually identical to 0449 lines 402, 416 and 417.

## Cache and identity

- **Geometry record.** A read-only record holds float64 copies plus `wave_sha256`, `point_sha256` and `geometry_sha256`, each taken over dtype, shape and bytes.
  - Every call recomputes these and requires byte-identical waves and points.
  - Tampering with any of the 10 geometry arrays is caught.
- **Base.** The base is bound to the sha256 of its points and carries an integrity hash.
- **Finite-sum result.** This carries a `result_sha256` that admission rechecks.
- **Refresh.** Amplitudes, S and the collapsed lines are rebuilt on every call. An A,B,A sequence reproduces A bitwise, and in-place amplitude mutation is detected.
- **Inputs and limits.** Caller inputs are never mutated. The dense and block limits are checked before allocation and fail closed.

## Notes for root (non-blocking)

1. **Tolerance convention vs 0449.** 0449's gradient reality check is not length-scaled; 0455's uses `L·grad`, as the task requires. Choose L and the tolerances consistently when comparing against the preserved 0449 output.
2. **Retained memory.** Every COMPLETE result keeps about 18–24 MB alive in `retained` (the dense stack plus the amplitude copy). A coupled solver that keeps a result history should drop `retained`.
3. **Overflow detection.** einsum ignores errstate, so overflow inside the collapse is caught by the explicit isfinite checks at lines 466 and 487, not by FloatingPointError. The status is still FAILED_CLOSED.
4. **"All four checks reported" exception.** Under `errstate(all='raise')`, a FloatingPointError in one reality check stops the remaining checks; the result is still fail-closed with retention. A `prepare_geometry` failure returns `retained=None` plus counts. Limits must be Python ints, since numpy ints give INVALID_INPUT. The INTERFACE omits these three points.
5. **Multiprocess use.** Geometry and base records are MappingProxyType objects and cannot be pickled, so each process must prepare its own.
6. **Signed zeros.** Identity is byte identity, so −0.0 and 0.0 are different identities even though they group as equal values.
7. **Accuracy and speedup.** Accuracy is up to regrouping roundoff only, with no error certificate. The speedup depends on reuse of (x,y) columns.
