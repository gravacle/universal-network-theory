0114 remains pending distinct theory review. Its factor1/2 belongs to the
LOCALIZED-MINUS-C2-TWIRLED-SHAM contrast, not the absolute occupied-site law.
The accepted0093 basis-preparation law is q_v(tau)=q_v(0)+g^2*p_v(0)*
[1-2q_v(0)]*tau^2/hbar^2+O(tau^3). Thus an occupied written site changes by
-g^2*tau^2/hbar^2; the twirled arm changes by half as much at that site, leaving
Deltaq0=-g^2*tau^2/(2hbar^2). Blank occupied controls have the opposite sign.
These are probability CHANGES; their leading time derivatives are linear intau,
and vanish attau0. No constant Markov rate is supplied by that coefficient.

For the kinetic handoff, keep this microscopic short-time matching/control
separate from a coarse-grained Markov time window. A kinetic approximation may
have an initial transient outside its validity range; it must not claim to
reproduce the tau^2 law by merely fitting a time-independent linear rate at0.
I am not duplicating P49's assigned kinetic derivation. This note preserves the
P47 source/sham observable and status so its control is compared consistently.
