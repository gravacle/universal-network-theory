## 1. Selection and bounded roster

Exclude NGC3198 categorically. Before admitting either name, root must compare aliases against the actual reserved validation registry; this note does not certify that comparison. Preserve other galaxy velocities, lensing and cosmological observables in their prospectively assigned validation roles. Selection below uses source coverage and geometry limitations, not UNT/MOND fit success, outer-curve flatness or conventional halo mass. No numerical values below are adopted as inputs.

**DDO154 — proposed first source-contract candidate.** The [official NRAO metadata page](https://science.nrao.edu/science/surveys/littlethings/data/d154.html) identifies THINGS HI observations and optical, H-alpha, UV and infrared products. Links were inspected as metadata, not downloaded. [Watts et al. 2018, introduction, Table1 and section2](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/477/4/5554/4983129) documents an extended gas-rich disk, resolved HST imaging and THINGS usage. Its distance is attributed to Jacobs et al.; inclination and position angle have separate THINGS/kinematic references. Thus substantial source coverage exists, but those geometry estimates are not automatically independent of the intended velocity read. [Jacobs et al. 2009](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3675) describes HST CMD/TRGB distance measurement. This supplies a distance route independent of galaxy force fitting; the exact DDO154 distance posterior, photometric calibration and covariance still require source-level custody. Table1 alone is not that likelihood.

The ordinary-gas dominance minimizes, without eliminating, stellar conversion sensitivity. One finite radial source profile can use the already qualified axisymmetric leading-static infrastructure, conditional on controlled warp, thickness, pressure, state and exterior remainders. No claim that the real galaxy is exactly stationary, isolated or axisymmetric follows from this choice. Favor DDO154 because source and distance metadata are concrete, not because a published dark-matter interpretation or gravity fit succeeded.

**NGC3741 — reserve only, not a second required campaign.** [Begum et al. 2005 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0502307) reports GMRT HI imaging, gas-dominated ordinary mass and a very extended gas disk relative to optical size. It offers a different source morphology and potential spatial-scale lever. However [Banerjee et al. 2013, geometry/bar methods](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/434/2/1257/1066698) explicitly treats radially changing inclination/position angle and a gas bar. Its adopted distance is cited rather than independently measured there. The original distance uncertainty and usable source-only radial products have not been audited here. Do not promote a cited distance or an assumed scaleheight to an independent geometric measurement.

NGC3741 is retained to test whether an additional, differently shaped source could separate spatial filtering from amplitude response, only after source-only scale and geometry admission. Its distinct physical length/acceleration leverage is a hypothesis to forecast from ordinary sources, not established by optical-size ratios or target speeds. If admitting it needs a new barred-gas dynamical/cube pipeline, defer it; that cost is not justified before the first galaxy pilot. No substitute candidate is silently added based on fit outcomes.

## 2. What can be identified

Reuse the reviewed route screen's current-action result, not a published MOND relation. At positive spectral cutoff sigma, the local relaxed small-source expansion is

    g-p(g) = C3 g^3 + O(g^5),  C3=2/gs^2+1/(gc^2 sigma^2).

This requires its asymptotic domain and is not a disk field equation. Reciprocal spatial filtering also contributes the zero-field operator I-S^2. A qualified resolved forward map could constrain C3 together with ell relative to independently inferred source lengths. DDO154 alone may leave those directions correlated with distance, geometry and projected state/exterior terms. NGC3741 could improve shape information only if its independent source profile produces a different sensitivity after those nuisance directions are retained. It does not ensure rank.

Neither low-field source guarantees separation of gc, gs and sigma: the displayed cubic coefficient is one combination of three. Higher-order resolved response may help only if forecast above total read/model uncertainty. Static relaxed data leave tau absent exactly. Do not fit tau from a rotation snapshot or assume all five parameters become identifiable. A joint region in the relevant combinations can suffice if it yields a finite useful NGC3198 prediction envelope. Do not use V^4=GMa0, halo decompositions or published a0 as this model's observation equation.

The ordinary source data do not directly measure z_x, xi or preferred-clock alignment. State admission may use a physically constrained common family, independently justified projected bounds, or joint calibration inference with declared identifiable nuisance directions. It must retain admitted alternatives, including the local geodesic null branch where compatible; never silently equate zero mean, old age or conservative evolution with relaxation. No requirement to reconstruct every mode or total universe energy is imposed.

## 3. Actual input and read schema for the first candidate

Each record below needs a source locator/version, units, uncertainty representation and shared-dependency identifier. Missing information stays missing rather than becoming zero or an independent Gaussian error.

| Record | Required contents and use |
|---|---|
| role_manifest | canonical name/aliases; proposed calibration-only role; exclusion-registry comparison; exposure ledger; finite source/read aperture fixed before examining response outcomes |
| distance_geometry | independent CMD/TRGB distance likelihood and calibration systematics; centre, inclination/PA versus angular radius, warp and their joint covariance/dependence on kinematic reduction |
| ordinary_source | angular HI column/flux profile and beam/mask; conversion and opacity assumptions; helium exactly once; stellar band profile/extinction/population mass conversion; molecular/diffuse-gas limits with censoring; radial coverage and tail uncertainty |
| volume_exterior | permitted thickness/source deprojection family, ordinary external field/source assumptions; boundary lift or exterior family and its observation-level error; no zero-wall physical assertion |
| state_contract | common physical parameters and admitted per-system state/projection family; predicted-read bound for unresolved polarization/stress and finite-window remainder; origin of constraints, including any jointly inferred calibration nuisance |
| read_definition | later allocated LOS velocity observable and spatial/beam kernel, or published circular-speed estimator with complete inclination, pressure/noncircular corrections and covariance; system velocity and geometry dependencies; no unowned conversion from centroid to circular speed |
| forward_output | finite-bin ordinary-motion read from the actual current-action solution and observer/material map; parameter/nuisance label, numerical residual and approximation error on that read; retained full coupled-validity conditions |
| likelihood_or_bounds | joint read/source covariance or deterministic uncertainty set; correlations across bins and systems; selection model, nuisance profiling and validation exclusions |

First read only the source/geometry records. Later response ingestion, if root admits the contract, should use DDO154 calibration values with the declared kernels and role. The pipeline is source conversion -> finite source/state/exterior problem -> actual primitive observable -> same reduction/kernel as measured -> joint residual. It is not a pointwise constitutive substitution into a published Newtonian acceleration array. A circular-speed table may avoid a full new cube pipeline only when its estimator and pressure/warp covariance are adequate for the retained bins. Otherwise report that precise deficiency; do not quietly ignore it or expand into a survey reduction.
