## Separation of programs

The record program and macro-response program are distinct.

### Record accumulation gates

| Gate | Objective | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **R-A: finite owner ledger** | Form and retain finite records with every actual source, transfer, and read owned once. | Exact cellwise/global ledger; complete read; hostile audit. |
| **R-B: multi-cell accumulation** | Increase the number of retained record histories on a larger finite member. | Positive retained total, explicit lineage count, nonzero owned currents where transport is active, exact telescoping, and common normalization across sizes. |
| **R-C: interaction/retention stress** | Turn on bounded interactions among accumulated records and test survival, mixing, or loss. | Full outcome census and quantitative retained-lineage change; either positive or negative results are admissible. |
| **R-D: repeated-scale record law** | Compare record observables over further finite sizes or depths. | Reproducible scaling of record quantities with declared preparation density and error/custody bounds. No continuum form is presumed. |

R-B does **not** require `p->2`, `xi/L`, a massless mode, a Ward remainder,
smoothness, locality beyond the declared supports, or any gravity-shaped law.
A flat, irregular, parity-dependent, saturating, or rapidly growing result is
a result about the records, not a gate failure unless it violates the declared
record ledger.

The microscopic balance quantity is called a **record-ledger residual**, not
a defect. A future nonzero residual may identify a newly active necessary
owner or collective term as accumulation becomes critical. It is classified
before it is judged; only an unowned term after the complete selected-parent
census is an accounting failure.

### Separate macro-response gates

The former V001 Gate-A physical Hessian/quotient completion and Gates B--E are
retained as the optional **M-series** investigation:

- **M-A:** common physical parent, global response owners, and lawful quotient;
- **M-B:** finite-size collective-response diagnostics;
- **M-C/D:** threshold and continuum tests, only if evidence motivates them;
- **M-E:** stress/exchange comparison.

No M-series behavior is inferred from passing an R-series gate. In particular,
record accumulation does not imply continuum behavior, a Ward identity,
Einstein/Fierz--Pauli form, gravity, `C_R`, or `G`.
