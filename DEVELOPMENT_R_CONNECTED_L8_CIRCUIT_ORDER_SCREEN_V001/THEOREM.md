# Connected L8 owner-once circuit order screen — numerical result

## Scope

The 16-record native-support parent, reversed stagger preparation, transfer
angle `pi/8`, depth three, complete failure-inclusive terminal product read,
and inherited adopted `alpha=r0` are held fixed. Only the order of the same 24
unique native transfer gates is varied:

- forward: first cycle, second cycle, then inter-cycle supports;
- reverse: the exact reverse sequence; and
- interleaved: corresponding first-cycle, second-cycle, and inter-cycle gates
  are alternated.

Each schedule owns all 24 supports exactly once per depth. Schedule is part of
the selected finite circuit parent; none is asserted to approximate a
simultaneous Hamiltonian or continuum time ordering. Inherited tuple labels
remain record enumeration, not a physical grid.

## Raw order dependence

All three schedules keep all 24 cumulative supports active, including all
eight inter-cycle supports, and preserve expected L8 retained total `128`
within `2.14e-13`. Their raw observables differ materially:

| schedule | event traffic | net edge throughput | net inter-cycle throughput | max abs edge correlation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| forward | 2.9296146640 | 2.6742223590 | 0.9477610040 | 0.0659258367 |
| reverse | 2.4885208071 | 1.8080344264 | 1.3785667455 | 0.0480763749 |
| interleaved | 2.8180758755 | 2.4370624130 | 0.7769580616 | 0.0663517709 |

For the complete terminal blank/content distributions, pairwise total
variation ranges from `0.1070161536` to `0.3665342011`. Pairwise state
fidelity ranges from `0.5157506266` to `0.9487856578`, and the largest local
occupation L1 distance is `0.4943831749`. Thus owner-once support census alone
does not determine the accumulated record distribution; the finite event
schedule is necessary parent data for this noncommuting circuit.

The raw record-ledger residual envelope is `2.30e-14` in L1 and `3.78e-15`
in Linf per cluster. Norm and record-number-law controls remain below
`1.12e-15` and `2.50e-16`.

## Classification

This is **controlled numerical evidence** of schedule dependence, not an
order-selection law. It prevents the forward schedule from being treated as
generic and makes any future schedule averaging or selection an explicit
theory choice rather than an implicit assumption.

Raw residuals remain unassigned until owner classification; they are not
physical defects or exact zeros. The screen establishes no preferred order,
generic retention, criticality, continuum behavior, Ward structure, mature
macroscopic dynamics, or gravity.
