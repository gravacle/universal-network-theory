## Fixed scheduling rules

1. Stages are dependency ordered.  A later stage cannot use a pending or
   failed predecessor.
2. Negative results are results.  A preregistered rejection halts its
   dependent branch without being converted into a candidate.
3. Target and hostile work may run in parallel only after their respective
   authorization gates pass.
4. L12 permits exactly two heavy one-thread workers plus lightweight
   monitoring.  The certified aggregate bounds are 34,865,626,528 bytes RAM
   and 19,201,886,636 bytes scratch; resources are resampled immediately
   before launch.
5. The live schedule is updated on every stage transition, blocking audit
   result, physical-run start/stop, or forecast shift of at least one hour or
   25 percent.
6. Baseline estimates are planning ranges, not evidence or guarantees.
