# Connected L8 onsite-preparation pattern screen — numerical result

## Fixed parent data and varied label

The inherited 16-record native support, forward owner-once schedule, transfer
angle `pi/8`, depth three, adopted `alpha=r0` attachment, and complete
failure-inclusive terminal read are fixed. The only varied record label is
the onsite pattern:

- `same`: equal stagger pattern on both cycles;
- `reversed`: opposite stagger patterns;
- `first_only`: stagger only the first cycle; and
- `second_only`: stagger only the second cycle.

Every onsite term is allowed by the previously audited finite carrier parent
and commutes with total occupation. No pattern is selected as preferred and
no distribution over patterns is introduced. Tuple labels enumerate the
inherited finite record family; they are not a physical grid.

## Raw pattern dependence

All four histories retain expected total `128` across L8 within `2.28e-13`.
The equal-pattern control has internal cycle traffic but zero cumulative
inter-cycle throughput within `7.22e-16`; exchange symmetry leaves its eight
inter-cycle supports inactive. The reversed pattern activates all eight with
net inter-cycle throughput `0.9477610039941`. Each one-cycle-only pattern also
activates all eight, with throughput `0.4179092011731`.

The full terminal record distributions depend strongly on preparation.
Pairwise total variation reaches `0.6574390434811216`, minimum state fidelity
is `0.0937061883806159`, and maximum local-occupation L1 distance is
`2.959881706601889`. The apparent labeled difference between `first_only`
and `second_only` is a ring-label exchange: after swapping the two eight-bit
cycle labels, their terminal-distribution total variation is
`3.01e-16`.

The raw per-cluster record-ledger residual envelope is `2.64e-14` in L1 and
`3.61e-15` in Linf. Norm and record-number-law controls remain below
`2.11e-15` and `2.78e-16`.

## Classification

This is **controlled numerical evidence** that preparation asymmetry is
necessary for active inter-cycle transport in this fixed symmetric circuit.
It does not derive a generic necessity theorem for other supports, schedules,
or histories. Preparation pattern is explicit selected-parent record data.

The screen does not choose a preferred preparation, measure, or averaging
rule. Raw residuals remain unassigned pending owner classification and are
not physical defects or exact zeros. No generic retention law, criticality,
continuum behavior, Ward structure, mature macroscopic dynamics, or gravity
is claimed.
