What the colours mean

  • Placed: Follows from UNT's rules plus settings the universe landed on (like α); stable, predicted first, checked by Codex2
  • Placed, if a rule change is adopted: Works only with a proposed change to UNT's rules that you have not adopted yet; once adopted it is simply placed
  • Placed, awaiting independent check: Meets every test except a second model family's check
  • Path exists: UNT has room for it; at least one step is still missing
  • Against: A checked result says today's rules contradict it
  • Open: No path yet

A piece counts as placed only when all six marks are ticked: derived, not put in by hand, stable, predicted first, checked by Codex2, scoped.

Settings the universe landed on, like α, are features of the universe, not assumptions. They are allowed and counted (fewer is better), but a setting cannot be the very fact being explained.
