## 1. Objective

The program's governing rule is that no physical conclusion is established by
declaring its required bridge to be true.  A definition fixes meaning.  An
adopted postulate supplies the antecedent of a conditional theorem.  Neither is
evidence that nature satisfies the antecedent.

The existing working-theory closure proves an implication of the form

```text
RGRL + WTC-H1--H5 + qualified record inputs
    -> leading Einstein--Hilbert response.
```

This program targets the missing implication:

```text
authenticated same-parent microscopic dynamics and retained lineage
    -> the physical content presently supplied by RGRL and WTC-H1--H5.
```

Every obligation below must end in one of three states: **proved at a stated
ceiling**, **falsified/bounded**, or **open**.  “Adopted” is never a passing
scientific result.

## 2. Evidence classes

Every recovered and future claim must carry exactly one primary class:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DEFINITION` | Fixes vocabulary or a mathematical object; has no truth claim about nature. |
| `ADOPTED` | Postulate or working law; may support a conditional theorem but is not itself proved. |
| `EXACT_DERIVED` | Analytic consequence of fully listed premises. |
| `COMPUTED` | Numerical result with frozen method, tolerances, custody, and reproducibility. |
| `EMPIRICAL` | Observation or experiment with a declared measurement and uncertainty model. |
| `NEGATIVE` | Controlled obstruction, null, counterexample, or failed gate. |
| `SUPERSEDED` | Historical result whose interpretation or logical role was withdrawn or replaced. |
| `OPEN` | Required step not yet discharged. |

An `EXACT_DERIVED` or `COMPUTED` result is also labeled **conditional** whenever
any load-bearing antecedent is `ADOPTED` or `OPEN`.

## 3. Non-negotiable proof rules

1. A theorem may prove an implication; it may not silently prove its
   antecedent.
2. A named physical identification must be derived from the parent or tested
   independently.  Renaming a mathematical variable as metric, curvature,
   stress, or gravity is not a proof.
3. All load-bearing objects must be fixed before opening scored results.  This
   includes graphs, metrics, kernels, nulls, scales, observables, signs, and
   decision thresholds.
4. Same labels do not establish same-parent identity.  Every composition must
   prove provenance, type compatibility, and parameter genealogy.
5. A finite result is not a continuum result without a controlled inter-size or
   refinement theorem.
6. A correlation is not a response law.  A response law is not universal
   gravity without common-metric, complete-stress, Ward/constraint, and
   back-reaction custody.
7. Negative and failed results remain in the main proof ledger.  They may close
   routes and cannot be hidden in development history.
8. Historical work is recovered by its current valid ceiling.  Superseded
   interpretations are not revived merely because their formulas are exact.
9. Independent audit checks logic and custody; it does not transform an adopted
   premise into evidence.
10. No multi-hour computation may start without the checkpoint/resume,
    storage, power, and recovery gate required by the long-run safety protocol.
