## Exact claim boundary

This null rules out only the preregistered claim that record concentration is
positively associated, at L8 and at the frozen effect/resolution threshold,
with curvature of the **formation-order path**.  It does not rule out:

- a different native lineage relation derived prospectively from unchanged
  dynamics;
- a weighted or directed graph justified independently of record density;
- curvature associations at other sizes or under a proved refinement map;
- the autonomous lineage-sensitive continuation experiment; or
- record-conditioned continuum geometry or gravity.

test neither promoted that path to emergent space nor allowed the measured
record concentration to choose its graph.  Richer graph tests must first earn
their vertices, edges, weights, and metric without reusing the response being
