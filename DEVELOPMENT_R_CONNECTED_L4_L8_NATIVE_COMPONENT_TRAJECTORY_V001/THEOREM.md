# Connected native-component L4-to-L8 trajectory — numerical result

## Common labeled recipe

For even `L`, prepared retained heads are labeled `(2r+1,y,z)`. At each odd
first label and even `z`, two native length-`L` generator cycles at `z,z+1`
are joined by their `L` native third-generator supports. The construction
therefore has `L^2/4` components, `2L` records per component, and `3L`
owner-once supports per circuit depth. It partitions all `L^3/2` heads.

At both `L=4` and `L=8`, the same explicit record labels are used: reversed
onsite preparation on the paired cycles, forward support schedule, transfer
angle `pi/8`, and depth three. The inherited adopted `alpha=r0` attachment
and complete failure-inclusive product read remain in force.

Coordinates are finite-family enumeration, not a physical grid or distance.
Schedule and preparation are selected-history labels, not universal laws.

## Classification

This is **controlled numerical evidence** for one explicitly labeled
connected finite-size trajectory. It strengthens the earlier disjoint-block
volume replication by keeping interaction components connected and growing
their record count from eight to sixteen. It does not erase the separately
audited schedule and preparation dependence.

No scaling exponent, generic accumulation law, criticality, continuum
behavior, Ward structure, mature macroscopic dynamics, or gravity is
derived. Raw residuals remain unassigned pending owner classification and
are not physical defects or exact zeros.
