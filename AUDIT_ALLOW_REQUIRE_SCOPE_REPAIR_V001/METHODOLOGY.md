integrated repair text or result was opened.  It does not choose the target's
conclusions.  It specifies the evidence and type checks that any proposed

explicit erratum, but the manifest must identify every modified live file.

## 3. Type-correctness screen

The target must define all of the following as distinct objects:

1. a parent/carrier specification;
2. its state space;
3. an admissibility predicate or admissible subset;
4. constraint generators;
5. characteristic projectors when such projectors are actually constructed;
6. an occupied state;
7. fixed-parent dynamics;
8. a coarse-graining/effective-description map; and
9. an enlarged parent with dynamical carrier labels or variables.

Predicates return truth values.  Sets contain states.  States encode
occupancy.  Constraints and projectors are operators only after their domains
and constructions are given.  Dynamics is a map.  A commutator is permitted
only between operators on a common domain.  No map from the words `allow` or
`require` to a Hilbert-space operator may be presumed.

`Required(P,phi)` must be typed as a quantification over the admissible set,
not as an extra dynamical force.  The existence of a characteristic projector
for a particular finite constraint does not establish a canonical projector
for an arbitrary modal predicate.

## 4. Fixed-parent theorem and enlarged-parent escape

The strongest valid fixed-parent statement is conditional on definitions:

> If the parent, state space, admissible subset, and evolution law are fixed,
> and the evolution maps admissible states to admissible states, then that
> internal evolution changes occupancy within the fixed admissible subset; it
> does not change the definition of that subset.

The audit must reject any target that silently drops these antecedents or
promotes this type theorem to a universal physical law.

The target must separately represent an enlarged parent, for example a
family or direct sum of fibers indexed by a carrier variable.  If that index
is dynamical and inter-fiber transitions are in the enlarged law, effective
admissible sets may change after conditioning or coarse-graining.  This is a
logical reopening only.  The repair does not prove that the repository's
physical parent realizes such transitions or emergent permissions.
