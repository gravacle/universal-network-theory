## Finding

The reviewed documents establish useful pieces, but do **not demonstrate the complete autonomous chain** in which record formation supplies the physical support and boundary, that same support has a nonempty global degree-two ice sector, and native loop dynamics act in that sector.

This is not a no-go against collective formation. The immediate unresolved interface is the physical support and its boundary under one declared parent and preparation. It is also not evidence that no records form: the existing support theorem explicitly supplies a finite programmed formation-and-retention construction.

### A distinction that matters for the next calculation

**Correction to the earlier discussion:** a general statement that retaining record-owned support requires freezing every link flip is too broad and is superseded by the construction-specific distinction below.

Do **not** infer that preserving the `K` support always requires switching off every link fluctuation. FH section 4, **“Exact padding/nonedge quarantine and history-blind hold,”** and theorem `FPSS-3` permit active `K`-gated link dynamics in the general qualified support hold. The projector there fixes the program and `K`, not the active `n` word. Both raw and `K`-gated flips are turned off in FH section 5, **“What the solder closes for the FD lane,”** for the separate **saturated FD carrier comparator**, because that comparator requires the `n` word itself to remain saturated.

Thus there are two different obstacles:

1. The raw finite q4 slab cannot support the requested global degree-two sector because of its boundary.
2. Exact saturated FD hopping cannot simultaneously be identified with degree-two ice on the same `n` field, even after a suitable boundary is supplied.

The second obstacle does not forbid studying coupled `n + psi` dynamics, nor does it require a new kinetic term merely to investigate a different collective effective law. Such a law would have to be calculated rather than assumed.
