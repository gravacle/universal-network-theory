# Research handoff: collective formation before an EM assumption

Date: 2026-10-28

## Objective and decisions to preserve

The research priority is the formation of retained, readable records and the possible emergence of electromagnetism. A downstream conditional gravity result does not by itself supply those upstream constructions.

The working hypothesis is that record formation may be collective from the outset. Do not impose an isolated-first-record demonstration as a prerequisite. Permit the event-dependent distinction to reside in a joint state or correlations, while specifying which resources and operations are assumed. A population of already completed records is a possible initial condition, but cannot simultaneously count as a result demonstrating their formation.

The user asked to explore several possibilities and preserve the results in the UNT GitHub project so later voice-led sessions can resume from the files. The immediate scope is small calculations and precise premise accounting. Physical spin interpretations and cosmological origin claims were explicitly deferred.

There is no established diagnosis that earlier failures were caused by starting with one record. The existing L4 intrinsic-admission protocol already starts with four loaded cells and a blank retained carrier. The relevant distinction may concern interactions and cooperation during formation rather than component count alone.

Interaction improves retention for the frozen example but slows writing relative to the independent trio. The model assumes the coupling, correlated equilibrium preparation, bath, source, and readout. See `cooperative_record_notes.md` and its script/JSON.
3. **Native loop audit:** source-level review of FE/FF/FH and two finite Boolean confirmations. It corrects the overbroad statement that all support retention requires all link flips off. See `native_loop_audit.md` for the controlling distinction and exact sources.

These are illustrative calculations and a bounded audit, not independent external validation of UNT. No new theorem status or physical EM claim is assigned.

### B. Test a native cooperative retention mechanism

Identify an already permitted interaction that could supply a retention barrier or protected correlation. Compare independent and interacting cases using the same component count, record criterion, and observation mission. Account for preparation and coupling resources. The completed stochastic toy begins with an already correlated equilibrium blank; a future claim about formation of those correlations needs its own dynamics and comparison.

Follow the construction-specific constraints in `native_loop_audit.md`; do not generalize the saturated FD comparator's flip-off condition to every FH support hold.
