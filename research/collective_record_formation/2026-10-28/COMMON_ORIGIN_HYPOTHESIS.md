This proposal does not assign a physical length scale, identify a spin, or establish a cosmological beginning. It does not require an isolated completed record to exist before collective formation. It asks whether the same explicitly specified underlying process can produce record formation and two independently justified physical responses.

## What “bend” must become in a calculation

A bend must be represented by a defined change in states, relations, an operator, or a transport rule, with a measurable or invariant consequence. Connectivity, distinguishability, connection curvature, and physical spacetime curvature are different mathematical claims.

In standard field theory, electromagnetic field strength is the curvature of a gauge connection; in general relativity, gravitational curvature belongs to the spacetime connection and metric geometry. Their related mathematical language does not identify them as the same physical field or prove a common microscopic origin.
