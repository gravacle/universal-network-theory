Law as premises, whether the owner-once lineage-writing dynamics predicts any
observable carrier behavior that cannot be reproduced by the same hard-core

## Gated schedule

- **Gate A -- algebra:** Steps 1--2 must establish whether tracing out lineage
  closes the carrier dynamics exactly.
- **Gate B -- implementation:** Steps 3--5 must independently reproduce the
  algebra before any larger calculation is authorized.
- **Gate C -- observable:** Steps 6--7 must identify a nontrivial joint
  record--carrier statistic after conditioning away total charge.
- **Gate D -- prospective test:** Step 8 is authorized only after the statistic
  and its controls are frozen.
- **Gate E -- dynamical mechanism:** Step 9 uses only the unchanged native
  owner-once revisit and carrier transport.  Its L4 pass establishes one
  mechanism, not size persistence; any larger-size continuation is a new
  prospective test.  Record-reading operators proved for other repository
  surfaces are not automatically imported.

## Resource boundary

No L14 computation is part of this plan.  The exact full L14 state would have
`C(42,14) = 52,860,229,080` complex amplitudes, approximately `787.7 GiB` for
one complex128 state.  The carrier-reduction question is algebraic and should
be settled without that expenditure.

## Claim boundary

The carrier-reduction result will not alter the already authenticated finite
record membership, majority mass, or probe-visibility calculations.  It will
determine whether the explicit lineage register has operational consequences
for carrier-only observables under the currently frozen one-pass dynamics.
