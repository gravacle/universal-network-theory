## 1. Selection derivation

The audited F3-MDC mission starts each active pair in
`|x>_writer |B>_tail |B>_head`. With the adopted attachment
`alpha=r0`, the write pulse has `Phi=pi/4` and gives

\[
 |B,B\rangle-i|x,B\rangle \over \sqrt2 .
\]

The subsequent native BS09 transfer has `theta=pi/2` and maps
`|x,B>` to `i|B,x>`. The phases cancel, so the carrier pair becomes

\[
 |B\rangle_{tail}\otimes{|B\rangle+|x\rangle\over\sqrt2}_{head}.
\tag{P01}
\]

This fixes the accumulation input state for every declared source lineage.
It is a **conditional physical-parent derivation** because `alpha=r0` remains
an adopted F3-MDC attachment, not a bare-F3 theorem. The source, writer, and
initial blank states are exact declared mission data.

After preparation, the source and formation ports are off and the fixed
incidence program is held. Literal BS09 then supplies

\[
 H_{car}=\epsilon_\psi Q-t\sum_e n_eT_e,
 \qquad U(\tau)=\exp(-iH_{car}\tau/\hbar).
\tag{P02}
\]

For a fixed physical generator, (P02)—or its chronological exponential when
time-dependent—selects the evolution. A forward, reverse, or interleaved
product of finite transfer gates is a different programmed controller
history unless proved equal to (P02); it is not an independent ambiguity in
the autonomous parent.

The BS09 onsite term is uniform. `Q` commutes with the hopping generator and
with every recorded occupation and current observable. The calculation uses
the zero representative for that uniform term. No node-dependent stagger is
present. The previously audited ordered staggered circuits therefore remain
valid **conditional programmed-control records**, but they are not selected
autonomous BS09 histories and cannot define the physical-parent accumulation
